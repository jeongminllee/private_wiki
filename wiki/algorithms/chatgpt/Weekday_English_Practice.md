# Day 1 — Explaining Your Learning Routine

오늘 목표는 **내가 무엇을 공부하고 있으며, 왜 공부하는지 60–90초 동안 자연스럽게 설명하는 것**입니다.

## 1. 워밍업 — 2분

다음 질문에 영어로 짧게 답해보세요.

> What are you studying these days, and why is it important to you?

먼저 문장을 완벽하게 만들려고 하지 말고, 아래 세 가지 정보만 말하세요.

- 요즘 공부하는 것
- 공부하는 이유
- 앞으로 이루고 싶은 목표

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 현재 학습 내용

> These days, I’m focusing on **[topic]** because I want to **[goal]**.

예:

> These days, I’m focusing on machine learning infrastructure because I want to understand how large models are trained and served.

### Template 2: 어려움과 대응

> One challenge I’ve faced is **[problem]**, so I’ve been trying to **[solution]**.

예:

> One challenge I’ve faced is understanding technical documentation, so I’ve been trying to read it more systematically.

### Template 3: 현재 수준과 장기 목표

> I’m not fully comfortable with **[skill]** yet, but my long-term goal is to **[goal]**.

예:

> I’m not fully comfortable with speaking English yet, but my long-term goal is to communicate confidently in technical discussions.

**말하기 팁:**  
`I want to`만 반복하지 말고 다음 표현도 번갈아 사용하세요.

- My goal is to …
- I’m working toward …
- I hope to …
- I’m aiming to …

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. focus on
**~에 집중하다**

> I’m currently focusing on improving my technical English.

주의:

- `focus in` ❌
- `focus on` ✅

### 2. work toward
**장기적인 목표를 향해 노력하다**

> I’m working toward becoming an ML engineer.

`work toward`는 아직 목표를 달성하지 않았지만 꾸준히 진행 중이라는 의미입니다.

### 3. encounter
**문제나 어려움을 마주치다**

논문과 업무 영어에서 `meet a problem`보다 자연스럽습니다.

> I encountered several issues during model training.

### 4. significant
**중요한, 상당한**

논문에서 매우 자주 등장합니다.

> The new method showed a significant improvement in accuracy.

주의: `significant`는 문맥에 따라 “중요한” 또는 “통계적으로 유의한”이라는 뜻입니다.

### 5. be applicable to
**~에 적용할 수 있다**

> This approach is applicable to real-world problems.

논문 표현:

> The proposed method may be applicable to other domains.

---

## 4. 문장 교정 연습 — 3분

아래 문장을 먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1
> I am studying machine learning since two years.

정답:

> I have been studying machine learning for two years.

설명:

- 현재까지 계속된 행동: `have been studying`
- 기간: `for two years`
- 시작 시점: `since 2024`

### 2
> I want to improve my English speaking skill.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> I want to improve my English speaking skills.

또는:

> I want to improve my spoken English.

`speaking skills`는 보통 복수형으로 사용합니다.

### 3
> I experienced many difficulties to understand papers.

정답:

> I had difficulty understanding academic papers.

또는:

> I encountered many difficulties when reading academic papers.

`difficulty` 뒤에는 일반적으로 동명사 `-ing`가 옵니다.

- difficulty understanding ✅
- difficulty to understand ❌

### 4
> This knowledge can apply to my work.

정답:

> This knowledge can be applied to my work.

설명:

지식이 “적용되는 것”이므로 수동태가 필요합니다.

- I can apply this knowledge.
- This knowledge can be applied.

---

## 5. 60초 말하기 — 2분

다음 구조로 소리 내어 말하세요.

1. These days, I’m focusing on …
2. I’m studying this because …
3. One challenge I’ve faced is …
4. To overcome this, I’ve been …
5. My long-term goal is …

가능하면 녹음한 뒤 다음 항목을 확인하세요.

- 문장마다 `I`로만 시작하지 않았는가
- 과거부터 지금까지 이어지는 행동에 현재완료를 사용했는가
- `because` 뒤에 구체적인 이유를 붙였는가
- 한 문장을 너무 길게 말하지 않았는가

---

> These days, I’m focusing on machine learning, English, and computer science fundamentals. I’m studying these subjects because I want to become a more capable machine learning engineer. One challenge I’ve faced is explaining technical concepts clearly in English. To overcome this, I’ve been practicing short speaking exercises and reading academic papers regularly. I’m not fully comfortable speaking English yet, but I’m working toward participating confidently in technical discussions. My long-term goal is to use English naturally when reading papers, attending interviews, and collaborating with international engineers.

### 한 단계 높은 표현

기본:

> I want to become a better engineer.

발전:

> I’m working toward becoming an engineer who can solve complex problems and communicate technical ideas clearly.

오늘의 핵심 교정은 이것입니다:

> I study English since two years. ❌  
> I have been studying English for two years. ✅

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
# Day 2 — Explaining a Technical Problem Clearly

오늘 목표는 **문제–원인–해결–결과** 구조로 60–90초 동안 설명하는 것입니다. 면접, 회의, 논문 발표에서 그대로 활용할 수 있는 패턴입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 영어로 답해보세요.

> Describe a technical problem you encountered recently. What caused it, and how did you solve it?

아래 네 가지 정보만 순서대로 말하면 됩니다.

1. 어떤 문제가 발생했는가  
2. 원인이 무엇이었는가  
3. 무엇을 시도했는가  
4. 결과적으로 무엇을 배웠는가  

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 문제 설명

> I ran into an issue where **[problem]**.

예:

> I ran into an issue where the model returned an empty response.

`run into an issue`는 `meet a problem`보다 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.

### Template 2: 원인 분석

> After investigating the issue, I found that **[cause]**.

예:

> After investigating the issue, I found that the endpoint configuration was incorrect.

### Template 3: 해결과 교훈

> I resolved the issue by **[solution]**, which taught me that **[lesson]**.

예:

> I resolved the issue by updating the configuration, which taught me that environment settings should always be verified first.

이 구조는 매우 강력합니다.

> problem → investigation → solution → lesson

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. run into an issue
**문제에 부딪히다**

> I ran into an issue while deploying the model.

자연스러운 대체 표현:

> I encountered an issue during deployment.

---

### 2. identify the root cause
**근본 원인을 파악하다**

> It took some time to identify the root cause of the error.

`root cause`는 단순한 증상이 아니라 문제의 근본 원인을 뜻합니다.

---

### 3. reproduce the problem
**문제를 재현하다**

> I reproduced the problem in a separate environment.

디버깅에서는 문제를 먼저 재현할 수 있어야 합니다.

---

### 4. resolve
**문제를 해결하다**

> The issue was resolved after I changed the dependency version.

`solve`도 가능하지만, 기술적 문제에는 `resolve an issue`가 자주 사용됩니다.

---

### 5. robust
**견고한, 쉽게 실패하지 않는**

> We need a more robust error-handling mechanism.

논문 예문:

> The proposed method is robust to noisy input data.

주의:

- `robust for noise`보다는
- `robust to noise`가 자연스럽습니다.

---

## 4. 교정 연습 — 3분

먼저 직접 고친 후 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> An error was happened during training.

정답:

> An error occurred during training.

또는:

> An error happened during training.

설명:

`happen`과 `occur`는 자동사이므로 수동태를 쓰지 않습니다.

- was happened ❌
- happened ✅
- occurred ✅

---

### 2

> I searched the reason of the error.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> I investigated the cause of the error.

또는:

> I tried to identify the cause of the error.

설명:

기술적 원인을 분석할 때 `search the reason`보다 `investigate the cause`가 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 3

> Finally, I could solve the problem.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> Eventually, I was able to solve the problem.

설명:

과거의 특정 상황에서 “해낼 수 있었다”는 의미라면 `was able to`가 더 자연스럽습니다.

- I could understand the explanation.  
  일반적인 능력이나 가능성
- I was able to fix the error.  
  실제로 한 번 성공한 결과

---

### 4

> This problem made me to check the configuration again.

정답:

> This problem made me check the configuration again.

설명:

`make + 사람 + 동사원형` 구조에서는 `to`를 쓰지 않습니다.

- made me to check ❌
- made me check ✅

---

### 5

> The method is robust for unexpected inputs.

정답:

> The method is robust to unexpected inputs.

설명:

`robust`는 일반적으로 `to`와 함께 사용합니다.

---

## 5. 60–90초 말하기 — 2분

아래 구조를 따라 소리 내어 말하세요.

> Recently, I ran into an issue where …  
> At first, I thought …  
> After investigating the issue, I found that …  
> I tried several approaches, including …  
> Eventually, I resolved the issue by …  
> This experience taught me that …

오늘은 `because`만 반복하지 말고 아래 연결어를 사용해보세요.

- At first
- After that
- Eventually
- As a result
- This experience taught me that

---

## Model Answer — 1분

> Recently, I ran into an issue while setting up a machine learning environment. A package listed in the project configuration was not available when I ran the code. At first, I assumed that the installation had failed completely. After investigating the issue, however, I found that I was using a different Python environment from the one managed by the project. I reproduced the problem, checked the interpreter path, and compared the installed packages in both environments. Eventually, I resolved the issue by selecting the correct interpreter and reinstalling the dependencies. This experience taught me that environment consistency is essential for building robust machine learning systems.

## 한 단계 높은 표현

기본:

> I fixed the problem.

발전:

> I identified the root cause and resolved the issue by verifying the environment configuration.

기본:

> I tried many things.

발전:

> I tested several possible solutions and ruled them out one by one.

오늘의 핵심 교정:

> An error was happened. ❌  
> An error occurred. ✅

> It made me to check it again. ❌  
> It made me check it again. ✅

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
# Day 3 — Comparing Two Approaches

오늘 목표는 **두 가지 방법을 비교하고, 하나를 선택한 이유를 60–90초 동안 설명하는 것**입니다. 기술 면접, 회의, 논문 요약에 자주 쓰이는 구조입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 영어로 답해보세요.

> Compare two tools, methods, or learning strategies you have used. Which one do you prefer, and why?

예시 주제:

- PyTorch vs. TensorFlow
- local GPU vs. cloud GPU
- reading papers vs. watching lectures
- studying alone vs. studying in a group

답변에는 다음 내용을 넣으세요.

1. 두 대상의 공통점  
2. 가장 중요한 차이  
3. 각각의 장단점  
4. 내가 선호하는 것과 이유  

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 공통점과 차이점

> Both **[A]** and **[B]** are useful for **[purpose]**, but they differ in terms of **[criterion]**.

예:

> Both local and cloud GPUs are useful for model training, but they differ in terms of cost and flexibility.

### Template 2: 장단점 비교

> While **[A]** is better suited for **[situation]**, **[B]** offers an advantage when **[situation]**.

예:

> While local hardware is better suited for repeated experiments, cloud infrastructure offers an advantage when rapid scaling is required.

### Template 3: 선택과 근거

> Overall, I prefer **[A]** because it allows me to **[benefit]**, even though **[limitation]**.

예:

> Overall, I prefer local hardware because it allows me to control the environment, even though maintenance can be time-consuming.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. in terms of
**~의 측면에서**

> The two models differ significantly in terms of inference speed.

자연스러운 사용:

- in terms of cost
- in terms of accuracy
- in terms of usability

---

### 2. be better suited for
**~에 더 적합하다**

> This method is better suited for small datasets.

주의:

- suited to도 가능
- suited for는 용도나 상황을 말할 때 자주 사용

---

### 3. trade-off
**상충 관계, 한쪽을 얻으면 다른 쪽을 일부 포기해야 하는 관계**

> There is a trade-off between accuracy and computational cost.

논문에서 매우 자주 등장하는 표현입니다.

---

### 4. outperform
**성능이 더 우수하다**

> The proposed model outperformed the baseline on three benchmark datasets.

주의:

> The model was more outperforming. ❌  
> The model outperformed the baseline. ✅

---

### 5. comparable
**비슷한 수준의, 비교할 만한**

> The smaller model achieved comparable performance with fewer parameters.

논문식 표현:

> The results were comparable to those reported in previous studies.

`comparable`은 일반적으로 `to`와 함께 사용합니다.

---

## 4. 교정 연습 — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1

> A is more faster than B.

정답:

> A is faster than B.

설명:

`faster` 자체가 비교급이므로 `more`를 함께 쓰지 않습니다.

- more faster ❌
- faster ✅

---

### 2

> Both methods have each advantage.

자연스러운 표현:

> Both methods have their own advantages.

또는:

> Each method has its own advantages.

설명:

- `both`와 함께 쓰면 `their`
- `each`와 함께 쓰면 단수 동사 `has`

---

### 3

> This model has a better performance.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> This model performs better.

또는:

> This model achieves better performance.

설명:

영어에서는 `have a better performance`보다 `perform better` 또는 `achieve better performance`가 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 4

> The accuracy of A is similar with B.

정답:

> The accuracy of A is similar to that of B.

설명:

- similar with ❌
- similar to ✅

또한 정확도를 비교하는 것이므로 `B` 자체가 아니라 `B의 정확도`와 비교해야 합니다.

---

### 5

> I prefer A than B.

정답:

> I prefer A to B.

또는:

> I prefer A over B.

설명:

`prefer`는 보통 다음 구조를 사용합니다.

- prefer A to B
- prefer A over B
- prefer doing A to doing B

---

## 5. 60–90초 말하기 — 2분

다음 순서로 소리 내어 답하세요.

> Both A and B are useful for …  
> However, they differ in terms of …  
> A is better suited for …  
> In contrast, B is more effective when …  
> There is a trade-off between … and …  
> Overall, I prefer … because …

이번에는 `good`, `bad`, `better`만 반복하지 말고 다음 표현을 써보세요.

- more efficient
- more flexible
- easier to maintain
- less expensive
- better suited for
- comparable to

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> Both local GPUs and cloud GPUs are useful for machine learning experiments, but they differ in terms of cost, scalability, and control. Local GPUs are better suited for repeated experiments because they provide a stable environment and do not require continuous usage fees. In contrast, cloud GPUs are more flexible when additional computing power is needed immediately. There is a clear trade-off between control and scalability. Cloud systems may offer better scalability, while local systems are often easier to customize and maintain over time. Overall, I prefer local hardware for long-term experimentation because it allows me to manage the environment directly, even though upgrading the hardware can be expensive.

## 오늘의 핵심 교정

> A is more faster than B. ❌  
> A is faster than B. ✅

> I prefer A than B. ❌  
> I prefer A to B. ✅

> A is similar with B. ❌  
> A is similar to B. ✅

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
# Day 4 — Summarizing a Research Finding with Appropriate Caution

오늘 목표는 **연구 결과를 요약하면서 과장하지 않고, 근거와 한계를 함께 설명하는 것**입니다. 논문 발표, 기술 면접, 연구 미팅에서 매우 중요한 능력입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> Describe a research result or technical finding you found interesting. What did the study show, and what limitations should we consider?

아래 구조를 따르세요.

1. 연구 주제  
2. 핵심 결과  
3. 결과가 의미하는 것  
4. 결과를 해석할 때 주의할 점  

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 연구 목적과 결과

> The study examined **[topic]** and found that **[main result]**.

예:

> The study examined the effect of model size on reasoning performance and found that larger models generally performed better.

### Template 2: 해석

> This suggests that **[interpretation]**, although it does not necessarily mean that **[overstated conclusion]**.

예:

> This suggests that scale can improve reasoning ability, although it does not necessarily mean that larger models are always more efficient.

### Template 3: 한계 제시

> One limitation is that **[limitation]**, so the results should be interpreted with caution.

예:

> One limitation is that the experiments were conducted on a limited set of benchmarks, so the results should be interpreted with caution.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. indicate
**~을 나타내다, 시사하다**

> The results indicate that the new method improves accuracy.

`show`보다 논문에서 조금 더 격식 있는 표현입니다.

주의:

> The results indicate us that … ❌  
> The results indicate that … ✅

---

### 2. suggest
**~일 가능성을 시사하다**

> The findings suggest that data quality may be more important than data quantity.

`suggest`는 확정적인 사실보다 **가능성이나 해석**을 말할 때 적합합니다.

---

### 3. be consistent with
**~와 일치하다**

> These findings are consistent with previous studies.

논문에서 기존 연구와 결과를 비교할 때 매우 자주 사용됩니다.

---

### 4. generalize to
**~에 일반화되다**

> It is unclear whether the results generalize to real-world environments.

머신러닝 문맥:

> The model performed well on the test set but did not generalize to unseen data.

---

### 5. interpret with caution
**신중하게 해석하다**

> The results should be interpreted with caution because the sample size was small.

연구 결과를 과장하지 않는 핵심 표현입니다.

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고친 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The result means that this method is always better.

더 적절한 표현:

> The result suggests that this method may be more effective under certain conditions.

설명:

논문 결과 하나만으로 `always better`라고 단정하면 과도한 일반화가 됩니다.

- means that: 강한 단정
- suggests that: 가능성 제시
- may be: 불확실성 표현

---

### 2

> According to the experiment, the model was improved.

정답:

> According to the experiment, the model’s performance improved.

또는:

> The experiment showed that the model performed better.

설명:

`the model was improved`는 누군가 모델 자체를 수정했다는 의미로 들릴 수 있습니다. 성능 향상을 말할 때는 `performance improved`가 정확합니다.

---

### 3

> This result is same with previous research.

정답:

> This result is consistent with previous research.

또는:

> This result is similar to the findings of previous studies.

설명:

- same with ❌
- the same as ✅
- similar to ✅
- consistent with ✅

---

### 4

> The model cannot generalize for other datasets.

정답:

> The model may not generalize to other datasets.

설명:

- generalize for ❌
- generalize to ✅

또한 실험 결과만으로 완전히 불가능하다고 단정하기 어렵기 때문에 `may not`이 더 적절합니다.

---

### 5

> We should be careful to interpret this result.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> We should interpret this result carefully.

또는:

> This result should be interpreted with caution.

설명:

`be careful to interpret`는 “빠뜨리지 말고 해석하다”처럼 들릴 수 있습니다. 연구 결과를 신중하게 해석한다는 뜻에는 위 표현들이 더 자연스럽습니다.

---

## 5. 60–90초 Speaking Practice — 2분

아래 흐름으로 소리 내어 말하세요.

> The study examined …  
> The main finding was that …  
> This suggests that …  
> The result is consistent with …  
> However, one limitation is that …  
> Therefore, the findings should be interpreted with caution.

오늘은 다음 표현을 의식적으로 사용하세요.

- suggests that
- may
- appears to
- is likely to
- does not necessarily mean

이 표현들은 약한 표현이 아니라, **근거 수준을 정확하게 조절하는 고급 영어 표현**입니다.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I recently read a study comparing small and large language models on reasoning tasks. The study found that larger models generally achieved higher accuracy, especially on complex problems. This suggests that model scale may contribute to stronger reasoning performance. However, it does not necessarily mean that larger models are always the best choice. They require more computational resources and may not be practical in every environment. One limitation is that the study relied heavily on benchmark datasets, so it is unclear whether the findings generalize to real-world applications. Overall, the results are promising, but they should be interpreted with caution.

## 오늘의 핵심 교정

> The result means that it is always better. ❌  
> The result suggests that it may be better under certain conditions. ✅

> It is same with previous research. ❌  
> It is consistent with previous research. ✅

> The model generalizes for other datasets. ❌  
> The model generalizes to other datasets. ✅

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
# Day 5 — Making a Recommendation Under Constraints

오늘 목표는 **여러 조건을 고려해 하나의 선택지를 추천하고, 그 판단 근거와 한계를 60–90초 동안 설명하는 것**입니다. 기술 면접, 설계 회의, 연구 제안에서 자주 필요한 말하기 방식입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 영어로 답해보세요.

> Suppose your team must choose between a larger, more accurate model and a smaller, more efficient model. Which would you recommend, and under what conditions?

답변에는 다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. 가장 중요한 제약 조건  
2. 두 선택지의 장단점  
3. 최종 추천  
4. 추천이 달라질 수 있는 조건  

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 판단 기준 제시

> The decision largely depends on **[constraint or priority]**.

예:

> The decision largely depends on the available computing resources and latency requirements.

`depend on` 앞에 `largely`, `primarily`, `ultimately`를 붙이면 더 자연스럽고 정교합니다.

### Template 2: 조건부 추천

> Given **[constraint]**, I would recommend **[option]** because **[reason]**.

예:

> Given the limited GPU memory, I would recommend the smaller model because it is easier to deploy and maintain.

### Template 3: 예외 조건 제시

> However, if **[condition changes]**, it may be worth considering **[alternative]**.

예:

> However, if accuracy is the highest priority, it may be worth considering the larger model.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. given
**~을 고려하면**

> Given the limited budget, a cloud-based solution may be more practical.

`Given + 명사`는 말하기와 문어체 모두에서 자주 사용됩니다.

주의:

> Considering about the budget … ❌  
> Given the budget … ✅  
> Considering the budget … ✅

---

### 2. prioritize
**~을 우선시하다**

> We should prioritize reliability over short-term speed.

자주 쓰는 구조:

> prioritize A over B

예:

> For production systems, I would prioritize stability over experimental performance.

---

### 3. feasible
**실현 가능한**

> The proposed approach is technically feasible but financially expensive.

논문과 프로젝트 제안서에서 매우 자주 등장합니다.

> It may not be feasible under the current resource constraints.

`possible`보다 현실적 조건과 실행 가능성을 강조합니다.

---

### 4. justify
**정당화하다, 충분한 근거를 제공하다**

> The performance gain may not justify the additional computational cost.

매우 유용한 비교 표현입니다.

> The improvement is too small to justify a full system redesign.

---

### 5. constraint
**제약 조건**

> Memory capacity is the main constraint in this environment.

논문 예문:

> The method performs well under strict computational constraints.

자주 쓰는 조합:

- budget constraint
- time constraint
- hardware constraint
- resource constraint

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고친 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> I recommend to use the smaller model.

정답:

> I recommend using the smaller model.

또는:

> I recommend that we use the smaller model.

설명:

`recommend` 뒤에는 일반적으로 동명사 또는 `that`절을 사용합니다.

- recommend to use ❌
- recommend using ✅
- recommend that we use ✅

---

### 2

> It depends on how much resources we have.

정답:

> It depends on how many resources we have.

또는 더 자연스럽게:

> It depends on how much computing capacity we have.

설명:

`resources`는 가산명사이므로 `many`를 사용합니다. 다만 컴퓨팅 자원을 하나의 총량으로 표현할 때는 `computing capacity`가 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 3

> Accuracy is more important rather than speed.

정답:

> Accuracy is more important than speed.

또는:

> We should prioritize accuracy over speed.

설명:

비교급에는 `than`을 사용합니다.

- more important rather than ❌
- more important than ✅

`rather than`은 다음처럼 선택을 대비할 때 사용합니다.

> We should improve the data rather than increase the model size.

---

### 4

> The larger model needs much more computations.

정답:

> The larger model requires significantly more computation.

설명:

`computation`은 전체 계산량을 뜻할 때 주로 불가산명사로 사용합니다.

- many computations: 개별 계산 작업들
- more computation: 더 많은 계산량

기술 영어에서는 `require`가 `need`보다 자주 쓰입니다.

---

### 5

> The accuracy improvement is not enough to pay the cost.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> The accuracy improvement may not justify the additional cost.

또는:

> The performance gain may not be worth the additional cost.

설명:

한국어의 “비용을 감수할 만큼 충분하지 않다”를 직역하기보다 `justify the cost` 또는 `be worth the cost`를 사용합니다.

---

## 5. 60–90초 Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 순서로 소리 내어 말하세요.

> The decision largely depends on …  
> The larger model offers …  
> However, it also requires …  
> Given our current constraints, I would recommend …  
> The performance gain may not justify …  
> However, if …, I would reconsider the decision.

오늘은 하나의 선택을 무조건 옳다고 말하지 말고, **조건부 판단**을 연습하세요.

아래 표현을 활용하면 더 고급스럽습니다.

- under the current constraints
- from a practical standpoint
- in a production environment
- unless accuracy is the sole priority
- this recommendation would change if …

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The decision largely depends on the deployment environment and the team’s priorities. A larger model may provide higher accuracy, but it also requires more memory, greater computational capacity, and higher operating costs. Given limited hardware resources, I would recommend the smaller model because it is more feasible to deploy and easier to maintain. From a practical standpoint, the performance gain of the larger model may not justify the additional cost and latency. However, this recommendation would change if accuracy were the sole priority or if the system were used for a high-risk application. In that case, it might be worth evaluating whether the larger model provides a meaningful improvement.

## 오늘의 핵심 교정

> I recommend to use it. ❌  
> I recommend using it. ✅

> It needs more computations. △  
> It requires more computation. ✅

> The improvement cannot pay the cost. ❌  
> The improvement may not justify the cost. ✅

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
# Day 6 — Expressing Uncertainty and Proposing Next Steps

오늘 목표는 **확실하지 않은 상황에서 추측과 사실을 구분하고, 합리적인 다음 행동을 제안하는 것**입니다. 연구 미팅, 디버깅, 기술 면접에서 매우 중요한 고급 말하기 능력입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A machine learning model performs well during testing but poorly after deployment. What might be causing the problem, and what would you investigate first?

답변에 다음 내용을 넣으세요.

1. 현재 확실히 아는 사실  
2. 가능한 원인 두 가지  
3. 가장 먼저 확인할 항목  
4. 추가 조사가 필요한 이유  

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 사실과 추측 구분

> What we know for certain is that **[fact]**, but it is still unclear whether **[uncertain point]**.

예:

> What we know for certain is that performance dropped after deployment, but it is still unclear whether the issue comes from the data or the system configuration.

### Template 2: 가능한 원인 제시

> One possible explanation is that **[cause]**, although another possibility is that **[alternative cause]**.

예:

> One possible explanation is that the production data differs from the training data, although another possibility is that preprocessing is inconsistent.

### Template 3: 다음 행동 제안

> To determine the root cause, I would first **[action]** and then **[next action]**.

예:

> To determine the root cause, I would first compare the input distributions and then verify the preprocessing pipeline.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. it is unclear whether
**~인지 불분명하다**

> It is unclear whether the performance drop is caused by data drift.

논문 예문:

> It remains unclear whether the findings generalize to larger datasets.

`I don’t know`보다 객관적이고 전문적인 표현입니다.

---

### 2. plausible explanation
**그럴듯한 설명, 개연성 있는 원인**

> A plausible explanation is that the training data did not represent real-world conditions.

`possible explanation`보다 근거가 어느 정도 있는 추론에 적합합니다.

---

### 3. rule out
**가능성을 배제하다**

> We need to rule out configuration errors before retraining the model.

자주 쓰는 구조:

> rule out a possibility  
> rule out hardware failure  
> rule out data leakage

---

### 4. account for
**~을 설명하다, 고려하다**

> The difference in data quality may account for the performance gap.

논문 표현:

> The current analysis does not account for seasonal variation.

문맥에 따라 두 가지 뜻이 있습니다.

- explain: 원인을 설명하다  
- consider: 분석에 반영하다  

---

### 5. warrant
**~할 필요가 있을 정도의 근거가 되다**

> The performance decline warrants further investigation.

논문 예문:

> These findings warrant additional experiments.

`need`보다 격식 있고 학술적인 표현입니다.

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고친 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> Maybe the reason is because the data is different.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> One possible reason is that the data is different.

또는:

> The problem may be caused by differences in the data.

설명:

`the reason is because`는 흔하지만 다소 중복된 구조입니다.

- the reason is that ✅
- this is because ✅
- one reason is that ✅

---

### 2

> I am not sure this problem comes from the data.

정답:

> I am not sure whether this problem comes from the data.

또는:

> It is unclear whether the problem is caused by the data.

설명:

두 가지 가능성 사이의 불확실성을 말할 때 `whether`를 사용합니다.

---

### 3

> We need to check the problem more deeply.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> We need to investigate the problem more thoroughly.

또는:

> The issue requires further investigation.

설명:

기술적 문제에는 `check deeply`보다 `investigate thoroughly`가 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 4

> The data distribution can be changed after deployment.

정답:

> The data distribution may have changed after deployment.

설명:

여기서는 일반적인 가능성이 아니라 과거에 변화가 일어났을 가능성을 추측합니다.

- may have changed: 변했을지도 모른다  
- can be changed: 변경될 수 있다 / 누군가 변경할 수 있다

---

### 5

> We should find the root reason before retrain the model.

정답:

> We should identify the root cause before retraining the model.

설명:

- root reason △
- root cause ✅
- before retrain ❌
- before retraining ✅

전치사 `before` 뒤에 행동을 넣을 때는 동명사를 사용할 수 있습니다.

---

## 5. 60–90초 Speaking Practice — 2분

아래 흐름을 따라 소리 내어 말하세요.

> What we know for certain is that …  
> However, it is unclear whether …  
> One plausible explanation is that …  
> Another possibility is that …  
> Before making a conclusion, we should rule out …  
> To determine the root cause, I would first …  
> The issue warrants further investigation because …

오늘은 확신의 정도를 구분해보세요.

**강한 확신**

> The problem is caused by data drift.

**중간 정도의 추론**

> The problem is likely to be caused by data drift.

**가능성 제시**

> The problem may be caused by data drift.

**매우 조심스러운 추론**

> One possible explanation is that data drift contributed to the problem.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> What we know for certain is that the model performed well during testing but showed lower accuracy after deployment. However, it is still unclear whether the problem is caused by data drift, inconsistent preprocessing, or a system-level issue. One plausible explanation is that the production data differs from the training data. Another possibility is that some preprocessing steps were implemented differently in the deployment pipeline. To determine the root cause, I would first compare the training and production data distributions. I would then verify that the same preprocessing logic is being used in both environments. Before retraining the model, we should rule out configuration and pipeline errors. The performance decline warrants further investigation because retraining alone may not solve the underlying problem.

## 오늘의 핵심 교정

> Maybe the reason is because … ❌  
> One possible reason is that … ✅

> I am not sure this comes from the data. △  
> I am not sure whether this comes from the data. ✅

> We should check it more deeply. △  
> We should investigate it more thoroughly. ✅
