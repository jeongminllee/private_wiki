### ❓ 질문 (User)

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
다음은 Foreign Language Freelance Writer(Korean) 직무에 맞춘 **이력서 템플릿**입니다. 

---

## [Your Name]  
**Email**: [Your Email Address]  
**Phone**: [Your Phone Number]  
**LinkedIn/Portfolio/Website**: [Your Profile Link]  
**Address**: [Your City, State, Country]

---

### 1. **Professional Summary**  
한 문단으로 작성하며, 본인의 경력, 역량, 그리고 해당 직무에 적합한 주요 특징을 강조합니다.  
예:  
"Korean language specialist with extensive experience in freelance writing, localization, and cultural adaptation. Skilled in delivering high-quality content that meets the unique needs of diverse audiences. Passionate about bridging linguistic and cultural gaps."

---

### 2. **Skills**  
직무와 관련된 기술 및 역량을 리스트 형식으로 정리합니다.  
- Native-level fluency in Korean and professional proficiency in English  
- Exceptional writing and editing skills in Korean  
- Familiarity with AP Style, SEO, or localization tools (e.g., CAT tools)  
- Deadline-oriented and highly adaptable to client requirements  

---

### 3. **Professional Experience**  
**[Job Title]** – [Company Name], [Location]  
[Start Date] – [End Date]  
- 직무 성과를 중심으로 간결하게 작성합니다 (숫자와 결과 중심).  
예:  
- Authored over 50 high-quality Korean articles per month on topics such as [Topic Examples].  
- Localized marketing materials and website content for a global audience, increasing engagement by 20%.  
- Conducted rigorous proofreading to ensure linguistic accuracy and cultural relevance.

---

### 4. **Education**  
**[Degree Name]** – [University Name], [Location]  
[Start Date] – [End Date]  
- 추가로 관련 학업 경험이나 프로젝트를 적습니다.  

---

### 5. **Certifications**  
직무 관련 자격증 및 수료증을 나열합니다.  
- [Certification Name], [Issuing Organization]  
- [Certification Name], [Issuing Organization]  

---

### 6. **Writing Samples (Optional)**  
링크나 설명을 추가하여 과거 작업물을 보여줄 수 있습니다.  
- Example: "[Title of Work]" – [Brief Description or Link]
