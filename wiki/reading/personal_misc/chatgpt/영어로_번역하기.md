### ❓ 질문 (User)

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
다음처럼 자연스럽게 말할 수 있어요:

- **Neutral:** *We enlisted in the same month and served in the military together for 21 months.*
- **Resume-friendly:** *We enlisted in the same month and completed 21 months of military service together.*
- **Casual:** *We joined in the same month and served together for 21 months.*

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
포멀하게는 아래처럼 표현할 수 있어요:

- *We commenced our military service in the same month and served alongside one another for twenty-one months.*  
- *We entered military service in the same month and served together for a period of twenty-one months.*  
- *We were inducted in the same month and subsequently served together for twenty-one months.*  
- *We began our military duty in the same month and jointly completed twenty-one months of service.*  
- *We were in the same enlistment cohort and served together for twenty-one months.*  

(필요하면 **branch/기간**을 넣어 더 격식 있게 쓸 수 있어요: *We commenced our service in the Republic of Korea Army in March 2022 and served together for twenty-one months.*)

### ❓ 질문 (User)
Commenced가 무슨 말이야 
2014년 5월부터 우리는 대한민국 육군에서 서비스를 xxx 했고 21개월간 같이 보냈다 잖아

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
좋은 질문이에요!  
**commence**는 격식 있는 표현으로 **“시작하다/개시하다(begin/start)”**라는 뜻입니다.  
그래서 *“commenced our (military) service”* = *“(군)복무를 시작했다”*예요.

요청하신 문장을 **포멀하게** 다듬으면:

- *We enlisted in the Republic of Korea Army in May 2014 and served together for twenty-one months.*  
- *We commenced our military service in the Republic of Korea Army in May 2014 and served together for twenty-one months.*  
- *We entered active duty in the Republic of Korea Army in May 2014 and subsequently served together for twenty-one months.*

작은 팁: **from May 2014**는 보통 **to/until**과 짝을 이룹니다. 기간을 끝까지 쓸 거면  
*“from May 2014 to February 2016”*처럼 쓰고, 끝을 특정하지 않으면 *“in May 2014”*가 더 자연스러워요.

### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
아주 잘 쓰셨어요! 문법·톤 모두 자연스럽습니다.  
더 포멀하게 다듬고 싶다면 숫자 표기를 **twenty-one**으로 쓰는 정도만 손보면 좋아요:

- **Your sentence (correct):** *We commenced our service in the Republic of Korea Army in May 2012 and served together for 21 months.*  
- **Polished formal:** *We commenced our military service in the Republic of Korea Army in May 2012 and served together for **twenty-one** months.*  
- **Slightly more formal:** *We commenced our military service with the Republic of Korea Army in May 2012 and subsequently served together for twenty-one months.*  
- **Alternative (active-duty nuance):** *We entered active duty in the Republic of Korea Army in May 2012 and served together for twenty-one months.*  

참고: 첫 언급 뒤에는 **(ROKA)**로 약어 사용 가능해요.
