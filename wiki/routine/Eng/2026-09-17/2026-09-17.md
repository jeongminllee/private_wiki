# Day 39 — Explaining Model Confidence and Calibration

오늘 목표는 **모델이 높은 확률로 예측했다고 해서 실제로 그 예측이 항상 맞는 것은 아니라는 점**을 설명하는 것입니다. 오늘은 **prediction confidence → calibration → overconfidence → reliability → deployment decision** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A model says it is 95% confident in many predictions, but only about 80% of those predictions are actually correct. Would you trust the confidence scores?

답변에는 **confidence와 actual accuracy의 차이, 왜 overconfidence가 위험한지, 어떤 상황에서 문제가 커지는지, 어떻게 평가해야 하는지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> I would be cautious about trusting the confidence scores because the model appears to be overconfident.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **예측 확률과 실제 정확도를 비교**할 때 사용합니다.

> If the model is well calibrated, predictions made with **[X%] confidence** should be correct approximately **[X%] of the time**.

예:

> If the model is well calibrated, predictions made with 90% confidence should be correct approximately 90% of the time.

두 번째는 **overconfidence를 설명**할 때 유용합니다.

> The model appears to be overconfident because **[confidence]** is substantially higher than **[observed accuracy]**.

예:

> The model appears to be overconfident because its reported confidence is substantially higher than its observed accuracy.

세 번째는 **실무 영향까지 연결**할 때 씁니다.

> This becomes particularly problematic when **[decision]** depends on **[confidence score]**.

예:

> This becomes particularly problematic when an automated system decides whether to escalate a case based on the confidence score.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**calibration**은 모델이 출력하는 confidence와 실제 정답률이 얼마나 잘 일치하는지를 의미합니다.

> The model has high accuracy but poor calibration.

자주 쓰는 표현은 `well calibrated`, `poorly calibrated`, `calibration error`입니다.

**overconfident**는 실제 정확도보다 지나치게 높은 확신을 보이는 상태입니다.

> The classifier is overconfident on unfamiliar inputs.

일반 영어에서도 사용할 수 있습니다.

> He sounded confident, but his conclusion was not well supported.

**uncertainty**는 모델이 얼마나 확실하지 않은지를 의미합니다.

> A reliable system should represent uncertainty appropriately.

논문에서는 `uncertainty estimation`이라는 표현이 자주 나옵니다.

**reliable**은 결과나 신호를 실제 의사결정에 믿고 활용할 수 있다는 뜻입니다.

> The confidence score is not reliable enough for automatic decision-making.

일상 영어에서도 매우 자주 사용합니다.

> We need a reliable source of information.

**threshold**는 특정 조치를 실행하기 위한 기준값입니다.

> The system sends a prediction for human review when confidence falls below a predefined threshold.

실무에서 `confidence threshold`, `decision threshold`, `classification threshold`를 자주 사용합니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The model has 95% confidence but correct only 80%.

정답:

> The model reports 95% confidence, but only 80% of those predictions are correct.

설명: `correct`는 여기서 동사가 아니므로 바로 사용할 수 없습니다.

- correct only 80% ❌
- 80% are correct ✅

### 2

> The model believes too much its prediction.

더 자연스럽게:

> The model is too confident in its predictions.

또는 기술적으로:

> The model is overconfident.

설명: 영어에서는 모델의 확신을 말할 때 `believe too much`보다 `be confident in`을 사용합니다.

- confident in its prediction ✅
- overconfident ✅

### 3

> High confidence doesn't always mean high accurate.

정답:

> High confidence does not always mean high accuracy.

설명: `accurate`는 형용사이고 `accuracy`는 명사입니다.

- high accurate ❌
- high accuracy ✅

또는:

> A highly confident prediction is not necessarily accurate.

이 표현도 매우 유용합니다.

### 4

> We should check confidence is reliable or not.

더 자연스럽게:

> We should check whether the confidence scores are reliable.

설명: 간접의문문에서는 `whether`가 자연스럽습니다.

- check confidence is reliable or not △
- check whether the confidence scores are reliable ✅

### 5

> If confidence is under 70%, human should check it.

더 자연스럽게:

> If the confidence score is below 70%, a human reviewer should check the prediction.

설명:

수치 기준에서는 `under`도 가능하지만 `below`가 더 자연스럽습니다.

그리고 `human`만 쓰기보다 역할을 구체화하면 더 전문적입니다.

- human should check it △
- a human reviewer should check the prediction ✅

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The model reports very high confidence, but …  
> This suggests that the model may be …  
> A well-calibrated model should …  
> Poor calibration is especially risky when …  
> I would therefore evaluate …  
> I would also set a threshold for …  
> Based on the current evidence, I would …

초급식 표현:

> The model says 95%, so we can trust it.

더 정확한 표현:

> A high confidence score does not necessarily mean that the prediction is correct.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> The gap between predicted confidence and observed accuracy indicates substantial miscalibration, which could make confidence-based decision rules unreliable in deployment.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I would not fully trust the confidence scores if predictions reported with 95% confidence are correct only about 80% of the time. This suggests that the model is overconfident and poorly calibrated. A well-calibrated model should produce confidence estimates that roughly correspond to observed accuracy. Poor calibration can be especially problematic in automated systems because confidence scores are often used to decide whether a prediction should be accepted, rejected, or sent for human review. I would therefore evaluate calibration across different confidence ranges and test whether the problem becomes worse on unfamiliar or difficult inputs. Before deployment, I would also consider recalibration methods and introduce a conservative confidence threshold for high-risk decisions.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“A high confidence score does not necessarily mean the prediction is correct.” → “The model appears to be overconfident.” → “A reliable system should represent uncertainty appropriately.”**
