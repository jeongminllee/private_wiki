# Day 1 — Explaining Your Learning Routine

오늘 목표는 **내가 무엇을 공부하고 있으며, 왜 공부하는지 60–90초 동안 자연스럽게 설명하는 것**입니다.

## 1. 워밍업 — 2분

다음 질문에 영어로 짧게 답해보세요.

> What are you studying these days, and why is it important to you?

먼저 문장을 완벽하게 만들려고 하지 말고, 아래 세 가지 정보만 말하세요.

* 요즘 공부하는 것
* 공부하는 이유
* 앞으로 이루고 싶은 목표

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 현재 학습 내용

> These days, I’m focusing on **[topic]** because I want to **[goal]**.

예:

> These days, I’m focusing on machine learning infrastructure because I want to understand how large models are trained and served.

### Template 2: 어려움과 대응

> One challenge I’ve faced is **[problem]**, so I’ve been trying to **[solution]**.

예:

> One challenge I’ve faced is understanding technical documentation, so I’ve been trying to read it more systematically.

### Template 3: 현재 수준과 장기 목표

> I’m not fully comfortable with **[skill]** yet, but my long-term goal is to **[goal]**.

예:

> I’m not fully comfortable with speaking English yet, but my long-term goal is to communicate confidently in technical discussions.

**말하기 팁:**
`I want to`만 반복하지 말고 다음 표현도 번갈아 사용하세요.

* My goal is to …
* I’m working toward …
* I hope to …
* I’m aiming to …

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. focus on

**~에 집중하다**

> I’m currently focusing on improving my technical English.

주의:

* `focus in` ❌
* `focus on` ✅

### 2. work toward

**장기적인 목표를 향해 노력하다**

> I’m working toward becoming an ML engineer.

`work toward`는 아직 목표를 달성하지 않았지만 꾸준히 진행 중이라는 의미입니다.

### 3. encounter

**문제나 어려움을 마주치다**

논문과 업무 영어에서 `meet a problem`보다 자연스럽습니다.

> I encountered several issues during model training.

### 4. significant

**중요한, 상당한**

논문에서 매우 자주 등장합니다.

> The new method showed a significant improvement in accuracy.

주의: `significant`는 문맥에 따라 “중요한” 또는 “통계적으로 유의한”이라는 뜻입니다.

### 5. be applicable to

**~에 적용할 수 있다**

> This approach is applicable to real-world problems.

논문 표현:

> The proposed method may be applicable to other domains.

## 4. 문장 교정 연습 — 3분

아래 문장을 먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1

> I am studying machine learning since two years.

정답:

> I have been studying machine learning for two years.

설명:

* 현재까지 계속된 행동: `have been studying`
* 기간: `for two years`
* 시작 시점: `since 2024`

### 2

> I want to improve my English speaking skill.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> I want to improve my English speaking skills.

또는:

> I want to improve my spoken English.

`speaking skills`는 보통 복수형으로 사용합니다.

### 3

> I experienced many difficulties to understand papers.

정답:

> I had difficulty understanding academic papers.

또는:

> I encountered many difficulties when reading academic papers.

`difficulty` 뒤에는 일반적으로 동명사 `-ing`가 옵니다.

* difficulty understanding ✅
* difficulty to understand ❌

### 4

> This knowledge can apply to my work.

정답:

> This knowledge can be applied to my work.

설명:

지식이 “적용되는 것”이므로 수동태가 필요합니다.

* I can apply this knowledge.
* This knowledge can be applied.

---

## 5. 60초 말하기 — 2분

다음 구조로 소리 내어 말하세요.

1. These days, I’m focusing on …
2. I’m studying this because …
3. One challenge I’ve faced is …
4. To overcome this, I’ve been …
5. My long-term goal is …

가능하면 녹음한 뒤 다음 항목을 확인하세요.

* 문장마다 `I`로만 시작하지 않았는가
* 과거부터 지금까지 이어지는 행동에 현재완료를 사용했는가
* `because` 뒤에 구체적인 이유를 붙였는가
* 한 문장을 너무 길게 말하지 않았는가

---

## 모델 답안 — 1분

> These days, I’m focusing on machine learning, English, and computer science fundamentals. I’m studying these subjects because I want to become a more capable machine learning engineer. One challenge I’ve faced is explaining technical concepts clearly in English. To overcome this, I’ve been practicing short speaking exercises and reading academic papers regularly. I’m not fully comfortable speaking English yet, but I’m working toward participating confidently in technical discussions. My long-term goal is to use English naturally when reading papers, attending interviews, and collaborating with international engineers.

### 한 단계 높은 표현

기본:

> I want to become a better engineer.

발전:

> I’m working toward becoming an engineer who can solve complex problems and communicate technical ideas clearly.

오늘의 핵심 교정은 이것입니다:

> I study English since two years. ❌
> I have been studying English for two years. ✅
