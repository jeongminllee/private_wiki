# Day 40 — Explaining Whether an Improvement Generalizes Beyond One Benchmark

오늘 목표는 **한 benchmark에서 좋아진 성능이 다른 데이터셋이나 실제 환경에서도 유지되는지 설명하는 것**입니다. 오늘은 **single-benchmark result → generalization → external validity → cross-dataset evaluation → cautious conclusion** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A new model performs extremely well on one benchmark, but the paper does not report results on any other dataset. How confident are you that the improvement will generalize?

답변에는 **왜 한 benchmark만으로 충분하지 않은지, benchmark-specific optimization 가능성, 다른 데이터셋에서 무엇을 확인해야 하는지, 현재 어느 정도까지 결론 내릴 수 있는지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The result is encouraging, but strong performance on a single benchmark does not necessarily guarantee that the improvement will generalize.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **한정된 평가 범위를 지적할 때** 사용합니다.

> The result is convincing within **[evaluation setting]**, but it is unclear whether it generalizes to **[broader setting]**.

예:

> The result is convincing within the current benchmark, but it is unclear whether it generalizes to other domains.

두 번째는 **benchmark 특화 가능성을 설명할 때** 유용합니다.

> One concern is that the method may be optimized specifically for **[benchmark/task]** rather than learning **[general capability]**.

예:

> One concern is that the method may be optimized specifically for this benchmark rather than learning a broadly useful representation.

세 번째는 **더 강한 평가를 제안할 때** 씁니다.

> To establish broader generalization, I would evaluate the model across **[datasets/domains/settings]**.

예:

> To establish broader generalization, I would evaluate the model across multiple datasets with different distributions and difficulty levels.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**generalize**는 학습하거나 평가한 조건을 넘어 새로운 데이터에서도 잘 작동한다는 뜻입니다.

> The model generalizes well to previously unseen domains.

명사형은 `generalization`입니다.

> Generalization is more important than performance on a single test set.

**external validity**는 연구 결과가 현재 실험 환경 밖에서도 적용될 수 있는지를 의미합니다.

> The study has strong internal results but limited external validity.

연구 논문에서 자주 쓰이는 표현입니다.

**benchmark-specific**은 특정 benchmark에만 잘 맞도록 특화된 상태를 뜻합니다.

> The performance gain may be benchmark-specific.

예:

> A benchmark-specific improvement may not translate into real-world performance.

**transfer**는 한 환경에서 얻은 성능이나 능력이 다른 환경에서도 유지되는 것을 의미합니다.

> The improvement did not transfer to the second dataset.

논문에서는 `transfer performance`, `transfer learning`, `cross-domain transfer` 같은 표현도 자주 나옵니다.

**hold up**은 결과나 주장이 다른 조건에서도 여전히 유지된다는 뜻입니다. 조금 더 자연스러운 구어 표현입니다.

> The result looks strong, but we need to see whether it holds up on other datasets.

학술적인 표현으로 바꾸면:

> We need to determine whether the effect remains consistent across datasets.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The model performed good in this benchmark.

정답:

> The model performed well on this benchmark.

설명: 두 가지를 고쳐야 합니다.

- perform good ❌
- perform well ✅

그리고 benchmark와 함께는 보통 `on`을 씁니다.

- in the benchmark △
- on the benchmark ✅

### 2

> We don't know this result can generalize or not.

더 자연스럽게:

> We don't know whether this result will generalize.

또는:

> It is unclear whether this result will generalize.

설명: `whether`를 사용하면 훨씬 자연스럽고 학술적으로 들립니다.

### 3

> Maybe they fitted too much to the benchmark.

더 정확하게:

> The method may be overly optimized for the benchmark.

또는:

> The model may have overfit to the benchmark.

설명: `fit too much`보다 `overfit`이 정확한 기술 용어입니다.

### 4

> They should test another datasets.

정답:

> They should test the model on other datasets.

설명:

- another + 단수명사
- other + 복수명사

따라서:

- another dataset ✅
- other datasets ✅

### 5

> One benchmark is not enough for say general performance.

정답:

> One benchmark is not enough to establish general performance.

더 자연스럽게:

> A single benchmark is not sufficient to establish broad generalization.

설명:

- enough for say ❌
- enough to say ✅

하지만 연구 문맥에서는 `establish`와 `broad generalization`이 더 정확합니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The model performs very well on …  
> However, this result is based on only …  
> One concern is that …  
> The improvement may therefore be benchmark-specific rather than …  
> To evaluate broader generalization, I would …  
> If the same trend holds across …  
> I would have greater confidence that …

초급식 표현:

> The model is good because it got the best score.

더 정확한 표현:

> The model achieves the best score on this benchmark, but we do not yet know whether the improvement generalizes.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although the model performs exceptionally well on the reported benchmark, the absence of cross-dataset evaluation limits our ability to determine whether the gain reflects broad generalization or benchmark-specific optimization.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The strong benchmark result is promising, but I would be cautious about assuming that the improvement will generalize. Performance on a single benchmark may reflect properties that are specific to that dataset, especially if the method was heavily optimized for it. To assess broader generalization, I would evaluate the model on multiple datasets with different domains, data distributions, and difficulty levels. I would also check whether the relative improvement over the baseline remains consistent across those settings. If the same trend holds on independent datasets, I would have much greater confidence that the method provides a genuine and transferable benefit. Based on the current evidence, I would say that the model performs strongly on the reported benchmark, but broader generalization has not yet been established.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“Strong performance on one benchmark does not guarantee generalization.” → “The improvement may be benchmark-specific.” → “We need cross-dataset evaluation to establish broader generalization.”**
