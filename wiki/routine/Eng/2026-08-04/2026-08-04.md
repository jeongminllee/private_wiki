# Day 7 — Defending a Technical Decision Professionally

오늘 목표는 **내 의견을 명확히 제시하면서도 반대 의견을 인정하고, 근거를 들어 기술적 결정을 방어하는 것**입니다. 면접, 코드 리뷰, 연구 미팅에서 매우 유용합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> Your teammate wants to use a large, complex model, but you believe a smaller model is more appropriate. How would you explain your position without dismissing their idea?

다음 네 단계를 포함하세요.

1. 상대방 의견의 장점 인정
2. 내 핵심 우려 사항 제시
3. 근거를 들어 대안 추천
4. 검증 가능한 타협안 제안

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 반대 의견 인정하기

> I understand the rationale behind **[proposal]**, especially because **[advantage]**.

예:

> I understand the rationale behind using a larger model, especially because it may provide higher accuracy.

`I understand your idea, but …`보다 더 전문적이고 구체적입니다.

### Template 2: 우려 사항 제시하기

> My main concern is that **[risk]**, which could lead to **[consequence]**.

예:

> My main concern is that the model may require too much memory, which could lead to higher latency in production.

### Template 3: 근거 기반 타협안 제안하기

> Rather than making the decision based on assumptions, we could **[experiment]** and compare **[metrics]**.

예:

> Rather than making the decision based on assumptions, we could benchmark both models and compare accuracy, latency, and operating cost.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. rationale

**판단이나 결정의 논리적 근거**

> Could you explain the rationale behind this design choice?

논문·업무 표현:

> The rationale for selecting this dataset was not clearly stated.

자주 쓰는 구조:

* the rationale behind a decision
* the rationale for using a method

---

### 2. raise a concern

**우려 사항을 제기하다**

> I would like to raise a concern about the model’s memory usage.

`complain about`보다 중립적이고 전문적인 표현입니다.

> Several reviewers raised concerns about the limited sample size.

---

### 3. outweigh

**~보다 더 중요하다, 단점을 능가하다**

> The benefits may not outweigh the additional computational cost.

반대 구조:

> The improvement in reliability outweighs the increase in latency.

`outweigh` 뒤에는 별도의 `than`을 쓰지 않습니다.

---

### 4. evidence-based

**근거에 기반한**

> We should make an evidence-based decision rather than rely on intuition.

논문·조직 의사결정에서 자주 사용됩니다.

> The recommendation is based on empirical evidence.

---

### 5. compromise

**타협안 또는 서로 양보하여 합의하다**

명사:

> A small-scale benchmark could be a reasonable compromise.

동사:

> We should not compromise reliability for speed.

주의할 점은 두 번째 문장에서 `compromise`가 **희생하다**라는 뜻이라는 것입니다.

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고친 다음 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> I agree your opinion partly.

정답:

> I partly agree with your opinion.

더 자연스럽게:

> I agree with part of your argument.

설명:

`agree`는 사람이나 의견 앞에서 보통 `with`를 사용합니다.

* agree your opinion ❌
* agree with your opinion ✅

---

### 2

> I think the disadvantage is bigger than the advantage.

더 전문적인 표현:

> I think the disadvantages outweigh the advantages.

또는:

> The potential benefits may not justify the additional complexity.

설명:

추상적인 비교에서는 `bigger`보다 `outweigh`, `justify`가 정확합니다.

---

### 3

> We need to discuss about this issue more.

정답:

> We need to discuss this issue further.

설명:

`discuss`는 타동사이므로 `about`을 쓰지 않습니다.

* discuss about the issue ❌
* discuss the issue ✅

반면 다음은 가능합니다.

> We need to talk about the issue.

---

### 4

> According to my experience, the smaller model is better.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> Based on my experience, the smaller model is more practical.

설명:

`according to`는 주로 외부 정보나 다른 사람의 말을 인용할 때 사용합니다.

> According to the report, accuracy improved.

자신의 경험을 말할 때는 다음이 자연스럽습니다.

* Based on my experience
* In my experience

---

### 5

> Why don’t we test both models before to decide?

정답:

> Why don’t we test both models before deciding?

또는:

> Why don’t we test both models before we make a decision?

설명:

전치사로 쓰인 `before` 뒤에는 동명사를 사용할 수 있습니다.

* before to decide ❌
* before deciding ✅
* before we decide ✅

---

## 5. 60–90초 Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름을 따라 말해보세요.

> I understand the rationale behind …
> The main advantage is that …
> However, I would like to raise a concern about …
> My concern is based on …
> In this case, the benefits may not outweigh …
> Rather than relying on assumptions, we could …
> That would allow us to make a more evidence-based decision.

### 즉시 적용할 말하기 원칙

상대 의견에 반대할 때 아래 표현은 피하는 것이 좋습니다.

> You are wrong.
> That model is too inefficient.
> My idea is better.

대신 판단의 대상을 **사람이 아니라 조건과 근거**로 바꾸세요.

> I see the potential benefit, but I’m concerned about the deployment cost.

> That approach may work well in research, but it could be difficult to maintain in production.

> Under our current constraints, the smaller model appears to be more practical.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I understand the rationale behind using a larger model, especially because it may achieve higher accuracy on complex tasks. However, I would like to raise a concern about its deployment cost and latency. Under our current hardware constraints, the additional performance may not outweigh the increase in memory usage and operating cost. Based on my experience, a smaller model would be more practical to deploy, monitor, and maintain. That said, I would not reject the larger model without testing it. Rather than relying on assumptions, we could benchmark both models on the same dataset and compare accuracy, latency, memory consumption, and cost. A limited experiment would give us stronger evidence and help the team make a more evidence-based decision.
