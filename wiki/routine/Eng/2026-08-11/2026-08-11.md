# Day 12 — Explaining *Why* a Method Works

오늘 목표는 단순히 “성능이 좋아졌다”를 말하는 데서 더 나아가, **왜 그런 결과가 나왔는지 가능한 메커니즘을 설명하고, 그 설명이 아직 가설인지 근거가 충분한지 구분하는 것**입니다. 연구 미팅, 논문 리뷰, 기술 면접에서 자주 필요한 C1형 말하기입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 답해보세요.

> A new training method improves model performance consistently across several runs. How would you explain why it might be working, and how would you test your explanation?

답변에 다음을 포함하세요.

1. 관찰된 결과
2. 가능한 작동 원리
3. 그 설명이 아직 가설인지 여부
4. 어떻게 검증할 것인지

---

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: 결과와 설명 분리

> The results show that **[observation]**, but they do not yet explain why **[mechanism]**.

예:

> The results show that the model converges faster, but they do not yet explain why the optimization is more stable.

### Template 2: 가능한 메커니즘 제시

> One hypothesis is that **[mechanism]**, which may lead to **[effect]**.

예:

> One hypothesis is that the new objective produces smoother gradients, which may lead to more stable training.

### Template 3: 가설 검증

> To test this hypothesis, I would **[experiment]** while keeping **[other factors]** fixed.

예:

> To test this hypothesis, I would compare gradient variance while keeping the data, optimizer, and learning rate fixed.

---

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. hypothesis

**가설**

> One hypothesis is that the improvement comes from better regularization.

논문에서는 `guess`보다 훨씬 적절합니다.

> We tested the hypothesis using an ablation study.

---

### 2. mechanism

**작동 원리, 메커니즘**

> The exact mechanism behind the improvement is still unclear.

자주 쓰는 표현:

* underlying mechanism
* possible mechanism
* mechanism behind the effect

---

### 3. attribute A to B

**A를 B의 결과로 돌리다 / B 때문이라고 해석하다**

> We cannot yet attribute the performance gain to the new loss function alone.

중요한 점은 **인과관계를 단정할 때 매우 조심해서 사용해야 한다**는 것입니다.

---

### 4. isolate

**특정 효과만 분리해서 확인하다**

> The ablation study was designed to isolate the effect of the attention module.

연구 영어에서 매우 자주 나옵니다.

> We changed only one component to isolate its contribution.

---

### 5. account for

**~을 설명하다 / ~을 고려하다**

> The proposed explanation may account for the improvement in stability.

또는:

> The analysis should account for differences in dataset size.

문맥에 따라 두 뜻이 있습니다.

* explain: 원인을 설명하다
* consider: 분석에 반영하다

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1

> The reason why the model improved is because of the new loss.

더 자연스럽게:

> One possible reason the model improved is the new loss function.

또는:

> The improvement may be due to the new loss function.

설명: `the reason is because`는 중복된 느낌이 있습니다.

* the reason is that ✅
* this is because ✅
* may be due to ✅

---

### 2

> I think this method works well because it can reduce the variance maybe.

정답:

> I think this method may work well because it reduces variance.

더 학술적으로:

> One hypothesis is that the method works by reducing variance.

설명: 영어에서는 `can + maybe`처럼 불확실성 표현을 겹치지 않는 것이 좋습니다.

* can reduce maybe △
* may reduce ✅
* one hypothesis is that ✅

---

### 3

> We need an experiment to know the real reason.

더 자연스럽게:

> We need an experiment to identify the underlying mechanism.

또는:

> We need additional experiments to test this explanation.

설명: 연구 문맥에서는 `real reason`보다 `underlying mechanism`이 더 정확합니다.

---

### 4

> The better result came from only the new component.

더 신중한 표현:

> We cannot yet conclude that the improvement came solely from the new component.

설명: `only`보다 `solely`가 학술 영어에 더 잘 맞습니다. 더 중요한 점은 **현재 실험만으로 인과관계를 단정하지 않는 것**입니다.

---

### 5

> We should compare after removing each components.

정답:

> We should compare performance after removing each component.

또는:

> We should conduct an ablation study by removing one component at a time.

설명:

* each components ❌
* each component ✅

`each` 뒤에는 단수명사를 사용합니다.

---

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름을 따라 말해보세요.

> The results consistently show that …
> However, this does not necessarily explain why …
> One hypothesis is that …
> This may account for …
> At this stage, I would avoid attributing the improvement solely to …
> To test the hypothesis, I would …
> If the effect remains after controlling for …, that would provide stronger evidence that …

오늘 핵심은 **결과와 설명을 분리하는 것**입니다.

너무 강한 표현:

> The method works because the gradients are more stable.

더 나은 표현:

> One possible explanation is that the method produces more stable gradients.

더 고급스러운 표현:

> The observed improvement may be explained by reduced gradient variance, although this mechanism still needs to be validated experimentally.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The results consistently show that the new training method improves performance, but that alone does not explain why it works. One hypothesis is that the method reduces gradient variance, which may lead to more stable optimization and faster convergence. However, I would avoid attributing the improvement solely to this mechanism without additional evidence. To test the hypothesis, I would measure gradient statistics across multiple runs and compare them with the baseline while keeping the dataset, optimizer, learning rate, and random seeds controlled. I would also conduct an ablation study to isolate the contribution of each component. If the performance improvement remains strongly associated with reduced gradient variance, that would provide more convincing evidence for the proposed explanation.

오늘 기억할 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“One hypothesis is that …” → “I would avoid attributing the improvement solely to …” → “We need to test the mechanism experimentally.”**
