# Day 45 — Explaining Limitations Without Undermining Your Research

오늘 목표는 **연구의 한계를 인정하면서도 결과의 가치를 지나치게 약화시키지 않는 방식으로 설명하는 것**입니다. 오늘은 **main finding → limitation → scope → implication → next step** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> Your model performs well on a carefully controlled dataset, but the dataset is relatively small and comes from only one domain. How would you present this result without overstating your conclusion?

답변에는 **무엇을 확실히 말할 수 있는지, 무엇은 아직 말할 수 없는지, 데이터셋의 한계가 왜 중요한지, 다음 실험은 무엇이어야 하는지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The results are encouraging within the current experimental setting, but the scope of the conclusion should remain limited.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **결과의 적용 범위를 제한해서 말할 때** 사용합니다.

> The findings are valid within **[current setting]**, but they may not generalize to **[broader setting]**.

예:

> The findings are valid within the current dataset, but they may not generalize to other domains.

두 번째는 **한계를 인정하면서 결과의 의미를 유지할 때** 유용합니다.

> This limitation does not invalidate the result, but it does restrict **[what we can conclude / how broadly we can apply it]**.

예:

> This limitation does not invalidate the result, but it does restrict how broadly we can apply it.

세 번째는 **후속 연구 방향으로 자연스럽게 연결할 때** 씁니다.

> A natural next step would be to evaluate **[method]** under **[broader conditions]**.

예:

> A natural next step would be to evaluate the method across larger and more diverse datasets.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**limitation**은 연구 결과를 해석할 때 고려해야 하는 제약이나 한계입니다.

> One limitation of this study is the relatively small sample size.

논문에서는 아주 자주 다음처럼 씁니다.

> The study has several limitations.

**scope**는 연구 결과를 어디까지 적용할 수 있는지를 의미합니다.

> The conclusion should be interpreted within the scope of the experiment.

일반 영어에서도 유용합니다.

> That issue is outside the scope of this project.

**overstate**는 근거보다 강하게 주장한다는 뜻입니다.

> We should avoid overstating the significance of the result.

반대 방향으로는 `understate`가 있습니다.

> The report may understate the operational risk.

**cautiously**는 신중하게 해석하거나 표현한다는 뜻입니다.

> The result should be interpreted cautiously.

연구 영어에서는 매우 자주 사용됩니다.

> cautiously optimistic  
> interpret cautiously

**preliminary**는 초기 단계라 아직 확정적이지 않은 결과를 뜻합니다.

> These findings should be considered preliminary.

일반 업무에서도 사용 가능합니다.

> This is only a preliminary estimate.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> Our dataset is small, so our result is not meaningful.

더 정확하게:

> Our dataset is relatively small, so the result should be interpreted cautiously.

설명: 작은 데이터셋이라는 이유만으로 결과가 `not meaningful`이라고 단정하는 것은 너무 강합니다. 연구에서는 **결과 자체를 부정하기보다 해석 범위를 제한**하는 것이 좋습니다.

### 2

> This result can apply every domain.

정답:

> This result may not apply to every domain.

또는:

> This result may not generalize across all domains.

설명: `apply`를 사용할 때는 보통 `apply to`가 필요합니다.

- apply every domain ❌
- apply to every domain ✅

연구 문맥에서는 `generalize across`가 더 자연스럽습니다.

### 3

> We need to be careful for conclusion.

정답:

> We need to be careful when drawing conclusions.

설명:

- careful for conclusion ❌
- careful when drawing conclusions ✅

`draw a conclusion`은 꼭 익혀둘 표현입니다.

### 4

> The limitation doesn't mean the experiment is failed.

정답:

> The limitation does not mean that the experiment failed.

더 자연스럽게:

> The limitation does not invalidate the experiment.

설명: `is failed`는 어색합니다.

- experiment is failed ❌
- experiment failed ✅
- invalidate the experiment ✅

### 5

> In future, we will test more various data.

정답:

> In future work, we will evaluate the method on more diverse datasets.

설명:

- more various data △
- more diverse datasets ✅

논문에서는 `future work`가 매우 자연스럽고, `test`보다 `evaluate`가 더 전문적입니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The results are promising because …  
> However, one important limitation is …  
> This means that …  
> The limitation does not invalidate …  
> but it restricts …  
> Therefore, I would avoid claiming that …  
> A natural next step would be …

초급식 표현:

> The dataset is small, so we cannot trust the result.

더 정확한 표현:

> The relatively small dataset limits how confidently we can generalize the result.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although the findings are encouraging, the limited dataset size and narrow domain coverage constrain the external validity of the study, so broader claims should be made cautiously.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The results are encouraging within the current experimental setting, but I would avoid making broad claims about general performance. One important limitation is that the dataset is relatively small and comes from only one domain. This does not invalidate the observed improvement, but it limits how confidently we can generalize the result to other settings. I would therefore describe the findings as preliminary evidence that the method is effective under the tested conditions. A natural next step would be to evaluate the model on larger and more diverse datasets, ideally across multiple domains. If the same trend remains consistent under those conditions, we could make a stronger claim about the method’s broader usefulness.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“This limitation does not invalidate the result, but it restricts how broadly we can generalize it.” → “The findings should be interpreted within the scope of the experiment.” → “A natural next step would be to evaluate the method on more diverse datasets.”**
