# Day 11 — Responding to Criticism and Revising Your Position

오늘 목표는 **내 주장에 반론이 들어왔을 때 방어적으로 반응하지 않고, 타당한 부분을 인정하면서 내 입장을 정교하게 수정하는 것**입니다. C1 수준에서는 단순히 “I agree / I disagree”를 넘어서, **concession → clarification → revised position** 구조로 말할 수 있어야 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 답해보세요.

> You argued that a new model performs better than the baseline. A colleague points out that your experiment used only one dataset and two random seeds. How would you respond?

답변에는 **상대 지적 인정 → 내 기존 주장의 범위 수정 → 추가 검증 계획 → 현재 가능한 결론**을 넣으세요.

---

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: 타당한 반론 인정

> That’s a valid point. Our current experiment does not fully account for **[limitation]**.

예:

> That’s a valid point. Our current experiment does not fully account for variability across random seeds.

`You're right.`도 가능하지만, 기술 토론에서는 `That's a valid point.`가 더 자연스럽고 전문적입니다.

### Template 2: 기존 주장 범위 조정

> I would therefore qualify my earlier claim and say that **[more cautious conclusion]**.

예:

> I would therefore qualify my earlier claim and say that the method appears promising under the conditions we tested.

여기서 `qualify a claim`은 “주장을 자격화하다”가 아니라 **주장의 범위를 제한하거나 조건을 붙이다**라는 뜻입니다.

### Template 3: 다음 검증 단계 제안

> To strengthen the evidence, we should **[additional experiment]** before drawing a broader conclusion.

예:

> To strengthen the evidence, we should evaluate the method across multiple datasets and random seeds before drawing a broader conclusion.

---

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. valid point

**타당한 지적**

> You raise a valid point about the limited sample size.

회의에서 반론을 인정할 때 아주 유용합니다.

> That is a valid concern, but I think we can address it experimentally.

### 2. qualify a claim

**주장의 범위나 강도를 제한하다**

> We should qualify the claim because the experiment was conducted on only one benchmark.

논문에서도 자주 쓰이는 사고방식입니다.

강한 주장:

> The method is superior.

수정된 주장:

> The method appears to outperform the baseline under the tested conditions.

### 3. account for

**~을 고려하다 / ~을 설명하다**

> The analysis does not account for differences in hardware.

또는:

> Data quality may account for the observed performance gap.

첫 번째는 “고려하다”, 두 번째는 “설명하다”입니다.

### 4. robust evidence

**견고한 근거**

> Two experiments are not enough to provide robust evidence.

논문 표현:

> More robust evidence is required before the method can be recommended for production use.

`strong evidence`도 좋지만, `robust evidence`는 여러 조건에서도 흔들리지 않는 근거라는 뉘앙스가 있습니다.

### 5. revise

**수정하다, 기존 판단을 다시 조정하다**

> I would revise my original interpretation based on this additional evidence.

주의:

* revise an argument
* revise a conclusion
* revise a manuscript

`modify`보다 **검토 후 개선하거나 수정한다**는 의미가 강합니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1

> I agree about your point.

정답:

> I agree with your point.

또는 더 자연스럽게:

> I agree that this is an important limitation.

설명: `agree with + 사람/의견` 구조를 사용합니다.

* agree about your point ❌
* agree with your point ✅

---

### 2

> My experiment didn't consider about different random seeds.

정답:

> My experiment did not account for different random seeds.

또는:

> My experiment did not consider variability across random seeds.

설명: `consider`는 타동사이므로 `about`을 쓰지 않습니다.

* consider about something ❌
* consider something ✅

기술적으로는 단순히 여러 seed가 존재한다기보다 **seed에 따른 변동성**이 핵심이므로 `variability across random seeds`가 더 정확합니다.

---

### 3

> I have to change my opinion more carefully.

더 자연스럽게:

> I need to revise my conclusion.

또는:

> I need to make my conclusion more cautious.

설명: 영어에서 `change my opinion carefully`는 의미가 모호합니다. 연구 결과를 다시 해석한다면 `revise my conclusion`이 정확합니다.

---

### 4

> Two seeds are too less to conclude it.

정답:

> Two random seeds are too few to support a strong conclusion.

설명:

가산명사에는 `few`를 사용합니다.

* too less seeds ❌
* too few seeds ✅

그리고 학술 영어에서는 `conclude it`보다:

> support a conclusion

이 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 5

> We should do more experiments for prove our result.

정답:

> We should conduct more experiments to validate our result.

설명:

목적은 `to + 동사원형`으로 표현합니다.

* for prove ❌
* to prove ✅

하지만 연구에서는 `prove`가 지나치게 강할 수 있으므로 `validate`가 더 적절합니다.

> prove the method works
> 보다
> validate the finding

이 안전하고 자연스럽습니다.

---

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름을 그대로 활용하세요.

> That’s a valid point.
> Our current experiment does not fully account for …
> Given this limitation, I would qualify my earlier claim.
> At this stage, the results suggest that …
> However, the evidence is not yet robust enough to conclude that …
> To strengthen the evidence, we should …
> Based on those results, I would then revise the conclusion if necessary.

오늘은 특히 **내 의견을 바꾸는 것이 약해 보인다고 생각하지 않는 것**이 중요합니다. 영어 기술 토론에서는 오히려 근거에 따라 주장의 범위를 조절하는 능력이 훨씬 전문적으로 들립니다.

강한 표현:

> My conclusion is correct.

더 좋은 표현:

> The current evidence supports this interpretation.

더 정교한 표현:

> The current evidence supports this interpretation, although the conclusion remains provisional.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> That’s a valid point. Our current experiment does not fully account for variability across datasets or random seeds. Because we evaluated the method on only one dataset with two seeds, I would qualify my earlier claim. At this stage, the results suggest that the new model may outperform the baseline under the conditions we tested, but the evidence is not yet robust enough to conclude that the improvement is consistent. To strengthen the evidence, I would run the experiment across more random seeds and evaluate the method on several datasets. I would also report the mean performance and variance rather than relying on a single best result. Based on those additional experiments, I would revise the conclusion if necessary.

오늘의 핵심 표현은 세 개만 확실히 기억하세요: **“That’s a valid point.” → “I would qualify my earlier claim.” → “We need more robust evidence.”** 이 세 문장만 자연스럽게 써도 기술 토론에서 훨씬 성숙한 영어가 됩니다.
