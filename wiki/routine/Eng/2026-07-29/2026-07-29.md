# Day 3 — Comparing Two Approaches

오늘 목표는 **두 가지 방법을 비교하고, 하나를 선택한 이유를 60–90초 동안 설명하는 것**입니다. 기술 면접, 회의, 논문 요약에 자주 쓰이는 구조입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 영어로 답해보세요.

> Compare two tools, methods, or learning strategies you have used. Which one do you prefer, and why?

예시 주제:

* PyTorch vs. TensorFlow
* local GPU vs. cloud GPU
* reading papers vs. watching lectures
* studying alone vs. studying in a group

답변에는 다음 내용을 넣으세요.

1. 두 대상의 공통점
2. 가장 중요한 차이
3. 각각의 장단점
4. 내가 선호하는 것과 이유

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 공통점과 차이점

> Both **[A]** and **[B]** are useful for **[purpose]**, but they differ in terms of **[criterion]**.

예:

> Both local and cloud GPUs are useful for model training, but they differ in terms of cost and flexibility.

### Template 2: 장단점 비교

> While **[A]** is better suited for **[situation]**, **[B]** offers an advantage when **[situation]**.

예:

> While local hardware is better suited for repeated experiments, cloud infrastructure offers an advantage when rapid scaling is required.

### Template 3: 선택과 근거

> Overall, I prefer **[A]** because it allows me to **[benefit]**, even though **[limitation]**.

예:

> Overall, I prefer local hardware because it allows me to control the environment, even though maintenance can be time-consuming.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. in terms of

**~의 측면에서**

> The two models differ significantly in terms of inference speed.

자연스러운 사용:

* in terms of cost
* in terms of accuracy
* in terms of usability

---

### 2. be better suited for

**~에 더 적합하다**

> This method is better suited for small datasets.

주의:

* suited to도 가능
* suited for는 용도나 상황을 말할 때 자주 사용

---

### 3. trade-off

**상충 관계, 한쪽을 얻으면 다른 쪽을 일부 포기해야 하는 관계**

> There is a trade-off between accuracy and computational cost.

논문에서 매우 자주 등장하는 표현입니다.

---

### 4. outperform

**성능이 더 우수하다**

> The proposed model outperformed the baseline on three benchmark datasets.

주의:

> The model was more outperforming. ❌
> The model outperformed the baseline. ✅

---

### 5. comparable

**비슷한 수준의, 비교할 만한**

> The smaller model achieved comparable performance with fewer parameters.

논문식 표현:

> The results were comparable to those reported in previous studies.

`comparable`은 일반적으로 `to`와 함께 사용합니다.

---

## 4. 교정 연습 — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1

> A is more faster than B.

정답:

> A is faster than B.

설명:

`faster` 자체가 비교급이므로 `more`를 함께 쓰지 않습니다.

* more faster ❌
* faster ✅

---

### 2

> Both methods have each advantage.

자연스러운 표현:

> Both methods have their own advantages.

또는:

> Each method has its own advantages.

설명:

* `both`와 함께 쓰면 `their`
* `each`와 함께 쓰면 단수 동사 `has`

---

### 3

> This model has a better performance.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> This model performs better.

또는:

> This model achieves better performance.

설명:

영어에서는 `have a better performance`보다 `perform better` 또는 `achieve better performance`가 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 4

> The accuracy of A is similar with B.

정답:

> The accuracy of A is similar to that of B.

설명:

* similar with ❌
* similar to ✅

또한 정확도를 비교하는 것이므로 `B` 자체가 아니라 `B의 정확도`와 비교해야 합니다.

---

### 5

> I prefer A than B.

정답:

> I prefer A to B.

또는:

> I prefer A over B.

설명:

`prefer`는 보통 다음 구조를 사용합니다.

* prefer A to B
* prefer A over B
* prefer doing A to doing B

---

## 5. 60–90초 말하기 — 2분

다음 순서로 소리 내어 답하세요.

> Both A and B are useful for …
> However, they differ in terms of …
> A is better suited for …
> In contrast, B is more effective when …
> There is a trade-off between … and …
> Overall, I prefer … because …

이번에는 `good`, `bad`, `better`만 반복하지 말고 다음 표현을 써보세요.

* more efficient
* more flexible
* easier to maintain
* less expensive
* better suited for
* comparable to

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> Both local GPUs and cloud GPUs are useful for machine learning experiments, but they differ in terms of cost, scalability, and control. Local GPUs are better suited for repeated experiments because they provide a stable environment and do not require continuous usage fees. In contrast, cloud GPUs are more flexible when additional computing power is needed immediately. There is a clear trade-off between control and scalability. Cloud systems may offer better scalability, while local systems are often easier to customize and maintain over time. Overall, I prefer local hardware for long-term experimentation because it allows me to manage the environment directly, even though upgrading the hardware can be expensive.

## 오늘의 핵심 교정

> A is more faster than B. ❌
> A is faster than B. ✅

> I prefer A than B. ❌
> I prefer A to B. ✅

> A is similar with B. ❌
> A is similar to B. ✅
