# Day 49 — Explaining Statistical Significance vs. Practical Significance

오늘 목표는 **통계적으로 유의한 차이가 있다고 해서 그 차이가 실제로 중요한 것은 아니라는 점**을 영어로 설명하는 것입니다. 오늘은 **measured difference → statistical significance → effect size → practical impact → balanced conclusion** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 답해보세요.

> A new model improves accuracy from 91.2% to 91.6%, and the difference is statistically significant. Would you consider the new model meaningfully better?

답변에는 **statistical significance와 practical significance의 차이, effect size, 추가 계산 비용, 실제 배포 가치**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The improvement is statistically significant, but I would not automatically assume that it is practically meaningful.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **통계적 유의성과 실제 중요성을 구분할 때** 사용합니다.

> **A statistically significant difference does not necessarily imply a practically meaningful improvement.**

예:

> A statistically significant difference does not necessarily imply a practically meaningful improvement in deployment.

두 번째는 **효과 크기와 비용을 함께 비교할 때** 유용합니다.

> Whether the improvement is worthwhile depends on **[effect size]** relative to **[cost / complexity / latency]**.

예:

> Whether the improvement is worthwhile depends on the effect size relative to the additional inference cost.

세 번째는 **균형 잡힌 결론을 만들 때** 씁니다.

> I would consider both **[statistical evidence]** and **[practical impact]** before concluding that the new method is superior.

예:

> I would consider both statistical evidence and operational impact before concluding that the new model is superior.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**statistically significant**는 관찰된 차이가 단순한 우연으로 발생했을 가능성이 낮다고 판단되는 상태입니다.

> The improvement was statistically significant.

다만 이것이 곧 “큰 개선”을 의미하지는 않습니다.

**practically meaningful**은 실제 상황에서 체감할 만큼 중요한 차이라는 뜻입니다.

> The gain is statistically significant but may not be practically meaningful.

일반 업무에서도 쓸 수 있습니다.

> The difference is measurable, but not practically meaningful for users.

**effect size**는 두 조건 사이의 차이가 실제로 얼마나 큰지를 나타냅니다.

> The p-value is small, but the effect size is also very small.

연구에서는 p-value와 함께 해석하는 경우가 많습니다.

**trade-off**는 한 가지 장점을 얻기 위해 다른 것을 희생해야 하는 관계를 뜻합니다.

> We need to consider the trade-off between accuracy and latency.

일반 영어에서도 매우 자주 사용됩니다.

> There is always a trade-off between speed and quality.

**worthwhile**은 비용이나 노력을 들일 가치가 있다는 뜻입니다.

> The improvement may not be worthwhile if inference becomes twice as expensive.

말하기에서도 매우 유용합니다.

> It is useful, but I am not sure it is worthwhile.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The improvement is significant, so it is very important.

더 정확하게:

> The improvement is statistically significant, but that does not necessarily mean it is practically important.

설명: 연구에서 `significant`는 일상적인 “중요한”과 통계적인 “유의한”이라는 두 의미가 있습니다. 혼동을 피하려면 `statistically significant`라고 명확히 말하는 것이 좋습니다.

### 2

> Accuracy increased only 0.4 percentage.

정답:

> Accuracy increased by only 0.4 percentage points.

설명:

91.2% → 91.6%는 **0.4% 증가**라기보다 **0.4 percentage points 증가**입니다.

- increased 0.4 percentage ❌
- increased by 0.4 percentage points ✅

### 3

> We should think the computation cost too.

정답:

> We should also consider the computational cost.

설명:

- think the cost ❌
- consider the cost ✅

그리고 형용사 형태는 보통 `computational cost`를 사용합니다.

### 4

> The new model needs two times more GPU.

더 자연스럽게:

> The new model requires roughly twice as much GPU compute.

또는:

> The new model requires about twice the computational resources.

설명: `two times more`는 의미가 모호할 수 있어 `twice as much`가 더 자연스럽습니다.

### 5

> Small difference can be statistically significant when data is very big.

정답:

> A small difference can be statistically significant when the dataset is very large.

설명:

- `difference` 앞에 관사 필요
- 데이터셋의 크기는 보통 `large`, 사람이나 물체의 물리적 크기는 `big`도 자연스럽습니다.

더 연구답게 말하면:

> With a sufficiently large sample, even a very small effect can become statistically significant.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The new model improves accuracy from … to …  
> The difference is statistically significant, but …  
> The absolute improvement is only …  
> I would therefore examine …  
> I would also compare …  
> If the additional cost is …  
> Based on both statistical and practical considerations, I would …

초급식 표현:

> The p-value is small, so the new model is much better.

더 정확한 표현:

> The small p-value suggests that the difference is unlikely to be due to random variation, but it does not tell us whether the improvement is large enough to matter.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although the improvement is statistically significant, its practical value depends on the magnitude of the gain and whether that gain justifies the additional computational and operational cost.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I would not automatically consider the new model meaningfully better just because the result is statistically significant. The accuracy improves from 91.2% to 91.6%, which is an absolute gain of only 0.4 percentage points. With a sufficiently large evaluation set, even a small difference can become statistically significant. I would therefore examine the effect size and compare the improvement with the additional computational cost, latency, and deployment complexity. If the new model requires substantially more resources for only a marginal gain, the improvement may not be worthwhile. On the other hand, in a high-risk application where even a small reduction in errors has important consequences, the same improvement could be valuable. I would therefore evaluate both statistical evidence and practical impact before deciding whether the new model is genuinely superior.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“Statistical significance does not necessarily imply practical significance.” → “The effect size should be considered alongside the p-value.” → “The improvement must be evaluated relative to its computational and operational cost.”**
