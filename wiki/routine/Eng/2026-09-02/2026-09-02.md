# Day 28 — Evaluating Whether a “State-of-the-Art” Claim Is Really Meaningful

오늘 목표는 **논문이나 발표에서 “state-of-the-art”라는 표현을 봤을 때, 단순히 최고 점수라고 받아들이지 않고 비교 조건과 실제 개선폭을 검토하는 것**입니다. 오늘은 **claim → baseline → comparability → effect size → conclusion** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A paper claims state-of-the-art performance because its model scores 0.8 points higher than the previous best result. However, it uses a larger model and more training data. How would you evaluate this claim?

답변에는 다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. 단순 점수 차이가 무엇을 보여주는지  
2. 비교 조건이 동일한지  
3. 추가 자원 사용이 결과에 미친 영향  
4. 0.8-point improvement가 실제로 중요한지  
5. 어떤 추가 비교가 필요한지  

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The reported score is higher, but I would be cautious about calling it a clear state-of-the-art improvement without examining the experimental conditions.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: claim과 evidence 분리하기

> The model achieves a higher score than **[baseline]**, but the comparison may not be entirely fair because **[difference]**.

예:

> The model achieves a higher score than the previous baseline, but the comparison may not be entirely fair because it uses substantially more training data.

이 구조는 논문 리뷰에서 매우 유용합니다.

### Template 2: 개선폭의 의미 평가하기

> The improvement is **[small/modest/substantial]**, so we need to determine whether it is **[statistically/practically] meaningful**.

예:

> The improvement is relatively modest, so we need to determine whether it is practically meaningful.

### Template 3: 더 공정한 비교 제안하기

> A more informative comparison would control for **[resource/setting]** and evaluate **[metric]** under the same conditions.

예:

> A more informative comparison would control for model size and training data and evaluate performance under the same computational budget.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. state-of-the-art
**현재 알려진 최고 수준의**

> The model achieves state-of-the-art performance on the benchmark.

주의할 점은 **숫자가 가장 높다고 해서 무조건 의미 있는 SOTA는 아니라는 것**입니다.

> The result is technically state-of-the-art, but the margin is very small.

### 2. comparable
**비교 가능한**

> The two experiments are not directly comparable because they use different training budgets.

자주 쓰는 표현:

> directly comparable  
> broadly comparable  
> not comparable under the current setup

### 3. computational budget
**계산 자원 예산**

> The new method uses a significantly larger computational budget.

논문에서 매우 자주 나오는 개념입니다.

> A fair comparison should account for the computational budget.

### 4. margin
**격차, 차이**

> The performance margin over the baseline is only 0.8 points.

일반 영어에서도 자연스럽습니다.

> We won by a narrow margin.

연구 영어에서는 `narrow margin`, `small margin`, `large margin`을 자주 씁니다.

### 5. incremental
**점진적인, 소폭의**

> The improvement appears incremental rather than substantial.

논문 리뷰에서 유용합니다.

> The method provides an incremental gain at a considerable computational cost.

즉, **성능 향상은 작지만 비용은 클 수 있다**는 뜻입니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> This model is the most state-of-the-art model.

정답:

> This model achieves state-of-the-art performance.

설명: `state-of-the-art` 자체가 이미 최고 수준이라는 의미이므로 `most`를 붙이지 않습니다.

- most state-of-the-art ❌
- state-of-the-art ✅

---

### 2

> The score is higher only 0.8 point.

정답:

> The score is only 0.8 points higher.

또는:

> The model outperforms the baseline by only 0.8 points.

설명: 비교 차이는 다음 구조가 자연스럽습니다.

> X is 0.8 points higher than Y.

또는:

> X outperforms Y by 0.8 points.

---

### 3

> The two models cannot compare directly.

정답:

> The two models cannot be compared directly.

설명: 여기서는 모델들이 스스로 비교하는 것이 아니라 **누군가에 의해 비교되는 대상**이므로 수동태가 필요합니다.

- cannot compare ❌
- cannot be compared ✅

---

### 4

> The new model used much more training data than previous model.

정답:

> The new model used much more training data than the previous model.

설명: 특정한 이전 모델을 가리키므로 `the previous model`이라고 해야 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 5

> We should check if 0.8 improvement has real meaning.

더 자연스럽게:

> We should determine whether the 0.8-point improvement is practically meaningful.

설명:

- `if`도 가능하지만 학술적 문맥에서는 `whether`가 더 자연스럽습니다.
- `has real meaning`보다 `is practically meaningful`이 정확합니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 끊지 않고 말해보세요.

> The paper reports a higher score than …  
> However, the comparison may not be directly comparable because …  
> The performance margin is only …  
> We also need to consider …  
> The improvement may be incremental rather than …  
> A fairer comparison would …  
> Therefore, I would describe the result as …

오늘은 특히 **“점수가 높다”와 “방법 자체가 더 낫다”를 분리해서 말하는 것**에 집중하세요.

초급식 표현:

> The score is higher, so the model is better.

더 정확한 표현:

> The model achieves a higher score, but it also uses more training data and compute.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although the model reports the highest benchmark score, the relatively narrow performance margin and substantially larger training budget make it difficult to attribute the gain to the method itself.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The paper reports a score that is 0.8 points higher than the previous best result, so technically it may qualify as state-of-the-art on that benchmark. However, I would be cautious about interpreting the result as evidence that the method itself is clearly superior. The new model uses more training data and a larger computational budget, so the two systems may not be directly comparable. The performance margin is also relatively small, which raises the question of whether the improvement is practically meaningful. A more informative comparison would control for model size, training data, and computational budget. If the method still outperforms the baseline under comparable conditions, then the claim would be much stronger. At this stage, I would describe the result as promising but incremental rather than definitively superior.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“The comparison may not be directly comparable.” → “The performance margin is relatively small.” → “A fairer comparison would control for the computational budget.”**
