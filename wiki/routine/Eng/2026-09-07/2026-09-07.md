# Day 31 — Reconciling Conflicting Results Across Two Papers

오늘 목표는 **서로 다른 논문이 같은 주제에 대해 다른 결론을 내렸을 때, 어느 한쪽을 바로 틀렸다고 하지 않고 차이가 발생한 이유를 구조적으로 설명하는 것**입니다. 오늘은 **conflicting findings → methodological differences → possible explanation → stronger conclusion** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> Paper A reports that increasing model size substantially improves reasoning performance. Paper B finds only a small improvement. How would you explain why the two studies may have reached different conclusions?

답변에는 **결과가 어떻게 다른지, 실험 조건에서 무엇이 달랐을 수 있는지, 어느 결과를 더 신뢰할지 판단하려면 무엇을 확인해야 하는지, 현재 어떤 결론이 가장 합리적인지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The two studies appear to reach different conclusions, but the discrepancy may be explained by differences in their experimental setups.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **결과 차이를 중립적으로 표현**할 때 사용합니다.

> The findings differ in terms of **[aspect]**, which may be partly explained by **[difference]**.

예:

> The findings differ in terms of the magnitude of the improvement, which may be partly explained by differences in dataset difficulty.

두 번째는 **조건 차이에 따라 결과가 달라질 수 있음을 설명**할 때 좋습니다.

> One possibility is that the effect depends on **[condition]**, rather than being consistent across all settings.

예:

> One possibility is that the effect depends on model scale and task difficulty, rather than being consistent across all settings.

세 번째는 **두 논문의 결론을 통합**할 때 사용합니다.

> Taken together, the studies suggest that **[balanced conclusion]**, although **[remaining uncertainty]**.

예:

> Taken together, the studies suggest that scaling can improve reasoning performance, although the magnitude of the benefit may depend on the evaluation setting.

이 `Taken together`는 논문 Discussion이나 Related Work에서 매우 자주 쓰입니다.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. discrepancy
**불일치, 차이**

> There is a noticeable discrepancy between the results of the two studies.

`difference`보다 조금 더 학술적입니다.

> We need to explain the discrepancy between the predicted and observed values.

### 2. methodological difference
**방법론적 차이**

> The conflicting results may stem from methodological differences.

예:

> Differences in preprocessing, sample size, or evaluation criteria could affect the outcome.

### 3. stem from
**~에서 비롯되다**

> The performance gap may stem from differences in training data.

`come from`보다 학술적으로 자연스럽습니다.

일반 영어에서도 가능합니다.

> The misunderstanding stemmed from unclear communication.

### 4. reconcile
**서로 모순되어 보이는 결과를 조화롭게 설명하다**

> It is difficult to reconcile the two findings without additional information.

논문에서 매우 유용합니다.

> One way to reconcile these results is to assume that the effect depends on dataset size.

### 5. context-dependent
**조건이나 맥락에 따라 달라지는**

> The effect may be highly context-dependent.

예:

> The benefits of a larger model appear to be context-dependent rather than universal.

연구 결과를 과도하게 일반화하지 않을 때 강력한 표현입니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The two papers have different result.

정답:

> The two papers report different results.

설명: `result`는 여기서 복수형이 필요하고, 논문이 결과를 “가지고 있다”기보다 **report**한다고 표현하는 것이 자연스럽습니다.

- have different result ❌
- report different results ✅

---

### 2

> Maybe the reason is because they used different datasets.

더 자연스럽게:

> One possible reason is that they used different datasets.

설명: `the reason is because`는 중복된 느낌이 있습니다.

- the reason is because △
- the reason is that ✅
- one possible reason is that ✅

---

### 3

> Paper A's dataset was more easy.

정답:

> Paper A's dataset was easier.

설명: `easy`의 비교급은 `easier`입니다.

- more easy ❌
- easier ✅

연구 영어에서는 더 정확하게:

> Paper A may have used an easier benchmark.

라고 할 수 있습니다.

---

### 4

> We need to compare their condition more carefully.

정답:

> We need to compare their experimental conditions more carefully.

설명: 여러 조건을 말하므로 `conditions`가 자연스럽습니다. `experimental conditions`라고 하면 무엇을 비교하는지 훨씬 명확합니다.

---

### 5

> We cannot say which paper is correct only by result.

더 자연스럽게:

> We cannot determine which conclusion is more reliable based on the reported results alone.

설명: 연구에서는 `correct / incorrect`보다 **more reliable**, **better supported** 같은 표현이 더 적절한 경우가 많습니다.

또한:

> based on X alone

은 매우 유용한 패턴입니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> Paper A reports that …  
> In contrast, Paper B finds that …  
> The discrepancy may stem from …  
> Another possibility is that …  
> This would suggest that the effect is context-dependent rather than …  
> To reconcile the findings, I would compare …  
> Taken together, the studies suggest that …

오늘은 특히 **서로 다른 연구 결과를 경쟁 관계로만 보지 않고 조건부로 통합하는 것**에 집중하세요.

초급식 표현:

> Paper A says yes, but Paper B says no.

더 정확한 표현:

> Paper A reports a strong effect, whereas Paper B finds only a modest improvement.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> The apparent contradiction may reflect differences in experimental design rather than a genuine disagreement about the underlying phenomenon.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> Paper A reports a substantial improvement in reasoning performance as model size increases, whereas Paper B finds only a modest gain. I would not immediately conclude that one of the studies is wrong. The discrepancy may stem from methodological differences, such as dataset difficulty, model architecture, training budget, or evaluation metrics. Another possibility is that the effect of model scale is context-dependent and becomes more pronounced only under certain conditions. To reconcile the findings, I would compare the experimental setups carefully and examine whether the same trend appears when the studies are evaluated under comparable conditions. Taken together, the papers may suggest that larger models can improve reasoning performance, but the magnitude of the benefit is likely to depend on the task and evaluation setting.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“The discrepancy may stem from …” → “The effect may be context-dependent.” → “Taken together, the studies suggest that …”**
