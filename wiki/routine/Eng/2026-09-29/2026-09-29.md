# Day 47 — Explaining Correlation vs. Causation

오늘 목표는 **두 현상이 함께 나타난다고 해서 하나가 다른 하나의 원인이라고 바로 결론 내리면 안 된다는 점**을 영어로 설명하는 것입니다. 오늘은 **observed relationship → correlation → confounding factor → causal claim → stronger evidence** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A study finds that teams using AI coding assistants finish tasks faster than teams that do not use them. Can we conclude that the AI assistant caused the productivity improvement?

답변에는 **왜 상관관계만으로는 충분하지 않은지, 다른 요인이 개입했을 가능성, 어떤 실험이 필요할지, 현재 어느 정도까지 말할 수 있는지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The result shows an association between AI-assistant use and faster task completion, but it does not necessarily establish causation.

---

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **상관관계와 인과관계를 구분할 때** 사용합니다.

> **[A] is associated with [B], but this does not necessarily mean that [A] causes [B].**

예:

> AI-assistant use is associated with higher productivity, but this does not necessarily mean that it causes the improvement.

두 번째는 **다른 요인이 있을 수 있다고 말할 때** 유용합니다.

> The observed relationship may be influenced by **[a third factor / confounding variable]**.

예:

> The observed relationship may be influenced by differences in developer experience.

세 번째는 **더 강한 인과 근거를 제안할 때** 씁니다.

> To make a stronger causal claim, we would need **[a controlled experiment / random assignment / better controls]**.

예:

> To make a stronger causal claim, we would need a controlled experiment with random assignment.

---

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**correlation**은 두 변수가 함께 변하는 관계를 뜻합니다.

> There is a positive correlation between model size and training cost.

주의할 점은:

> Correlation does not imply causation.

이 문장은 연구 영어에서 매우 자주 나옵니다.

**causation**은 한 요인이 다른 결과를 직접적으로 만들어내는 인과관계를 뜻합니다.

> The study shows correlation, but it does not establish causation.

**confounding factor**는 두 변수 사이의 관계를 왜곡할 수 있는 제3의 요인입니다.

> Developer experience may be a confounding factor.

예를 들어 숙련된 개발자들이 AI 도구도 더 적극적으로 쓴다면, 실제 원인은 AI 도구가 아니라 경험 수준일 수 있습니다.

**control for**는 특정 변수의 영향을 통제한다는 뜻입니다.

> The analysis controls for age, experience, and team size.

논문에서 매우 자주 쓰입니다.

> control for confounding variables  
> control for baseline differences

**attribute A to B**는 A의 원인을 B라고 돌린다는 뜻입니다.

> We cannot attribute the entire performance gain to the new method.

이 표현은 Discussion 섹션에서 특히 유용합니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> Because they use AI, their productivity became higher.

더 신중하게:

> Their productivity was higher among teams using AI, but we cannot conclude that AI caused the difference.

설명: 원래 문장은 인과관계를 이미 확정해버립니다. 연구 영어에서는 근거 수준에 맞춰 표현 강도를 조절해야 합니다.

---

### 2

> There has correlation between AI use and productivity.

정답:

> There is a correlation between AI use and productivity.

설명:

- there has correlation ❌
- there is a correlation ✅

`correlation`은 셀 수 있는 명사로 자주 씁니다.

---

### 3

> Maybe another factor affected to the result.

정답:

> Another factor may have affected the result.

설명:

`affect`는 타동사이므로 `to`가 필요 없습니다.

- affect to the result ❌
- affect the result ✅

그리고 연구 영어에서는 `maybe`보다 `may`가 더 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 4

> We should control other variables for know the real effect.

정답:

> We should control for other variables to estimate the true effect.

설명:

- control other variables △
- control for other variables ✅
- for know ❌
- to know / to estimate ✅

연구 문맥에서는 `estimate the true effect`가 더 정확합니다.

---

### 5

> This study proves AI increases productivity.

더 신중하게:

> This study suggests that AI use may be associated with higher productivity.

또는 인과 근거가 충분한 경우:

> This study provides evidence that AI use increases productivity.

설명: `prove`는 연구 글쓰기에서 매우 강한 표현입니다. 대부분의 경우 `suggests`, `provides evidence`, `is associated with`가 더 적절합니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The study finds that …  
> However, this only shows …  
> It does not necessarily mean that …  
> One possible confounding factor is …  
> Another explanation could be …  
> To make a stronger causal claim, I would …  
> Based on the current evidence, I would say …

초급식 표현:

> AI made the teams faster.

더 정확한 표현:

> Teams using AI completed tasks faster, but the result does not by itself establish causation.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although AI-assistant use is associated with faster task completion, the observational result does not allow us to rule out confounding factors or alternative explanations.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I would not immediately conclude that the AI assistant caused the productivity improvement. The study shows an association between AI use and faster task completion, but correlation alone does not establish causation. Other factors may have influenced the result. For example, more experienced developers may have been more likely to use the AI assistant, or the teams may have differed in task difficulty. To make a stronger causal claim, I would want to see a controlled experiment in which similar participants are randomly assigned to use or not use the tool. I would also control for factors such as experience, team size, and task complexity. Based on the current evidence, I would say that AI use is associated with higher productivity, but the causal effect is not yet clear.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“Correlation does not necessarily imply causation.” → “The observed relationship may be influenced by confounding factors.” → “We need stronger evidence before making a causal claim.”**
