# Day 46 — Explaining Why Average Performance Can Hide Important Failures

오늘 목표는 **전체 평균 성능이 좋아 보여도 특정 집단이나 특정 조건에서는 성능이 크게 떨어질 수 있다는 점**을 설명하는 것입니다. 오늘은 **overall average → subgroup performance → hidden failure → risk → more informative evaluation** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A model achieves 92% overall accuracy, but its accuracy drops to 68% for one important subgroup. Would you still consider the model reliable?

답변에는 **왜 전체 평균만으로 충분하지 않은지, subgroup performance가 왜 중요한지, 어떤 위험이 있는지, 어떻게 평가를 개선해야 하는지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The overall accuracy looks strong, but the average may hide important performance differences across subgroups.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **평균이 문제를 가릴 수 있다고 말할 때** 사용합니다.

> The overall average may hide **[important differences / systematic failures]** across **[groups/conditions]**.

예:

> The overall average may hide systematic failures across different user groups.

두 번째는 **특정 집단에서 성능이 낮다고 설명할 때** 유용합니다.

> Performance is substantially lower for **[group]**, which suggests that **[problem]** may not be evenly distributed.

예:

> Performance is substantially lower for one subgroup, which suggests that model errors may not be evenly distributed.

세 번째는 **더 나은 평가를 제안할 때** 씁니다.

> I would report both **[overall metric]** and **[subgroup-level metrics]** before concluding that the model is reliable.

예:

> I would report both overall accuracy and subgroup-level accuracy before concluding that the model is reliable.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**subgroup**는 전체 데이터 안의 특정 하위 집단을 뜻합니다.

> The model performs well overall but poorly on one subgroup.

연구에서는 `subgroup analysis`, `subgroup performance`가 자주 나옵니다.

**aggregate**는 여러 결과를 합쳐 하나의 전체 값으로 본다는 뜻입니다.

> Aggregate performance can hide important variation.

예:

> The aggregate score is high, but individual categories show very different results.

**disparity**는 집단 간 성능이나 결과의 차이가 크다는 뜻입니다.

> The evaluation revealed a substantial performance disparity between groups.

일반 영어에서도 사용합니다.

> There is a large income disparity between the two regions.

**systematic**은 우연히 한두 번 발생하는 것이 아니라 일정한 패턴으로 반복된다는 뜻입니다.

> The error appears to be systematic rather than random.

논문에서는 매우 자주 쓰입니다.

> systematic bias  
> systematic error  
> systematic difference

**mask**는 어떤 문제나 차이를 가린다는 뜻입니다.

> A high average score can mask serious weaknesses in specific cases.

일반 영어에서도 쓸 수 있습니다.

> Strong sales figures masked deeper financial problems.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The average accuracy is high, so model is reliable.

정답:

> The average accuracy is high, but that does not necessarily mean the model is reliable.

설명: `so`는 너무 빠르게 결론으로 넘어갑니다. 연구 영어에서는 `does not necessarily mean`을 사용해 해석을 조심스럽게 만드는 것이 좋습니다.

### 2

> One group has very low performance than others.

정답:

> One group has much lower performance than the others.

설명:

- very low performance than ❌
- much lower performance than ✅

비교급을 강조할 때는 `very`가 아니라 `much`를 사용합니다.

### 3

> Average can hide some problem.

더 자연스럽게:

> The average can hide important problems.

더 전문적으로:

> The aggregate metric can mask important subgroup failures.

설명: 특정 평균값을 말하므로 `the average`가 자연스럽습니다. `mask`는 연구 영어에서 “문제를 가린다”는 의미로 매우 유용합니다.

### 4

> We should check each groups separately.

정답:

> We should check each group separately.

또는:

> We should evaluate each subgroup separately.

설명: `each` 뒤에는 단수명사가 옵니다.

- each groups ❌
- each group ✅

### 5

> This difference maybe caused from data imbalance.

정답:

> This difference may be caused by data imbalance.

더 자연스럽게:

> This disparity may partly result from data imbalance.

설명:

- maybe caused ❌
- may be caused ✅
- caused from ❌
- caused by ✅

`maybe`는 부사이고, `may be`는 조동사 + 동사 구조입니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The overall accuracy is high, but …  
> The subgroup results show that …  
> This suggests that …  
> The aggregate metric may therefore …  
> I would want to examine …  
> I would also report …  
> Based on the current evidence, I would …

초급식 표현:

> The model is good because average accuracy is 92%.

더 정확한 표현:

> The model performs well overall, but its reliability is questionable because performance drops sharply for one subgroup.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although the aggregate accuracy is strong, the substantial subgroup disparity indicates that the model’s errors are not uniformly distributed, which raises concerns about its reliability in deployment.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I would not consider the model fully reliable based on the overall accuracy alone. A 92% average looks strong, but the 68% accuracy for one subgroup indicates a substantial performance disparity. This suggests that the aggregate metric is masking an important weakness. I would therefore evaluate the model separately across relevant subgroups and examine whether the failure is systematic. I would also investigate possible causes such as data imbalance, insufficient representation, or domain differences. Before deployment, I would want both strong overall performance and reasonably consistent performance across important groups. Based on the current evidence, the model appears effective on average, but its reliability is uneven.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“The overall average may hide important subgroup differences.” → “The error appears to be systematic rather than random.” → “Aggregate performance alone is not sufficient to establish reliability.”**
