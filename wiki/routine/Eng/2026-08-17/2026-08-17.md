# Day 16 — Explaining Robustness, Not Just Average Performance

오늘 목표는 **평균 성능이 좋아 보인다고 해서 바로 “좋은 모델”이라고 결론 내리지 않고, 변동성·최악의 경우·환경 변화까지 고려해 robustness를 설명하는 것**입니다. 오늘부터는 단순한 결과 설명보다 한 단계 더 나아가 **average result → variability → robustness → decision** 구조로 말해봅니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> Model A has slightly higher average accuracy than Model B, but its performance varies much more across random seeds and datasets. Which model would you prefer for production, and why?

답변에는 평균 성능, 결과의 변동성, 실제 운영에서의 위험, 그리고 어떤 추가 정보를 확인할지를 포함하세요.

말문이 막히면 이렇게 시작하면 됩니다.

> Although Model A performs slightly better on average, I would not make the decision based on the mean performance alone.

---

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **평균과 안정성을 분리해서 말하는 구조**입니다.

> Although **[A]** performs better on average, **[B]** appears to be more consistent across **[conditions]**.

예:

> Although Model A performs better on average, Model B appears to be more consistent across datasets and random seeds.

두 번째는 **production 관점의 판단**입니다.

> For a production system, I would place more weight on **[factor]** than on **[factor]**.

예:

> For a production system, I would place more weight on consistent performance than on a small improvement in average accuracy.

세 번째는 **결론을 내리기 전에 필요한 검증**을 말할 때 사용합니다.

> Before making a final decision, I would examine **[metric/evidence]** to determine whether **[question]**.

예:

> Before making a final decision, I would examine the variance across runs to determine whether the performance gain is reliable.

---

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. robust

**여러 조건이 바뀌어도 안정적인, 견고한**

> The method appears robust across different datasets.

논문에서 `good`보다 훨씬 구체적인 표현입니다.

> We need to determine whether the improvement is robust to changes in hyperparameters.

자주 쓰는 조합은 `robust to noise`, `robust across datasets`, `robust under distribution shift`입니다.

### 2. variability

**변동성**

> There is considerable variability across random seeds.

`variation`도 가능하지만, 성능이 얼마나 흔들리는지를 이야기할 때 `variability`가 매우 자연스럽습니다.

> High variability makes the result difficult to reproduce.

### 3. consistent

**일관된, 안정적으로 반복되는**

> The model produced consistent results across five independent runs.

주의할 점은 `consistent`가 반드시 “높은 성능”이라는 뜻은 아니라는 것입니다.

> The model was consistently mediocre.

처럼 낮은 성능이 일관될 수도 있습니다.

### 4. sensitive to

**~의 변화에 민감한**

> The method appears to be highly sensitive to the learning rate.

논문에서 매우 자주 등장합니다.

> Performance was sensitive to the choice of random seed.

반대쪽 개념은:

> relatively insensitive to hyperparameter changes

입니다.

### 5. reliability

**신뢰성, 반복해서 비슷한 결과를 낼 수 있는 정도**

> Reliability may matter more than peak performance in production.

논문·실무를 연결하기 좋은 표현입니다.

> We need more runs to assess the reliability of the reported improvement.

`accuracy`와 `reliability`를 구분해서 말할 수 있으면 표현력이 크게 올라갑니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 영어 문장을 직접 고쳐본 후 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> Model A has more higher average accuracy.

정답:

> Model A has higher average accuracy.

설명: `higher` 자체가 이미 비교급이기 때문에 `more`를 붙이지 않습니다.

* more higher ❌
* higher ✅
* significantly higher ✅

강조하고 싶다면 `much higher`, `slightly higher`, `significantly higher`를 사용하세요.

---

### 2

> The result changes a lot depending of the random seed.

정답:

> The result varies considerably depending on the random seed.

설명: `depend`는 `on`과 결합합니다.

* depending of ❌
* depending on ✅

그리고 기술 영어에서는 `changes a lot`보다 `varies considerably`가 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 3

> Model B is more stable even its accuracy is lower.

정답:

> Model B is more stable even though its accuracy is lower.

설명: `even`만으로 두 절을 연결할 수 없습니다.

* even + 명사/비교 표현
* even though + 완전한 문장

예:

> Even with lower accuracy, Model B may be preferable.

이 구조도 매우 유용합니다.

---

### 4

> We should see the variance before decide which model is better.

정답:

> We should examine the variance before deciding which model is better.

더 전문적으로:

> We should examine the variance before determining which model is more reliable.

설명: 전치사 `before` 뒤에 행동을 넣을 때는 `-ing`를 사용합니다.

* before decide ❌
* before deciding ✅

또한 데이터나 통계량을 분석할 때는 `see`보다 `examine`, `analyze`, `look at`이 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 5

> The model is very sensitive for hyperparameters.

정답:

> The model is very sensitive to hyperparameters.

설명: 반드시 기억할 조합입니다.

> sensitive to something

예:

> The result is sensitive to initialization.

> Performance is highly sensitive to the learning rate.

---

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

아래 흐름으로 끊지 않고 말해보세요.

> Although Model A performs slightly better on average, …
> The main concern is that …
> Its performance appears to be sensitive to …
> In contrast, Model B …
> For production, I would place more weight on …
> Before making a final decision, I would examine …
> If Model B remains more consistent across …, I would …

오늘은 특히 다음 세 단계의 표현 차이를 의식하세요.

단순한 표현:

> Model B is safer.

더 정확한 표현:

> Model B produces more consistent results.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Model B may be preferable in production because its lower variability provides greater confidence in its expected behavior.

핵심은 어려운 단어 자체가 아니라 **왜 안정성이 중요한지를 한 문장 더 설명하는 것**입니다.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> Although Model A has slightly higher average accuracy, I would not choose it based on the mean performance alone. Its high variability suggests that the method may be sensitive to random seeds, datasets, or other experimental conditions. Model B, on the other hand, appears to provide more consistent results. For a production system, I would generally place more weight on reliability and predictable behavior than on a marginal improvement in average accuracy. Before making a final decision, I would examine the mean, standard deviation, worst-case performance, and results across multiple datasets. If Model B continues to show lower variability while maintaining competitive accuracy, I would probably prefer it for deployment because its performance appears to be more robust.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다:

> **Although it performs better on average, …**

> **The result appears to be sensitive to …**

> **I would place more weight on reliability than on peak performance.**

특히 마지막 문장은 연구 발표뿐 아니라 **기술 면접에서 “왜 최고 성능 모델을 선택하지 않았나요?”라는 질문에 바로 활용할 수 있는 문장**입니다.
