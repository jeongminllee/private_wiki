# Day 21 — Explaining a Negative Result Without Calling It a Failure

오늘 목표는 **“유의미한 차이가 없었다”는 결과를 실패로 취급하지 않고, 그 결과가 무엇을 의미하고 무엇을 의미하지 않는지 설명하는 것**입니다. 연구 발표, 논문 리뷰, 실험 회고에서 매우 중요한 능력입니다. 오늘은 **result → interpretation → limitation → next step** 구조로 말해봅니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> You tested a new training method, but its performance was not significantly different from the baseline. How would you explain this result to your team?

답변에는 다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. 무엇을 비교했는가
2. 어떤 결과가 나왔는가
3. “효과가 없다”고 단정할 수 있는지
4. 다음에 무엇을 확인해야 하는지

시작 문장으로는 다음이 좋습니다.

> The experiment did not show a statistically significant improvement over the baseline.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **차이가 없었다는 결과를 정확하게 말할 때** 사용합니다.

> We did not observe a significant difference between **[A]** and **[B]**.

예:

> We did not observe a significant difference between the new method and the baseline.

두 번째는 **“효과가 없다고 증명했다”는 과도한 결론을 피할 때** 씁니다.

> This does not necessarily mean that **[method]** has no effect; it may mean that **[alternative explanation]**.

예:

> This does not necessarily mean that the method has no effect; it may mean that the effect is too small to detect with the current experiment.

세 번째는 **다음 검증을 제안할 때** 사용합니다.

> Before drawing a firm conclusion, I would examine **[factor]** and consider **[next step]**.

예:

> Before drawing a firm conclusion, I would examine the variance across runs and consider increasing the sample size.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. null result

**유의미한 효과나 차이를 발견하지 못한 결과**

> The experiment produced a null result.

중요한 점은 `null result`가 **실험 실패**라는 뜻은 아니라는 것입니다.

> A null result can still provide useful information about the hypothesis.

### 2. fail to detect

**~을 발견하지 못하다**

> The experiment failed to detect a meaningful improvement.

여기서 `fail`은 연구 자체가 실패했다는 뜻이 아니라 **통계적으로 효과를 검출하지 못했다**는 의미입니다.

> We failed to detect a significant difference between the groups.

### 3. statistical power

**통계적 검정력**

> The experiment may have had insufficient statistical power to detect a small effect.

검정력이 낮으면 실제 효과가 존재하더라도 이를 발견하지 못할 수 있습니다.

> Increasing the sample size can improve statistical power.

### 4. inconclusive

**결론을 내리기 어려운**

> The current evidence is inconclusive.

이 표현은 아주 유용합니다.

> The results are inconclusive, so additional experiments are needed.

`wrong`, `failed`, `useless`보다 훨씬 정확합니다.

### 5. rule out

**가능성을 배제하다**

> The current experiment does not rule out a small performance improvement.

이 문장은 논문에서 매우 중요한 사고방식을 담고 있습니다.

> Failure to find an effect does not necessarily rule out the possibility that an effect exists.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> There was no significant difference, so the new method doesn't work.

더 적절한 표현:

> There was no significant difference, but we cannot conclude that the new method has no effect.

설명: **차이를 발견하지 못했다**와 **효과가 없다는 것을 증명했다**는 서로 다릅니다.

* We found no significant effect. ✅
* We proved that there is no effect. ❌

---

### 2

> The experiment couldn't find the improvement.

더 자연스럽게:

> The experiment did not detect a significant improvement.

설명: 연구 결과에서는 `find`도 가능하지만 `detect`가 훨씬 전문적입니다.

* find an improvement △
* detect an improvement ✅

---

### 3

> Maybe the number of data was too small.

정답:

> The amount of data may have been too small.

또는 더 정확하게:

> The sample size may have been too small.

설명:

`data`를 단순히 개수로 말하기보다 실험 참가자나 관측치 수를 의미한다면 `sample size`가 가장 정확합니다.

또한 과거 상황을 추측하므로:

> may have been

을 사용합니다.

---

### 4

> We need more experiments for checking if the result is true.

정답:

> We need more experiments to determine whether the result is reliable.

설명:

목적은 `to + 동사원형`으로 표현합니다.

* for checking ❌
* to determine ✅

또한 연구 결과에 대해서는 `true`보다 `reliable`, `robust`, `replicable` 같은 표현이 더 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 5

> The result is not meaningful.

더 정확한 표현:

> The result was not statistically significant.

또는:

> The evidence is currently inconclusive.

설명: `not meaningful`은 너무 모호합니다.

실제로는 통계적으로 유의하지 않아도 실무적으로 중요한 패턴이 있을 수 있습니다. 반대로 통계적으로 유의해도 효과가 매우 작을 수 있습니다.

---

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 소리 내어 말해보세요.

> We compared …
> We did not observe a statistically significant difference between …
> However, this does not necessarily mean that …
> One possibility is that …
> Another possibility is that the experiment lacked sufficient …
> Before drawing a firm conclusion, I would examine …
> At this stage, I would describe the evidence as …

오늘은 특히 다음 표현의 차이를 의식하세요.

너무 강한 표현:

> The method does not work.

더 정확한 표현:

> We did not detect a significant improvement.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> The current experiment provides insufficient evidence to conclude that the method improves performance, but it also does not rule out a small effect.

이 마지막 문장은 **“증거 부족”과 “효과 없음”을 정확하게 구분하는 표현**입니다.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> We compared the new training method with the baseline under the same experimental conditions, but we did not observe a statistically significant improvement. However, I would not conclude that the method has no effect. One possibility is that the true improvement is relatively small, and the experiment did not have enough statistical power to detect it. Another possibility is that performance varies substantially across random seeds, which may have obscured the effect. Before drawing a firm conclusion, I would examine the variance across runs, increase the number of seeds, and possibly evaluate the method on additional datasets. At this stage, I would describe the evidence as inconclusive rather than saying that the method failed.

오늘은 이 세 문장을 입에 붙이세요: **“We did not observe a significant difference.” → “This does not necessarily mean that there is no effect.” → “The current evidence is inconclusive.”**
