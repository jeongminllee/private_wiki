# Day 14 — Explaining a Failed Experiment and What You Learned

오늘 목표는 **실패한 실험을 단순히 “안 됐다”라고 말하지 않고, 원인 → 증거 → 수정 → 학습으로 구조화해서 설명하는 것**입니다. 연구 미팅뿐 아니라 기술 면접의 *“Tell me about a failure”* 질문에도 그대로 활용할 수 있습니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 답해보세요.

> Describe an experiment or technical approach that did not work as expected. What went wrong, how did you identify the problem, and what did you change afterward?

답변은 다음 순서로 구성하세요.

1. 원래 목표
2. 예상과 실제 결과의 차이
3. 원인 조사
4. 수정한 내용
5. 그 경험에서 배운 점

처음에는 아래 한 문장으로 시작해도 좋습니다.

> I once tried an approach that looked promising initially, but the results did not match my expectations.

---

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: 예상과 실제 결과 비교

> We expected **[expected result]**, but instead we observed **[actual result]**.

예:

> We expected the new augmentation strategy to improve generalization, but instead we observed unstable validation performance.

### Template 2: 원인 분석

> After investigating the issue, we found that **[cause]** was likely contributing to **[problem]**.

예:

> After investigating the issue, we found that inconsistent preprocessing was likely contributing to the performance drop.

### Template 3: 배운 점 정리

> The main lesson I took from this was that **[lesson]**, so I now **[changed behavior]**.

예:

> The main lesson I took from this was that experimental controls matter as much as model complexity, so I now verify the pipeline before changing the model.

---

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. fall short of expectations

**기대에 미치지 못하다**

> The model’s performance fell short of our expectations.

일상 영어에서도 사용할 수 있습니다.

> The presentation fell short of what I had hoped for.

논문이나 기술 발표에서는 `failed`보다 덜 극단적이고 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 2. diagnose

**문제의 원인을 진단하다**

> We spent several hours diagnosing the training instability.

기술 문맥에서는 매우 자주 사용합니다.

> The logs helped us diagnose the underlying issue.

`find the problem`보다 전문적입니다.

---

### 3. bottleneck

**병목, 전체 성능을 제한하는 요소**

> Data loading turned out to be the main bottleneck.

또는:

> GPU memory became a bottleneck as the batch size increased.

`bottleneck`은 시스템뿐 아니라 프로세스에도 쓸 수 있습니다.

> Communication was the main bottleneck in the project.

---

### 4. overlook

**간과하다, 놓치다**

> We initially overlooked a mismatch in the preprocessing pipeline.

면접에서도 매우 유용합니다.

> Looking back, I had overlooked an important assumption.

단순히 `I didn't see it`보다 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 5. iterate on

**반복적으로 개선하다**

> We iterated on the training pipeline rather than replacing the entire system.

개발·연구 영어에서 자주 사용됩니다.

> We tested the prototype, collected feedback, and iterated on the design.

`repeat`와 차이가 있습니다.

* repeat = 같은 것을 반복하다
* iterate on = 반복하면서 개선하다

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1

> The experiment was failed.

정답:

> The experiment failed.

또는:

> The experiment did not produce the expected results.

설명:

`fail`은 여기서 자동사입니다.

* The experiment failed. ✅
* The experiment was failed. ❌

하지만 연구에서는 두 번째 표현이 더 정확한 경우가 많습니다. 실험 자체가 완전히 실패한 것이 아니라 **예상 결과를 얻지 못했을 뿐**일 수 있기 때문입니다.

---

### 2

> I didn't know why the problem happened at first.

더 자연스럽게:

> At first, I wasn't sure what was causing the problem.

또는:

> Initially, the root cause was unclear.

설명:

기술 문제에서는 `why the problem happened`보다 **what was causing the problem**이 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 3

> We found the reason by checking one by one.

더 자연스럽게:

> We identified the cause by checking each component systematically.

또는:

> We isolated the issue by testing one component at a time.

설명:

한국어의 “하나씩 확인했다”를 `one by one`으로 직역해도 문법적으로 틀리지는 않지만, 실험·디버깅에서는 `systematically`, `one component at a time`이 훨씬 정확합니다.

---

### 4

> I learned that I have to check more carefully before experiment.

정답:

> I learned that I need to check the setup more carefully before running an experiment.

설명:

`before experiment`처럼 명사를 바로 쓰기보다:

* before an experiment
* before running an experiment

을 사용하세요.

또한 `have to`보다 `need to`가 개인적인 교훈을 말할 때 더 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 5

> Because of this experience, now I always check the data first.

더 자연스럽게:

> Because of this experience, I now check the data first.

또는 더 고급스럽게:

> Since then, I have made it a habit to verify the data before modifying the model.

설명:

영어에서는 `now` 위치가 중요합니다.

* now I always check △
* I now check ✅

특히 **Since then, I have made it a habit to ...**는 면접에서 매우 강력한 표현입니다.

---

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

아래 흐름을 따라 소리 내어 말해보세요.

> I was trying to …
> We expected …, but instead we observed …
> At first, the root cause was unclear.
> After investigating the issue, we found that …
> We had initially overlooked …
> To address the problem, I …
> The main lesson I took from this was that …
> Since then, I have made it a habit to …

오늘은 특히 **실패를 결과가 아니라 과정으로 설명하는 것**에 집중하세요.

초급식 표현:

> It failed, so I tried again.

더 좋은 표현:

> The initial approach fell short of expectations, so I investigated the failure mode before modifying the system.

더 자연스럽고 면접에 적합한 표현:

> Rather than immediately changing the model, I first tried to isolate the source of the problem.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I once tested a new training configuration that I expected to improve model performance. However, the validation results fell short of my expectations and varied considerably across runs. At first, I assumed the model architecture was the problem, but after investigating the pipeline, I found that inconsistent preprocessing was likely contributing to the instability. I had initially overlooked a difference between the training and validation transformations. After correcting that issue, the results became much more stable. The main lesson I took from this experience was that it is important to diagnose the pipeline systematically before modifying the model itself. Since then, I have made it a habit to verify the data, preprocessing, and evaluation settings first whenever an experiment behaves unexpectedly.

오늘은 이 세 문장을 입에 붙이는 것이 핵심입니다: **“The results fell short of my expectations.” → “I tried to isolate the source of the problem.” → “Since then, I have made it a habit to …”**
