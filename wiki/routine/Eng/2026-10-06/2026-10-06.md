# Day 52 — Explaining Ablation Studies and What Actually Causes an Improvement

오늘 목표는 **모델 성능이 좋아졌을 때 “전체 시스템이 좋아졌다”에서 끝내지 않고, 어떤 구성 요소가 실제 개선에 기여했는지 설명하는 것**입니다. 연구 발표와 논문 Discussion에서 매우 자주 쓰이는 **overall improvement → ablation → contribution → interaction → conclusion** 구조를 연습합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> You add three changes to a model at the same time: a new attention mechanism, a different normalization method, and a larger training dataset. Performance improves by 5%. Can you say that the new attention mechanism caused the improvement?

답변에는 **왜 전체 결과만으로 특정 구성 요소의 효과를 알 수 없는지, ablation study를 어떻게 설계할지, 각 요소를 하나씩 제거하거나 추가하는 이유, 구성 요소 사이의 interaction 가능성**을 포함하세요.

시작 문장:

> The overall improvement is promising, but we cannot attribute it to the new attention mechanism without isolating the effect of each change.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

**원인을 분리할 때**

> To isolate the effect of **[component]**, I would keep **[other conditions]** fixed and change only **[variable]**.

예:

> To isolate the effect of the attention mechanism, I would keep the dataset and normalization method fixed and change only the attention module.

**기여도를 설명할 때**

> The ablation results suggest that **[component]** contributes **[substantially / modestly]** to the overall improvement.

예:

> The ablation results suggest that the new attention mechanism contributes substantially to the overall improvement.

**여러 요소가 함께 작용할 가능성을 말할 때**

> The gain may result from an interaction between **[A]** and **[B]**, rather than from either component alone.

예:

> The gain may result from an interaction between the new attention mechanism and the larger dataset.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**ablation study**: 시스템의 구성 요소를 제거하거나 변경하면서 각 요소가 성능에 얼마나 기여하는지 확인하는 실험입니다.

> The paper includes an ablation study to identify which components are responsible for the performance gain.

**isolate**: 다른 요인을 통제하고 특정 요인의 효과만 분리해 보는 것입니다.

> We need to isolate the effect of the new architecture.

일상 영어에서도 유용합니다.

> It is difficult to isolate the exact cause of the problem.

**contribution**: 어떤 요소가 전체 결과에 기여한 정도입니다.

> The largest contribution came from the additional training data.

논문에서 자주 쓰는 표현은 `individual contribution`, `relative contribution`, `main contributor`입니다.

**account for**: 어떤 결과의 원인이나 설명이 되다는 뜻입니다.

> The larger dataset may account for most of the observed improvement.

일반 영어에서도:

> Transportation accounts for a large part of the total cost.

**interaction effect**: 두 개 이상의 요소가 독립적으로 작용하는 것이 아니라 서로 영향을 주면서 결과를 만드는 현상입니다.

> There may be an interaction effect between model size and training data.

이 표현은 통계·ML 연구 모두에서 매우 유용합니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1
> We changed three things at same time.

정답:

> We changed three things at the same time.

설명: 고정 표현은 `at the same time`입니다. `the`를 빼면 어색합니다.

### 2
> We cannot know which component made the performance better.

더 자연스럽게:

> We cannot determine which component caused the performance improvement.

설명: `made the performance better`도 이해는 되지만 연구 영어에서는 `caused the improvement`, `contributed to the improvement`가 더 정확합니다.

### 3
> We should remove each components one by one.

정답:

> We should remove each component one by one.

설명: `each` 뒤에는 단수 명사가 옵니다.

`each components` ❌  
`each component` ✅

### 4
> The attention mechanism gave 3% improvement.

더 정확하게:

> The attention mechanism resulted in a 3-percentage-point improvement.

또는:

> Adding the attention mechanism improved accuracy by 3 percentage points.

설명: 수치 차이를 말할 때 `gave improvement`보다 `resulted in`, `improved ... by`가 더 자연스럽습니다.

### 5
> Maybe two components affect together.

정답:

> The two components may interact with each other.

또는 더 연구답게:

> There may be an interaction between the two components.

설명: 영어에서는 `affect together`라고 하지 않습니다. 서로 영향을 주는 경우 `interact`를 씁니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

이 흐름으로 말해보세요:

> The full model improves performance by …  
> However, three changes were introduced simultaneously.  
> Therefore, we cannot determine whether …  
> I would first …  
> Then I would …  
> The ablation results would show …  
> I would also examine whether …  
> Based on those results, …

초급식 표현:

> We changed many things, so we don't know what helped.

더 정확한 표현:

> Because several components were changed simultaneously, we cannot identify which one contributed most to the improvement.

C1 수준으로 올리면:

> Since multiple modifications were introduced simultaneously, the aggregate improvement cannot be attributed to any single component without a controlled ablation analysis.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I would not attribute the entire 5% improvement to the new attention mechanism because several changes were introduced at the same time. To identify the contribution of each component, I would conduct an ablation study. I would keep the training conditions fixed and evaluate the model while adding or removing one component at a time. This would help isolate the effect of the attention mechanism, the normalization method, and the larger dataset. I would also test combinations of components because the improvement may result from interaction effects rather than from a single modification. If the attention mechanism consistently improves performance while the other conditions remain unchanged, we would have much stronger evidence that it is responsible for part of the observed gain.

오늘 입에 붙일 핵심 세 문장은 **“We need to isolate the effect of each component.” → “The ablation results show which component contributes to the improvement.” → “The gain may result from an interaction between multiple components.”**
