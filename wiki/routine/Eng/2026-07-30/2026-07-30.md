# Day 4 — Summarizing a Research Finding with Appropriate Caution

오늘 목표는 **연구 결과를 요약하면서 과장하지 않고, 근거와 한계를 함께 설명하는 것**입니다. 논문 발표, 기술 면접, 연구 미팅에서 매우 중요한 능력입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> Describe a research result or technical finding you found interesting. What did the study show, and what limitations should we consider?

아래 구조를 따르세요.

1. 연구 주제
2. 핵심 결과
3. 결과가 의미하는 것
4. 결과를 해석할 때 주의할 점

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 연구 목적과 결과

> The study examined **[topic]** and found that **[main result]**.

예:

> The study examined the effect of model size on reasoning performance and found that larger models generally performed better.

### Template 2: 해석

> This suggests that **[interpretation]**, although it does not necessarily mean that **[overstated conclusion]**.

예:

> This suggests that scale can improve reasoning ability, although it does not necessarily mean that larger models are always more efficient.

### Template 3: 한계 제시

> One limitation is that **[limitation]**, so the results should be interpreted with caution.

예:

> One limitation is that the experiments were conducted on a limited set of benchmarks, so the results should be interpreted with caution.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. indicate

**~을 나타내다, 시사하다**

> The results indicate that the new method improves accuracy.

`show`보다 논문에서 조금 더 격식 있는 표현입니다.

주의:

> The results indicate us that … ❌
> The results indicate that … ✅

---

### 2. suggest

**~일 가능성을 시사하다**

> The findings suggest that data quality may be more important than data quantity.

`suggest`는 확정적인 사실보다 **가능성이나 해석**을 말할 때 적합합니다.

---

### 3. be consistent with

**~와 일치하다**

> These findings are consistent with previous studies.

논문에서 기존 연구와 결과를 비교할 때 매우 자주 사용됩니다.

---

### 4. generalize to

**~에 일반화되다**

> It is unclear whether the results generalize to real-world environments.

머신러닝 문맥:

> The model performed well on the test set but did not generalize to unseen data.

---

### 5. interpret with caution

**신중하게 해석하다**

> The results should be interpreted with caution because the sample size was small.

연구 결과를 과장하지 않는 핵심 표현입니다.

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고친 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The result means that this method is always better.

더 적절한 표현:

> The result suggests that this method may be more effective under certain conditions.

설명:

논문 결과 하나만으로 `always better`라고 단정하면 과도한 일반화가 됩니다.

* means that: 강한 단정
* suggests that: 가능성 제시
* may be: 불확실성 표현

---

### 2

> According to the experiment, the model was improved.

정답:

> According to the experiment, the model’s performance improved.

또는:

> The experiment showed that the model performed better.

설명:

`the model was improved`는 누군가 모델 자체를 수정했다는 의미로 들릴 수 있습니다. 성능 향상을 말할 때는 `performance improved`가 정확합니다.

---

### 3

> This result is same with previous research.

정답:

> This result is consistent with previous research.

또는:

> This result is similar to the findings of previous studies.

설명:

* same with ❌
* the same as ✅
* similar to ✅
* consistent with ✅

---

### 4

> The model cannot generalize for other datasets.

정답:

> The model may not generalize to other datasets.

설명:

* generalize for ❌
* generalize to ✅

또한 실험 결과만으로 완전히 불가능하다고 단정하기 어렵기 때문에 `may not`이 더 적절합니다.

---

### 5

> We should be careful to interpret this result.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> We should interpret this result carefully.

또는:

> This result should be interpreted with caution.

설명:

`be careful to interpret`는 “빠뜨리지 말고 해석하다”처럼 들릴 수 있습니다. 연구 결과를 신중하게 해석한다는 뜻에는 위 표현들이 더 자연스럽습니다.

---

## 5. 60–90초 Speaking Practice — 2분

아래 흐름으로 소리 내어 말하세요.

> The study examined …
> The main finding was that …
> This suggests that …
> The result is consistent with …
> However, one limitation is that …
> Therefore, the findings should be interpreted with caution.

오늘은 다음 표현을 의식적으로 사용하세요.

* suggests that
* may
* appears to
* is likely to
* does not necessarily mean

이 표현들은 약한 표현이 아니라, **근거 수준을 정확하게 조절하는 고급 영어 표현**입니다.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I recently read a study comparing small and large language models on reasoning tasks. The study found that larger models generally achieved higher accuracy, especially on complex problems. This suggests that model scale may contribute to stronger reasoning performance. However, it does not necessarily mean that larger models are always the best choice. They require more computational resources and may not be practical in every environment. One limitation is that the study relied heavily on benchmark datasets, so it is unclear whether the findings generalize to real-world applications. Overall, the results are promising, but they should be interpreted with caution.

## 오늘의 핵심 교정

> The result means that it is always better. ❌
> The result suggests that it may be better under certain conditions. ✅

> It is same with previous research. ❌
> It is consistent with previous research. ✅

> The model generalizes for other datasets. ❌
> The model generalizes to other datasets. ✅
