# Day 36 — Statistical Significance vs. Practical Significance

오늘 목표는 **“통계적으로 유의하다”와 “실제로 중요한 차이다”를 구분해서 설명하는 것**입니다. 연구 결과를 말할 때 단순히 `p < 0.05`만 보고 강한 결론을 내리지 않고, **effect size → uncertainty → practical impact**까지 함께 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A new model improves accuracy from 90.0% to 90.4%, and the difference is statistically significant. Would you consider the improvement important?

답변에는 **통계적 유의성만으로 충분한지, 성능 차이의 크기, 추가 계산 비용, 실제 배포에서의 가치**를 포함해보세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The result may be statistically significant, but that does not necessarily mean the improvement is practically meaningful.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: 통계적 유의성과 실제 중요성 구분

> The difference is statistically significant, but the practical impact appears **[small/substantial/unclear]**.

예:

> The difference is statistically significant, but the practical impact appears relatively small.

### Template 2: 비용까지 포함해 평가

> Whether the improvement is worthwhile depends on **[cost/resource/operational consequence]**.

예:

> Whether the improvement is worthwhile depends on the additional inference cost and latency.

### Template 3: 더 신중한 결론

> I would consider both **[effect size]** and **[uncertainty/cost]** before concluding that **[claim]**.

예:

> I would consider both the effect size and computational cost before concluding that the new model is meaningfully better.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**statistically significant**는 관찰된 차이가 단순한 우연만으로 발생했을 가능성이 낮다는 의미입니다.

> The difference was statistically significant across multiple runs.

중요한 점은 이것이 곧 **큰 차이**를 의미하지는 않는다는 것입니다.

**practically meaningful**은 실제 업무나 시스템 관점에서도 충분히 중요한 차이라는 뜻입니다.

> The improvement is statistically significant but may not be practically meaningful.

**effect size**는 차이가 존재하는지만 보는 것이 아니라 **그 차이가 얼마나 큰지**를 나타냅니다.

> We should examine the effect size rather than focusing only on the p-value.

**negligible**은 너무 작아서 사실상 중요하지 않다는 뜻입니다.

> The performance gain may be negligible in a production environment.

일반 영어에서도 사용합니다.

> The difference in cost was negligible.

**justify**는 비용이나 결정을 정당화할 만큼 충분한 이유가 있다는 뜻입니다.

> A 0.4-point improvement may not justify doubling the inference cost.

일반 업무 영어에서도 매우 유용합니다.

> The expected benefit does not justify the additional expense.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The result has statistical significance.

더 자연스럽게:

> The result is statistically significant.

설명: 연구 영어에서는 보통 `be statistically significant` 형태를 사용합니다.

- has statistical significance △
- is statistically significant ✅

### 2

> The accuracy increased only 0.4%.

정확하게 차이를 말하려면:

> The accuracy increased by 0.4 percentage points.

설명: 90.0%에서 90.4%로 상승한 것은 엄밀히 말하면 **0.4 percentage points**입니다.

`percent`와 `percentage points`는 다릅니다.

> from 90% to 91% = 1 percentage point

### 3

> This improvement is too small for meaningful.

정답:

> This improvement may be too small to be meaningful.

설명:

- too small for meaningful ❌
- too small to be meaningful ✅

`too + adjective + to + verb` 구조를 기억하세요.

### 4

> We should consider how much cost increases.

더 자연스럽게:

> We should consider how much the cost increases.

또는 기술적으로:

> We should consider the additional computational cost.

설명: 간접의문문에서도 주어가 필요합니다.

- how much cost increases △
- how much the cost increases ✅

### 5

> The new model is statistically better, but maybe not actually better.

더 정확하게:

> The new model performs significantly better statistically, but the practical benefit may be limited.

또는 더 자연스럽게:

> The improvement is statistically significant, but its practical value may be limited.

설명: `statistically better`는 의미는 전달되지만 연구 영어에서는 보통 **difference/improvement is statistically significant**라고 표현합니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 말해보세요.

> The new model improves accuracy from … to …  
> The difference is statistically significant, but …  
> The effect size appears …  
> We also need to consider …  
> If the improvement requires substantially more …  
> the additional gain may not justify …  
> Therefore, I would conclude that …

초급식 표현:

> The p-value is small, so the new model is much better.

더 정확한 표현:

> The result is statistically significant, but the improvement itself is relatively small.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although the performance difference is statistically significant, its small effect size raises the question of whether the gain is meaningful enough to justify the additional computational cost.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The improvement from 90.0% to 90.4% may be statistically significant, but I would not automatically consider it practically important. Statistical significance tells us that the observed difference is unlikely to be explained by random variation alone, but it does not tell us whether the effect is large enough to matter in practice. I would also examine the effect size, confidence intervals, inference latency, memory usage, and computational cost. If the new model requires substantially more resources for only a 0.4-percentage-point gain, the benefit may not justify the additional cost. On the other hand, in a high-stakes application, even a small improvement could be valuable. Therefore, I would evaluate statistical significance together with practical impact rather than relying on the p-value alone.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“Statistical significance does not necessarily imply practical significance.” → “We should examine the effect size, not just the p-value.” → “The improvement may not justify the additional computational cost.”**
