

# Day 65 — Explaining Why an Experiment Failed
### 실패한 실험을 영어로 분석하고 방어하기
**15-Minute English Practice | TOEIC 500–600 → CEFR C1+**

오늘은 기술 면접과 논문 Discussion에서 아주 중요한 상황을 연습합니다.

> **“The experiment didn't work. Why?”**

초급 답변은:

> The experiment failed because the performance was bad.

C1 수준에서는 **관찰한 사실 → 가능한 원인 → 근거 → 불확실성 → 다음 실험**으로 설명합니다.

### 오늘의 구조

**Observation → Hypothesis → Evidence → Uncertainty → Next Step**

---

## 1. 🎤 Speaking Prompt — 2분

상황:

> You added an optimization to an LLM serving system.
>
> You expected throughput to increase by 15%.
>
> Instead, throughput decreased by 5%.
>
> You suspect that the optimization introduced additional synchronization and communication overhead.

면접관이 묻습니다.

> **“Why do you think the optimization made performance worse?”**

### 60–90초 답변

다음 네 가지를 포함하세요.

1. 예상과 실제 결과
2. 가능한 원인
3. 어떻게 그 원인을 확인할 것인지
4. 아직 확실하지 않다는 점

**시작 문장**

> The result was contrary to our initial expectation. Instead of improving throughput, the optimization reduced it by approximately 5%.

오늘은 특히 **`because` 하나로 원인을 단정하지 않는 것**이 중요합니다.

---

# 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1 — 예상과 다른 결과

> **Contrary to our expectation, A resulted in B.**

> Contrary to our expectation, increasing the batch size resulted in lower throughput.

`contrary to our expectation`

= 우리의 예상과 달리

논문에서도 매우 유용합니다.

---

### Template 2 — 가능한 원인 제시

> **One possible explanation is that A introduced B.**

> One possible explanation is that the optimization introduced additional synchronization overhead.

더 신중하게:

> **This may be because A introduced B.**

하지만 연구에서는 첫 번째 표현이 더 명확합니다.

---

### Template 3 — 가설 검증

> **To test this hypothesis, I would measure A and compare it with B.**

> To test this hypothesis, I would measure synchronization overhead and compare it with the baseline.

C1 확장:

> To test this hypothesis, I would isolate the additional overhead and compare its contribution to total execution time against the baseline.

---

# 3. Five Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### ① Contrary to — ~와 반대로

> Contrary to our expectations, latency increased after the optimization.

예상과 달리 최적화 이후 latency가 증가했습니다.

**패턴**

> contrary to expectations  
> contrary to our hypothesis  
> contrary to previous findings

---

### ② Introduce — 새롭게 발생시키다 / 도입하다

> The optimization introduced additional communication overhead.

최적화가 추가적인 통신 오버헤드를 발생시켰습니다.

기술 영어에서 매우 중요합니다.

> introduce overhead  
> introduce latency  
> introduce a bottleneck  
> introduce additional complexity

---

### ③ Bottleneck — 병목

> Communication became a bottleneck at higher GPU counts.

GPU 수가 많아지면서 통신이 병목이 되었습니다.

일반 영어:

> The approval process became a bottleneck for the project.

---

### ④ Account for — ~을 설명하다 / ~을 차지하다

> Communication overhead accounts for a significant portion of the total execution time.

통신 오버헤드가 전체 실행 시간의 상당 부분을 차지합니다.

오늘은 두 번째 의미가 특히 중요합니다.

> X accounts for 30% of Y.

= X가 Y의 30%를 차지한다.

---

### ⑤ Attribute A to B — A의 원인을 B로 돌리다

> We attribute the performance degradation to additional synchronization overhead.

성능 저하를 추가적인 동기화 오버헤드 때문이라고 판단합니다.

하지만 **충분한 증거가 없다면**:

> We tentatively attribute the degradation to ...

처럼 말하는 것이 더 안전합니다.

---

# 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

## ①

❌ The result was opposite than we expected.

✅ **The result was contrary to what we expected.**

또는:

> **The result was the opposite of what we expected.**

### 왜?

`opposite`는 일반적으로:

> the opposite **of**

를 사용합니다.

반면 `contrary`는:

> contrary **to**

입니다.

---

## ②

❌ The optimization caused the performance decreased.

✅ **The optimization caused performance to decrease.**

또는 더 신중하게:

> **The optimization may have caused performance to decrease.**

### 핵심 문법

`cause + 목적어 + to + 동사`

> cause performance to decrease  
> cause latency to increase

---

## ③

❌ We need to check why it was happened.

✅ **We need to determine why it happened.**

### 왜?

`happen`은 자동사이므로 수동태로 만들지 않습니다.

- it happened ✅
- it was happened ❌

그리고 연구에서는 `check why`보다:

> determine why

가 더 정확하고 전문적입니다.

---

## ④

❌ The communication overhead is reason of the degradation.

✅ **Communication overhead is a possible cause of the degradation.**

또는:

> **We suspect that communication overhead contributed to the degradation.**

### 왜?

`reason of` ❌

다음 표현을 익혀두세요.

> a cause **of**  
> a reason **for**  
> contribute **to**

---

## ⑤

❌ We need more data for prove our hypothesis.

✅ **We need more data to test our hypothesis.**

또는:

> **We need additional evidence to support our hypothesis.**

### 중요한 차이

연구에서는 항상 `prove`를 목표로 삼는 것이 적절하지 않습니다.

> test a hypothesis  
> support a hypothesis  
> provide evidence for a hypothesis

가 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.

---

# 5. Progressive Speaking Challenge — 2분

## Level 1 — Basic

> We expected throughput to increase, but it decreased by 5%. I think communication overhead may have caused the problem. We need more tests.

좋습니다.

하지만 원인과 검증 방법이 아직 부족합니다.

---

## Level 2 — Intermediate

> Contrary to our expectation, throughput decreased by 5% after applying the optimization. One possible explanation is that the optimization introduced additional synchronization and communication overhead. I would run additional experiments to determine whether this overhead accounts for the performance degradation.

이제 **결과 → 가설 → 검증** 구조가 생겼습니다.

---

## Level 3 — C1

> Contrary to our initial hypothesis, the optimization resulted in a 5% reduction in throughput. One plausible explanation is that the additional computation introduced by the optimization was outweighed by synchronization and communication overhead. However, I would not attribute the degradation to communication alone without further evidence. To test this hypothesis, I would profile the system, quantify the additional overhead, and compare its contribution to total execution time against the baseline.

### C1 포인트

**was outweighed by**

→ A보다 B의 영향이 더 컸다는 고급 표현

**I would not attribute ... without further evidence**

→ 과도한 인과관계를 피함

**quantify the additional overhead**

→ 단순히 "확인한다"가 아니라 수치화한다는 전문적인 표현

---

# 6. 🎤 60-Second Interview Challenge

면접관이 추가로 묻습니다.

> **“How would you verify your hypothesis?”**

다음 구조를 사용해보세요.

> First, I would ...

> Then, I would ...

> This would allow me to ...

가능하면 다음 세 표현을 넣으세요.

- **profile the system**
- **quantify the overhead**
- **compare it against the baseline**

### 더 어려운 도전

마지막을 이렇게 끝내보세요.

> **This would allow me to determine whether the observed degradation is actually attributable to the additional overhead.**

---

# 7. Compact Model Answer — 1분

> Contrary to our initial expectation, the optimization reduced throughput by approximately 5%. One plausible explanation is that the optimization introduced additional synchronization and communication overhead that outweighed its computational benefits. However, I would not conclude that this is the definitive cause without further evidence. To test the hypothesis, I would profile the system, quantify the additional overhead, and compare its contribution to total execution time against the baseline. If the overhead increases significantly under the optimized configuration, that would provide stronger evidence that it is responsible for the observed performance degradation.

### 🔍 핵심 표현

**Contrary to our initial expectation**

→ 예상과 다른 결과

**One plausible explanation is...**

→ 가능한 원인

**I would not conclude that...**

→ 성급한 결론 방지

**provide stronger evidence**

→ 추가 실험의 의미 설명

---

# 8. Today's Three Key Sentences

### ① 예상과 다른 결과

> **Contrary to our initial expectation, the optimization reduced throughput.**

### ② 원인에 대한 신중한 가설

> **One plausible explanation is that the optimization introduced additional overhead.**

### ③ 검증

> **To test this hypothesis, I would quantify the overhead and compare it against the baseline.**

# 🎯 Final Mission — 30초

영어로 답해보세요.

> **Your optimization made the system 5% slower. How would you explain this result to a technical interviewer?**

최소 세 개를 사용하세요.

- **contrary to**
- **plausible explanation**
- **introduce overhead**
- **attribute A to B**
- **test this hypothesis**
- **provide stronger evidence**

### 오늘의 핵심

좋은 연구자는 **실패한 실험을 숨기는 사람이 아니라, 실패한 결과를 논리적으로 분석할 수 있는 사람**입니다.

초급:

> **The optimization failed because communication was slow.**

C1:

> **Contrary to our initial hypothesis, the optimization reduced throughput. One plausible explanation is that the additional synchronization and communication overhead outweighed its computational benefits. However, further profiling is required before attributing the degradation to communication overhead alone.**

이제 영어에서도 **“무슨 일이 일어났는가” → “왜 그런 것 같은가” → “아직 무엇을 모르는가” → “어떻게 확인할 것인가”**를 하나의 논리적 흐름으로 설명하는 단계로 넘어가고 있습니다.
