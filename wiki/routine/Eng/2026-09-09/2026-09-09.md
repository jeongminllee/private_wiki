# Day 33 — Explaining Why a Fair Baseline Matters

오늘 목표는 **새로운 방법이 정말 더 좋은지 판단하려면 무엇과 비교해야 하는지 설명하는 것**입니다. 연구에서는 새 모델의 점수만 높다고 끝나는 것이 아니라, **baseline quality → fair comparison → controlled conditions → meaningful improvement** 구조로 말할 수 있어야 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A paper introduces a new method and reports better performance than a simple baseline. However, the baseline is several years old and has not been carefully tuned. Is the comparison convincing?

답변에는 **왜 baseline 선택이 중요한지, 오래되거나 약한 baseline이 어떤 문제를 만드는지, 공정한 비교를 위해 어떤 조건을 맞춰야 하는지, 어떤 추가 실험이 필요한지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The improvement looks promising, but the comparison is only convincing if the baseline is reasonably strong and fairly configured.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: 비교의 타당성 평가

> The comparison is meaningful only if **[condition]**.

예:

> The comparison is meaningful only if both methods are evaluated under comparable conditions.

### Template 2: 약한 baseline 문제 설명

> If the baseline is **[weak/outdated/poorly tuned]**, the reported gain may overstate **[claim]**.

예:

> If the baseline is poorly tuned, the reported gain may overstate the advantage of the new method.

### Template 3: 더 공정한 검증 제안

> A fairer evaluation would compare **[new method]** against **[strong baseline]** while keeping **[conditions]** fixed.

예:

> A fairer evaluation would compare the new method against a well-tuned modern baseline while keeping the dataset and compute budget fixed.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. baseline
**비교 기준**

> The proposed method outperformed the baseline by three percentage points.

일상 영어에서도 비슷한 의미로 쓸 수 있습니다.

> We need a baseline before we can measure improvement.

---

### 2. well-tuned
**충분히 조정된, 하이퍼파라미터가 적절히 최적화된**

> The new model should be compared with a well-tuned baseline.

논문 리뷰에서 매우 자주 쓰입니다.

> A poorly tuned baseline can make an improvement look larger than it really is.

---

### 3. fair comparison
**공정한 비교**

> A fair comparison requires similar training and evaluation conditions.

자연스러운 조합:

> ensure a fair comparison  
> make a fair comparison  
> provide a fair comparison

---

### 4. overstate
**실제보다 크게 보이게 하다, 과장하다**

> The reported result may overstate the benefit of the method.

일반 영어에서도 자연스럽습니다.

> The article overstated the importance of the change.

---

### 5. controlled comparison
**조건을 통제한 비교**

> A controlled comparison helps isolate the effect of the proposed method.

논문식 표현:

> We need a controlled comparison to determine whether the improvement comes from the method itself.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The baseline is too old, so comparison is not fair.

정답:

> The baseline is too old, so the comparison may not be fair.

설명: 특정 비교를 말하므로 `the comparison`이 자연스럽습니다. 또한 바로 단정하기보다 `may not be fair`라고 하면 학술적으로 더 적절합니다.

---

### 2

> We need to compare with more stronger baseline.

정답:

> We need to compare it with a stronger baseline.

설명:

- more stronger ❌
- stronger ✅

비교 대상인 `it`도 넣어주는 것이 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 3

> Maybe the baseline didn't tuned enough.

정답:

> The baseline may not have been tuned sufficiently.

또는 더 자연스럽게:

> The baseline may not have been well tuned.

설명: 과거의 가능성을 말하므로:

> may have been + p.p.

구조를 사용합니다.

---

### 4

> We should match all conditions same.

정답:

> We should keep the experimental conditions the same.

더 학술적으로:

> We should control the experimental conditions.

설명: 한국어의 “조건을 같게 맞추다”를 `match all conditions same`으로 직역하지 않습니다.

---

### 5

> If new model wins weak baseline, it doesn't mean much.

더 자연스럽게:

> If the new model only outperforms a weak baseline, the result is less convincing.

설명: `wins baseline`은 어색합니다. 모델 비교에서는 `outperform`을 사용하세요.

- win a competition ✅
- outperform a baseline ✅

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The new method reports …  
> However, the baseline appears to be …  
> This matters because …  
> If the baseline is poorly tuned, …  
> The comparison is meaningful only if …  
> A fairer evaluation would …  
> If the improvement remains under those conditions, …

오늘은 특히 **“새 모델 점수가 더 높다”와 “새 방법이 실제로 더 좋다”를 분리해서 말하는 것**에 집중하세요.

초급식 표현:

> The new model is better because its score is higher.

더 정확한 표현:

> The new model achieves a higher score, but the baseline may be too weak to support a strong conclusion.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> The reported gain is difficult to interpret without a strong, well-tuned baseline evaluated under comparable experimental conditions.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The reported improvement is encouraging, but I would not consider the comparison fully convincing if the baseline is outdated or poorly tuned. A weak baseline can overstate the apparent benefit of a new method, especially if the two systems are not evaluated under comparable conditions. I would therefore compare the proposed method with a strong, well-tuned modern baseline while keeping the dataset, compute budget, training procedure, and evaluation protocol as consistent as possible. A controlled comparison would help isolate whether the performance gain comes from the proposed method itself rather than from differences in implementation or tuning quality. If the improvement remains consistent under those conditions, the claim would be much more convincing.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“The comparison is meaningful only if …” → “A weak baseline may overstate the improvement.” → “A fairer evaluation would compare both methods under comparable conditions.”**
