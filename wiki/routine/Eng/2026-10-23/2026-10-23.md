# Day 66 — Explaining a Performance Bottleneck
### 병목의 원인을 분석하고 기술적으로 설명하기
**15-Minute English Practice | TOEIC 500–600 → CEFR C1+**

오늘은 지난 연습의 다음 단계입니다.

지난번에는 **실패한 실험의 원인**을 설명했다면, 오늘은 한 단계 더 나아가 **“어디가 병목이고, 왜 그렇게 판단했는가?”**를 영어로 설명합니다.

> **Observation → Evidence → Bottleneck → Cause → Action**

## 1. 🎤 Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 상황을 가정하세요.

> You benchmarked an LLM serving system with 1, 2, 4, and 8 GPUs.
>
> As the number of GPUs increased, throughput improved at first.
>
> However, after 4 GPUs, the improvement became very small.
>
> Profiling showed that inter-GPU communication time increased significantly.

면접관이 묻습니다.

> **“What is limiting scalability in this system?”**

### 60–90초 답변

다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. GPU를 늘렸을 때 어떤 변화가 있었는가?
2. 어느 지점부터 scaling efficiency가 떨어졌는가?
3. profiling 결과는 무엇을 보여주는가?
4. 가장 가능성 높은 bottleneck은 무엇인가?
5. 어떻게 개선할 것인가?

**시작 문장**

> The system scales reasonably well up to four GPUs, but the performance gains become marginal beyond that point.

### 오늘의 도전

다음 표현을 최소 2개 사용하세요.

- **scaling efficiency**
- **become a bottleneck**
- **account for**
- **diminishing returns**
- **from a profiling perspective**

---

# 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1 — 병목 지점 설명

> **A becomes the main bottleneck once B exceeds C.**

> Inter-GPU communication becomes the main bottleneck once the GPU count exceeds four.

일상 영어:

> Traffic becomes a bottleneck once the number of commuters exceeds the road's capacity.

---

### Template 2 — 근거를 제시하기

> **This is supported by the fact that A increases as B increases.**

> This is supported by the fact that communication time increases substantially as the GPU count increases.

더 자연스럽게:

> **The profiling results support this hypothesis, as communication time increases significantly beyond four GPUs.**

`as`를 사용하면 두 현상의 변화 관계를 자연스럽게 연결할 수 있습니다.

---

### Template 3 — 해결책 제시

> **To address this issue, we could [action].**

> To address this issue, we could reduce communication overhead or investigate a different parallelization strategy.

C1 확장:

> **To mitigate this bottleneck, we could investigate communication-efficient parallelization strategies.**

`mitigate` = 문제의 심각도나 영향을 완화하다

---

# 3. Five Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### ① Bottleneck — 병목

> Communication becomes a bottleneck at higher GPU counts.

더 일반적으로:

> Lack of engineers has become a bottleneck for the project.

**자주 쓰는 조합**

- identify a bottleneck
- remove a bottleneck
- become a bottleneck
- system bottleneck
- performance bottleneck

---

### ② Scaling efficiency — 확장 효율

> Scaling efficiency decreases as we add more GPUs.

GPU를 추가할수록 scaling efficiency가 떨어집니다.

기술 면접에서 매우 유용한 표현입니다.

> poor scaling efficiency  
> near-linear scaling  
> scaling efficiency degrades

---

### ③ Diminishing returns — 한계효용 감소 / 추가 투입 대비 효과 감소

> We observe diminishing returns beyond four GPUs.

4개 GPU를 넘어가면 추가적인 GPU 투입의 효과가 감소합니다.

일반 영어:

> Spending more money on the project is producing diminishing returns.

**중요:** 단순히 “성능이 감소한다”가 아닙니다.

**추가 투입은 계속되지만 추가적인 이득이 점점 작아진다**는 의미입니다.

---

### ④ Account for — ~을 차지하다

> Communication accounts for a significant portion of the total execution time.

통신이 전체 실행 시간에서 상당한 부분을 차지합니다.

숫자와 함께 사용하면 특히 좋습니다.

> Communication accounts for approximately 30% of the total latency.

---

### ⑤ Mitigate — 완화하다

> We need to mitigate the impact of communication overhead.

통신 오버헤드의 영향을 완화해야 합니다.

일반 영어:

> The new policy aims to mitigate the risks associated with the project.

**비슷하지만 다른 표현**

- eliminate = 완전히 제거
- reduce = 감소
- mitigate = 영향을 완화

연구에서는 `mitigate`가 매우 유용합니다.

---

# 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

## ①

❌ The performance is going down when we add GPUs.

✅ **Performance degrades as we add more GPUs.**

또는:

> **Performance gains diminish as we add more GPUs.**

### 왜?

`go down`은 일상 영어에서는 괜찮지만 기술적인 분석에서는 너무 일반적입니다.

- performance degrades
- performance deteriorates
- gains diminish

같은 표현이 더 정확합니다.

---

## ②

❌ Communication takes many percentage of execution time.

✅ **Communication accounts for a significant percentage of execution time.**

또는 숫자가 있다면:

> **Communication accounts for 35% of the total execution time.**

### 왜?

`percentage`는 보통 구체적인 수치와 함께 사용합니다.

> 35 percent of the time

또는 수치가 없으면:

> a significant proportion  
> a substantial portion

이 자연스럽습니다.

---

## ③

❌ The more GPUs we use, the performance improvement becomes smaller.

✅ **The more GPUs we use, the smaller the performance improvement becomes.**

### 핵심 문법

`The + comparative, the + comparative`

> The more GPUs we use, the smaller the gains become.

> The larger the model, the more memory it requires.

이 구조는 **C1 말하기에서 매우 유용합니다.**

---

## ④

❌ We need to solve this bottleneck.

✅ **We need to address this bottleneck.**

또는:

> **We need to mitigate the impact of this bottleneck.**

### 왜?

`solve a bottleneck`도 이해는 되지만 자연스럽지 않습니다.

기술 영어에서는:

> address an issue  
> address a bottleneck  
> mitigate a problem  
> reduce the overhead

가 더 일반적입니다.

---

## ⑤

❌ Adding more GPUs doesn't make much performance.

✅ **Adding more GPUs provides diminishing returns.**

또는:

> **Adding more GPUs results in only marginal performance gains.**

### 핵심

`make performance`는 한국어식 표현입니다.

성능 향상은:

> improve performance  
> provide performance gains  
> result in higher throughput  
> yield marginal improvements

처럼 표현합니다.

---

# 5. Progressive Speaking Challenge — 2분

## Level 1 — Basic

> Adding more GPUs improves throughput at first, but the improvement becomes smaller after four GPUs. I think communication is the main problem.

좋습니다.

하지만 아직 **근거와 기술적 판단**이 부족합니다.

---

## Level 2 — Intermediate

> The system scales reasonably well up to four GPUs, but we observe diminishing returns beyond that point. Profiling shows that inter-GPU communication time increases significantly, suggesting that communication overhead has become a major bottleneck.

이제:

**Observation → Evidence → Interpretation**

이 연결되었습니다.

---

## Level 3 — C1

> The system exhibits relatively strong scaling up to four GPUs, after which the marginal throughput gains become increasingly small. From a profiling perspective, this degradation appears to be closely associated with the growing proportion of time spent on inter-GPU communication. As communication overhead begins to dominate execution time, adding more GPUs provides diminishing returns. To mitigate this bottleneck, I would investigate communication-efficient parallelization strategies and determine whether the workload can be partitioned to reduce synchronization and data-transfer overhead.

### C1 포인트

**exhibits relatively strong scaling**

→ 단순히 `scales well`보다 학술적입니다.

**marginal throughput gains**

→ 추가로 얻는 처리량 증가분

**appears to be closely associated with**

→ 인과관계를 지나치게 단정하지 않음

**begins to dominate execution time**

→ 전체 실행시간에서 특정 요소의 영향이 커지는 상황

**communication-efficient**

→ 기술적인 복합 형용사

# 6. 🎤 60-Second Interview Challenge

면접관:

> **“If adding more GPUs gives diminishing returns, does that mean scaling to more GPUs is useless?”**

바로 답해보세요.

다음 표현을 사용하세요.

> **Not necessarily.**

> **It depends on...**

> **The key issue is...**

> **From a practical standpoint...**

### 추천 시작

> **Not necessarily. The key issue is whether the additional throughput justifies the additional cost and communication overhead.**

그리고 이어서:

> From a practical standpoint, ...

로 마무리해보세요.

---

# 7. Compact Model Answer — 1분

> Not necessarily. The key issue is whether the additional throughput justifies the cost of adding more GPUs. In our case, the system continues to improve as we add GPUs, but the marginal gains become increasingly small beyond four GPUs. Profiling suggests that inter-GPU communication is becoming a major bottleneck and accounts for a growing portion of execution time. From a practical standpoint, adding more GPUs may still make sense if we need to meet a specific throughput target, but it may not be cost-effective if the workload is relatively small.

### 🔍 오늘의 C1 표현

**Not necessarily.**

→ 상대방의 전제를 바로 반박하지 않고 정교하게 수정합니다.

**whether A justifies B**

→ 비용 대비 효과를 판단합니다.

**marginal gains become increasingly small**

→ 한계효과 감소를 정확하게 표현합니다.

**may not be cost-effective**

→ 기술적 판단을 비즈니스 관점까지 확장합니다.

### ② Scaling 한계

> **The key question is whether the additional throughput justifies the additional cost.**

영어로 답해보세요.

> **Why might adding more GPUs fail to produce proportional performance improvements?**

최소 세 개를 사용하세요.

- **bottleneck**
- **scaling efficiency**
- **diminishing returns**
- **account for**
- **mitigate**

### 오늘의 핵심

단순한 영어:

> **More GPUs make the system slower.**

보다 훨씬 좋은 기술적 설명은:

> **Although adding GPUs initially improves throughput, the marginal gains diminish beyond a certain point because inter-GPU communication begins to account for a larger proportion of execution time.**

그리고 C1 수준에서는 여기서 한 단계 더 나아가:

> **From a practical standpoint, the relevant question is not whether additional GPUs improve performance, but whether the incremental throughput justifies the associated computational and communication costs.**
