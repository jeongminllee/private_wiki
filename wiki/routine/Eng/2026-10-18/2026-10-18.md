# Day 61 — Making a Recommendation Based on Evidence
### 근거를 바탕으로 의견과 의사결정을 영어로 설명하기
**15-Minute English Practice | TOEIC 500–600 → CEFR C1+**

오늘은 한 단계 더 올라갑니다. 단순히 실험 결과를 설명하는 것이 아니라, **여러 근거를 종합해서 “그래서 무엇을 해야 하는가?”를 영어로 주장하는 연습**입니다.

오늘의 핵심 구조:

> **Evidence → Trade-off → Judgment → Recommendation**

---

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 상황을 가정하세요.

> You compared two inference configurations.
>
> **Configuration A**
> - Throughput: 1,000 tokens/s
> - P99 latency: 300 ms
> - GPU memory: 32 GB
>
> **Configuration B**
> - Throughput: 1,100 tokens/s
> - P99 latency: 420 ms
> - GPU memory: 36 GB
>
> A teammate asks:
>
> **“Which configuration should we use?”**

### 🎤 60–90초 답변

단순히:

> B is faster.

라고 끝내지 마세요.

다음 구조를 사용하세요.

1. 어떤 차이가 있는가?
2. 어떤 trade-off가 있는가?
3. 어떤 workload에서는 A가 유리한가?
4. 어떤 workload에서는 B가 유리한가?
5. 최종적으로 무엇을 추천하는가?

**시작 문장**

> Configuration B provides higher throughput, but this comes at the cost of substantially higher P99 latency.

---

# 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1 — 장점과 비용을 동시에 설명

> **A offers [benefit], but this comes at the cost of [drawback].**

> A offers higher throughput, but this comes at the cost of higher latency.

일상 영어:

> Working from home offers greater flexibility, but this comes at the cost of less face-to-face interaction.

**핵심 표현**

> **come at the cost of ~**

= ~을 대가로 하다

---

### Template 2 — 조건에 따라 판단하기

> **Whether A is preferable depends on [factor].**

> Whether Configuration B is preferable depends on the workload.

일상 영어:

> Whether I take the job depends on the salary and working conditions.

**C1 확장**

> Whether the additional throughput justifies the increase in latency depends largely on the application's latency requirements.

---

### Template 3 — 최종 추천하기

> **Given these trade-offs, I would recommend [A/B] for [condition].**

> Given these trade-offs, I would recommend Configuration A for latency-sensitive applications.

더 강하게:

> **Given these trade-offs, I would favor A unless throughput is the primary objective.**

`favor` = ~을 더 선호하다

이 표현은 기술 토론과 논문 Discussion에서 매우 유용합니다.

---

# 3. Five Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### ① Trade-off — 상충관계

> There is a clear trade-off between throughput and latency.

처리량과 지연시간 사이에는 명확한 상충관계가 있습니다.

일상 영어:

> There is often a trade-off between convenience and cost.

**핵심:** A가 좋아지면 B가 나빠질 수 있는 상황을 설명할 때 사용합니다.

---

### ② Justify — 정당화하다

> The throughput improvement may not justify the additional memory usage.

처리량 개선이 추가 메모리 사용량을 정당화하지 못할 수도 있습니다.

일상 영어:

> The benefits don't justify the additional cost.

**자주 쓰는 구조**

> `justify + 명사`

> `justify doing something`

---

### ③ Substantial — 상당한, 큰

> Configuration B introduces a substantial increase in latency.

B 구성은 상당한 지연시간 증가를 가져옵니다.

일상 영어:

> The project requires a substantial amount of time.

`substantial`은 `big`보다 전문적이고 정확한 표현입니다.

---

### ④ Latency-sensitive — 지연시간에 민감한

> Configuration A is more appropriate for latency-sensitive applications.

A 구성이 지연시간에 민감한 애플리케이션에 더 적합합니다.

예:

- latency-sensitive applications
- latency-sensitive workloads
- latency-sensitive services

LLM serving 면접에서 바로 사용할 수 있는 표현입니다.

---

### ⑤ Unless — ~하지 않는 한

> I would choose Configuration A unless throughput is the primary objective.

처리량이 최우선 목표가 아닌 한 A를 선택하겠습니다.

일상 영어:

> I won't buy a new laptop unless my current one becomes unusable.

**주의**

`unless` 자체에 이미 부정적인 의미가 포함되어 있으므로 불필요하게 `not`을 붙이지 않습니다.

- unless it doesn't work ❌ *(대부분의 경우 의도와 달라질 수 있음)*
- unless it works ✅

---

# 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

### Question 1

❌ B is faster than A, but latency is more high.

✅ **B is faster than A, but its latency is higher.**

**왜?**

`high`의 비교급은 `higher`입니다.

- more high ❌
- higher ✅

그리고 반복해서 `latency`라고 하기보다 `its latency`라고 하면 자연스럽습니다.

---

### Question 2

❌ The advantage cannot compensate the additional memory usage.

✅ **The advantage cannot compensate for the additional memory usage.**

**핵심**

`compensate for + 명사`

`for`를 빼면 안 됩니다.

> compensate for the cost  
> compensate for the loss  
> compensate for the additional overhead

---

### Question 3

❌ It depends on that how much throughput we need.

✅ **It depends on how much throughput we need.**

또는:

> **It depends on how much throughput is required.**

**왜?**

`depend on` 뒤에 간접의문문이 올 수 있습니다.

> depend on **how much...**

`that`은 필요하지 않습니다.

---

### Question 4

❌ We should choose B because it has more throughput.

✅ **We should choose B because it provides higher throughput.**

**왜?**

`throughput`에는 일반적으로 `more`보다 `higher`가 자연스럽습니다.

> higher throughput  
> higher latency  
> lower memory usage

그리고 기술 영어에서는:

> provide higher throughput

이 자연스럽습니다.

---

### Question 5

❌ The benefit is not enough to justify to use B.

✅ **The benefit is not enough to justify using B.**

**핵심 문법**

`justify + 동명사`

> justify using  
> justify deploying  
> justify adopting

`justify to use` ❌

---

# 5. Progressive Speaking Challenge — 2분

## Level 1 — Basic

> B has higher throughput, but it also has higher latency. I think A is better for applications that need low latency.

---

## Level 2 — Intermediate

> Configuration B provides 10% higher throughput, but this comes at the cost of substantially higher P99 latency. Therefore, I would prefer A for latency-sensitive workloads.

---

## Level 3 — C1

> Although Configuration B delivers approximately 10% higher throughput, the improvement comes at the cost of a substantial increase in P99 latency and GPU memory consumption. Given these trade-offs, I would favor Configuration A for latency-sensitive workloads, unless maximizing throughput is the primary objective. The appropriate choice ultimately depends on the application's performance requirements.

### 🎤 60초 실전

다음 구조를 사용해서 직접 말해보세요.

> **Configuration A/B provides...**

> **However, this comes at the cost of...**

> **Whether this trade-off is acceptable depends on...**

> **Given these considerations, I would recommend...**

> **I would choose the alternative if...**

---

# 6. Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I would choose Configuration A for most latency-sensitive applications. Although Configuration B provides 10% higher throughput, its P99 latency increases from 300 to 420 milliseconds, which is a substantial deterioration. It also requires an additional 4 GB of GPU memory. The higher throughput may justify these costs for batch-oriented workloads where throughput is the primary objective. However, for interactive applications, the latency increase is likely to have a greater impact on the user experience. Therefore, I would favor Configuration A unless maximizing throughput is more important than latency.

### 🔍 C1 표현 포인트

**Although A, B**

양보 구조를 사용하여 단순한 문장 연결을 피합니다.

**a substantial deterioration**

단순히 `latency is worse`라고 하지 않고 전문적으로 표현합니다.

**is likely to have a greater impact**

확정하지 않고 합리적인 추론을 제시합니다.

**unless maximizing throughput is more important**

조건을 붙여 자신의 추천을 정교하게 만듭니다.

# 7. Today's Three Key Sentences

### ① Trade-off

> **The improvement comes at the cost of higher latency.**

### ② 조건부 판단

> **Whether this trade-off is acceptable depends on the workload.**

### ③ 최종 추천

> **Given these considerations, I would favor A for latency-sensitive applications.**

## 🎯 Final Mission — 30초

다음 질문에 영어로 답해보세요.

> **If you had to choose between 10% higher throughput and 30% lower latency, which would you prioritize? Why?**

오늘 배운 표현 중 최소 두 개를 사용하세요.

- **trade-off**
- **justify**
- **unless**
- **depends on**
- **I would favor...**

**오늘의 핵심은 “A가 더 좋다”에서 끝내지 않는 것입니다.**

C1 수준의 답변은:

> **A is better.**

가 아니라,

> **Although B offers higher throughput, the additional latency may not justify the performance gain for latency-sensitive workloads. Given these trade-offs, I would favor A unless throughput is the primary objective.**

처럼 **근거 → 상충관계 → 조건 → 결론**을 하나의 논리로 연결합니다.
