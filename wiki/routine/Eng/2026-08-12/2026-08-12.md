# Day 13 — Explaining a Trade-off and Reaching a Balanced Conclusion

오늘 목표는 **두 가지 중요한 목표가 충돌할 때, 어느 한쪽만 고집하지 않고 trade-off를 설명한 뒤 조건부 결론을 내리는 것**입니다. 연구 발표, 모델 선택, 시스템 설계, 면접 답변에서 매우 자주 쓰는 구조입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A new model is 2% more accurate than the baseline, but it is twice as slow and uses much more memory. Would you adopt it?

답변에는 다음을 포함하세요.

1. 성능 향상의 장점
2. 속도와 메모리 비용
3. 어떤 환경에서는 채택할지
4. 어떤 환경에서는 채택하지 않을지

---

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: trade-off 제시

> There is a clear trade-off between **[benefit]** and **[cost]**.

예:

> There is a clear trade-off between accuracy and inference efficiency.

### Template 2: 조건부 판단

> Whether the improvement is worthwhile depends on **[context]**.

예:

> Whether the improvement is worthwhile depends on the latency requirements of the application.

### Template 3: 균형 잡힌 결론

> On balance, I would **[decision]**, unless **[exception]**.

예:

> On balance, I would keep the baseline, unless the additional accuracy is critical for the application.

---

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. worthwhile

**시간·비용을 들일 가치가 있는**

> The accuracy gain is useful, but I am not sure it is worthwhile given the higher latency.

자주 쓰는 구조:

> be worthwhile
> make something worthwhile

---

### 2. marginal

**미미한, 크지 않은**

> The new model provides only a marginal improvement in accuracy.

논문에서 자주 쓰입니다.

> The performance gain was statistically significant but practically marginal.

---

### 3. substantial

**상당한, 큰**

> The model requires a substantial increase in memory.

`big`보다 훨씬 자연스럽고 전문적입니다.

---

### 4. offset

**장점을 상쇄하다**

> The higher computational cost may offset the benefit of improved accuracy.

좋은 구조:

> A may offset the benefits of B.

예:

> Increased latency may offset the performance advantage.

---

### 5. practical significance

**실질적 중요성, 실제로 의미 있는 정도**

> The improvement is statistically significant, but its practical significance is unclear.

이 표현은 논문을 읽을 때 매우 중요합니다.

* statistical significance = 통계적으로 유의한가
* practical significance = 실제로 쓸 만큼 중요한가

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1

> The model is two times more slower.

정답:

> The model is twice as slow.

또는:

> The model is two times slower.

설명:

`slower` 자체가 비교급이므로 `more slower`는 틀립니다.

* more slower ❌
* slower ✅
* twice as slow ✅

---

### 2

> The accuracy increased only 2%.

더 정확한 표현:

> The accuracy increased by 2 percentage points.

설명:

변화량 앞에는 `by`가 필요합니다.

그리고 절대 변화라면 `percentage points`가 더 정확합니다.

---

### 3

> I think the speed is more important comparing with accuracy.

정답:

> I think speed is more important than accuracy.

또는 더 전문적으로:

> In this application, I would prioritize speed over accuracy.

설명:

비교급에는 `than`을 사용합니다.

* more important comparing with ❌
* more important than ✅

---

### 4

> The improvement is too small for changing the model.

더 자연스럽게:

> The improvement is too small to justify changing the model.

설명:

`too + 형용사 + to + 동사` 구조를 쓰는 것이 자연스럽습니다.

또한 `justify`를 넣으면 논리적 판단이 더 명확해집니다.

---

### 5

> The extra cost cancels the advantage.

더 자연스럽게:

> The additional cost may offset the advantage.

설명:

`cancel`도 의미는 통하지만, 기술적·분석적 문맥에서는 `offset`이 더 자연스럽습니다.

---

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름을 따라 말해보세요.

> The new model provides a modest improvement in accuracy.
> However, this comes at the cost of …
> There is a clear trade-off between … and …
> Whether the improvement is worthwhile depends on …
> In a latency-sensitive environment, I would …
> On the other hand, if accuracy were critical, I would …
> On balance, I would …

오늘은 **무조건 좋다 / 나쁘다**가 아니라, 조건에 따라 판단을 조절하세요.

강한 표현:

> The new model is better.

더 좋은 표현:

> The new model is more accurate, but not necessarily more practical.

더 고급스러운 표현:

> The additional accuracy may not justify the substantial increase in latency and memory usage.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The new model provides a modest improvement in accuracy, but this comes at a substantial computational cost. There is a clear trade-off between predictive performance and inference efficiency. Whether the 2-percentage-point improvement is worthwhile depends on the application. In a latency-sensitive production system, I would probably keep the baseline because the additional memory usage and slower inference may offset the benefit of higher accuracy. However, if the application were safety-critical and even a small accuracy improvement had practical significance, I would consider adopting the new model. On balance, I would not switch models based on accuracy alone. I would evaluate latency, memory usage, operating cost, and the practical value of the improvement before making the final decision.

오늘의 핵심 표현은 세 개입니다: **“There is a trade-off between …” → “Whether it is worthwhile depends on …” → “The cost may offset the benefit.”**
