# Day 34 — Distinguishing Correlation from Causation

오늘 목표는 **두 변수의 관계가 관찰되었다고 해서 곧바로 원인과 결과의 관계라고 결론 내리지 않는 것**입니다. 오늘은 **observed relationship → possible confounder → causal claim → stronger evidence** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A study finds that people who use AI tools more frequently tend to complete programming tasks faster. Can we conclude that using AI tools directly causes higher productivity?

답변에는 **관찰된 관계가 무엇인지, 왜 바로 인과관계라고 할 수 없는지, 어떤 제3의 변수가 영향을 줄 수 있는지, 더 강한 인과적 근거를 얻으려면 어떤 실험이 필요한지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The study shows an association between AI-tool usage and task completion speed, but that does not necessarily establish a causal relationship.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **상관관계와 인과관계를 구분할 때** 사용합니다.

> The observed relationship between **[A]** and **[B]** does not necessarily mean that **[A] causes B**.

예:

> The observed relationship between AI-tool usage and productivity does not necessarily mean that AI tools directly cause higher productivity.

두 번째는 **제3의 변수를 제시할 때** 유용합니다.

> One possible confounding factor is **[factor]**, which could influence both **[A]** and **[B]**.

예:

> One possible confounding factor is developer experience, which could influence both AI-tool adoption and task completion speed.

세 번째는 **더 강한 근거를 제안할 때** 씁니다.

> To make a stronger causal claim, we would need **[study design/evidence]**.

예:

> To make a stronger causal claim, we would need a controlled experiment in which participants are randomly assigned to different conditions.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**association**은 두 변수가 함께 변화하는 관계를 뜻합니다.

> The study found a positive association between tool usage and productivity.

`relationship`보다 연구 문맥에서 더 정확하게 사용할 수 있습니다.

**causal relationship**은 원인과 결과의 관계입니다.

> The data do not provide sufficient evidence for a causal relationship.

논문에서 자주 쓰이는 표현은 `causal effect`, `causal inference`, `causal claim`입니다.

**confounding factor**는 두 변수 모두에 영향을 주어 관계를 왜곡할 수 있는 제3의 변수입니다.

> Experience level may be a confounding factor.

예를 들어 숙련된 개발자가 AI 도구도 더 많이 사용하고 코딩도 더 빠르다면, AI 사용 자체의 효과를 분리하기 어렵습니다.

**account for**는 어떤 현상이나 차이를 설명한다는 뜻입니다.

> Differences in experience may account for part of the observed productivity gap.

일반 영어에서도 매우 자주 씁니다.

> How do you account for the difference?

**randomized controlled experiment**는 참가자를 무작위로 조건에 배정하여 원인 효과를 더 강하게 검증하는 실험입니다.

> A randomized controlled experiment would provide stronger evidence of causality.

논문에서는 간단히 `randomized experiment`라고도 많이 표현합니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> AI usage has correlation with productivity.

더 자연스럽게:

> AI usage is correlated with productivity.

또는:

> There is a correlation between AI usage and productivity.

설명: `has correlation with`보다 위 두 표현이 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.

- is correlated with ✅
- correlation between A and B ✅

### 2

> This result means AI makes people more productive.

더 정확하게:

> This result does not necessarily mean that AI makes people more productive.

설명: 관찰 연구에서 바로 인과관계를 단정하면 안 됩니다. `does not necessarily mean`은 매우 중요한 표현입니다.

### 3

> Maybe experienced people use AI more often.

더 학술적으로:

> More experienced developers may be more likely to use AI tools.

설명: `Maybe`로 문장을 시작하는 것보다 `may`, `might`, `could`를 활용하면 연구 영어에서 더 자연스럽습니다.

### 4

> Experience can affect to both variables.

정답:

> Experience can affect both variables.

설명: `affect`는 타동사이므로 `to`를 쓰지 않습니다.

- affect to ❌
- affect ✅

반대로 `effect`는 보통 명사입니다.

> have an effect on

### 5

> We need experiment for prove causality.

정답:

> We need an experiment to provide stronger evidence of causality.

설명: 두 가지를 고쳐야 합니다.

- for prove ❌
- to prove ✅

하지만 연구에서는 `prove causality`라고 너무 강하게 말하기보다 `provide stronger evidence of causality`가 더 정확합니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 말해보세요.

> The study found an association between …  
> However, this does not necessarily mean that …  
> One possible confounding factor is …  
> This factor could influence both … and …  
> As a result, the observed relationship may partly reflect …  
> To make a stronger causal claim, we would need …  
> Based on the current evidence, I would conclude that …

오늘은 특히 **“A와 B가 같이 움직인다”와 “A가 B를 만든다”를 분리해서 말하는 것**에 집중하세요.

초급식 표현:

> People who use AI work faster, so AI makes people faster.

더 정확한 표현:

> People who use AI tools tend to complete tasks faster, but the relationship may be influenced by other factors.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although AI-tool usage is positively associated with faster task completion, the observational design does not allow us to isolate the causal effect of the tool itself.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The study shows that more frequent use of AI tools is associated with faster task completion, but I would not interpret this as direct evidence of causation. One important issue is that other variables may influence both AI-tool usage and productivity. For example, more experienced developers may adopt AI tools more frequently while also completing tasks faster regardless of the tools they use. Experience could therefore act as a confounding factor. To make a stronger causal claim, I would prefer a randomized controlled experiment in which participants are assigned to use either AI-assisted or non-AI workflows under comparable conditions. If a consistent productivity difference remains after controlling for relevant factors, the evidence for a causal effect would be much stronger. Based on the current study, I would conclude that AI usage is associated with productivity, but causality has not yet been established.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“Correlation does not necessarily imply causation.” → “A confounding factor may influence both variables.” → “We need stronger evidence before making a causal claim.”**
