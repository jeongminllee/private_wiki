# Day 27 — Choosing a Decision Threshold, Not Just a Model

오늘 목표는 **모델 자체의 성능과 실제 의사결정 기준을 분리해서 설명하는 것**입니다. 같은 모델이라도 threshold를 어떻게 설정하느냐에 따라 precision, recall, false positives, false negatives가 달라질 수 있습니다. 오늘은 **model score → threshold → trade-off → operational objective** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A classifier outputs probabilities between 0 and 1. The default threshold is 0.5, but missing a positive case is much more costly than generating a false alarm. Would you keep the threshold at 0.5? Why or why not?

답변에는 다음을 포함하세요.

* 0.5가 항상 최선은 아닌 이유
* threshold를 낮추면 어떤 변화가 생기는지
* recall과 precision 사이의 trade-off
* 실제 업무 목표에 맞게 threshold를 정해야 하는 이유

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> I would not automatically use a threshold of 0.5 because the optimal threshold depends on the cost of different types of errors.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: 기본값을 그대로 쓰지 않는 이유

> The default threshold of **[X]** is convenient, but it may not align with **[objective]**.

예:

> The default threshold of 0.5 is convenient, but it may not align with the goal of minimizing false negatives.

### Template 2: threshold 변화의 효과 설명

> Lowering the threshold would generally increase **[metric]**, but it may come at the cost of **[metric]**.

예:

> Lowering the threshold would generally increase recall, but it may come at the cost of lower precision.

### Template 3: 실제 목적과 연결

> I would choose the threshold based on **[cost / capacity / risk tolerance]**, rather than using a fixed value by default.

예:

> I would choose the threshold based on the cost of missed cases and the team’s capacity to review false alarms.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. threshold

**의사결정 임계값**

> We classified cases as positive when the predicted probability exceeded the threshold.

자주 쓰는 표현:

> lower the threshold
> raise the threshold
> threshold selection

---

### 2. false alarm

**잘못된 경고, false positive**

> Lowering the threshold may generate more false alarms.

일반 영어에서도 자연스럽습니다.

> The security system triggered several false alarms.

---

### 3. miss a case

**실제 중요한 사례를 놓치다**

> In this application, missing a positive case is more costly than reviewing a false alarm.

기술적으로는 `false negative`를 뜻하지만, 말하기에서는 `miss a case`가 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 4. operating point

**모델을 실제로 운용할 때 선택한 threshold 지점**

> We selected an operating point that prioritizes recall over precision.

논문에서 자주 나옵니다.

> Different operating points may be appropriate for different deployment settings.

---

### 5. capacity constraint

**처리 가능한 양의 한계**

> The threshold should also reflect the team’s capacity constraints.

예:

> Even if a lower threshold improves recall, it may create more alerts than the review team can handle.

이 표현은 실무 영어에서 매우 유용합니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> We should reduce the threshold for increase recall.

정답:

> We should lower the threshold to increase recall.

설명: 목적 표현은 `to + 동사원형`을 사용합니다.

* for increase ❌
* to increase ✅

그리고 threshold에는 `reduce`보다 `lower`가 더 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 2

> If threshold becomes lower, recall will be more higher.

정답:

> If the threshold is lowered, recall will generally increase.

설명:

* more higher ❌
* higher ✅

기술적으로는 항상 반드시 증가한다고 단정하기보다 `generally increase`가 더 안전합니다.

---

### 3

> We need to consider about false negatives.

정답:

> We need to consider false negatives.

설명: `consider`는 타동사이므로 `about`을 쓰지 않습니다.

* consider about ❌
* consider ✅

---

### 4

> 0.5 is not always the best threshold for every situations.

정답:

> A threshold of 0.5 is not always optimal for every situation.

설명:

* every situations ❌
* every situation ✅

또한 연구·기술 영어에서는 `best`보다 `optimal`이 더 정확할 때가 많습니다.

---

### 5

> We should choose threshold by business purpose.

더 자연스럽게:

> We should choose the threshold based on the business objective.

또는:

> The threshold should align with the operational objective.

설명: `by business purpose`는 한국어식 직역에 가깝습니다.

* based on the objective ✅
* align with the objective ✅

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 말해보세요.

> I would not automatically use …
> The threshold should depend on …
> If we lower the threshold, …
> This would reduce … but increase …
> In this application, missing a positive case is …
> However, we also need to consider …
> Therefore, I would choose an operating point that …

오늘 핵심은 **“모델이 좋다”와 “어떤 threshold에서 쓸 것인가”를 분리해서 말하는 것**입니다.

초급식 표현:

> Recall is important, so we should lower the threshold.

더 정확한 표현:

> Lowering the threshold may improve recall, but it will likely increase the number of false positives.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> The appropriate threshold should reflect the relative cost of false negatives and false positives, as well as the system’s operational capacity.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I would not automatically keep the threshold at 0.5 because the appropriate decision threshold depends on the operational objective. If missing a positive case is very costly, I would consider lowering the threshold to increase recall. However, this would likely reduce precision and generate more false alarms. The final decision should therefore reflect both the cost of false negatives and the team’s capacity to review additional alerts. Rather than treating 0.5 as a universal default, I would evaluate several operating points and choose the one that provides an acceptable trade-off between recall, precision, and workload. In other words, threshold selection should be driven by the real-world consequences of prediction errors, not by convention alone.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“The optimal threshold depends on the objective.” → “Lowering the threshold may increase recall at the cost of precision.” → “Threshold selection should reflect real-world error costs.”**
