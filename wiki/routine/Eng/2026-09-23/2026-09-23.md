# Day 43 — Explaining Confidence Intervals and Uncertainty

오늘 목표는 **평균 점수 하나만 보고 결과를 판단하지 않고, 그 추정값이 얼마나 불확실한지까지 설명하는 것**입니다. 오늘은 **point estimate → confidence interval → uncertainty → overlap → cautious interpretation** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> Model A achieves 84% accuracy and Model B achieves 86% accuracy. However, their confidence intervals overlap substantially. Can we confidently say that Model B is better?

답변에는 **평균 점수 차이만으로 충분한지, confidence interval이 무엇을 보여주는지, 왜 불확실성을 함께 봐야 하는지, 어떤 추가 실험이 필요한지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> Model B has a higher average accuracy, but the overlapping confidence intervals suggest that the difference may be less certain than it first appears.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **평균값과 불확실성을 함께 말할 때** 사용합니다.

> Although **[model A]** has a higher average **[metric]**, the uncertainty around the estimate should also be considered.

예:

> Although Model B has a higher average accuracy, the uncertainty around the estimate should also be considered.

두 번째는 **신뢰구간을 해석할 때** 유용합니다.

> The confidence interval indicates the range within which **[the true value / the estimate]** is likely to lie.

예:

> The confidence interval indicates the range within which the true performance is likely to lie.

세 번째는 **강한 결론을 피할 때** 씁니다.

> Based on the current results, I would say that **[trend]**, but the evidence is not yet strong enough to conclude that **[strong claim]**.

예:

> Based on the current results, I would say that Model B appears better on average, but the evidence is not yet strong enough to conclude that it is consistently superior.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**confidence interval**은 추정값 주변의 불확실성을 나타내는 구간입니다.

> The 95% confidence interval was relatively wide.

논문에서는 `95% CI`라고 줄여서 쓰는 경우가 많습니다.

> Accuracy was 86% (95% CI: 82–89%).

**point estimate**는 하나의 숫자로 표현된 대표 추정값입니다.

> The point estimate suggests better performance, but the uncertainty is still large.

예를 들어 평균 정확도 86% 자체가 point estimate입니다.

**uncertainty**는 추정 결과가 얼마나 불확실한지를 의미합니다.

> We should report uncertainty rather than presenting only the average score.

**overlap**은 두 구간이 서로 겹치는 것을 뜻합니다.

> The confidence intervals overlap substantially.

일반 영어에서도 자주 씁니다.

> Their responsibilities overlap.

**consistent**는 여러 반복 실험에서도 비슷한 경향이 유지된다는 뜻입니다.

> The improvement was consistent across multiple runs.

연구 영어에서는 `consistent improvement`, `consistent trend`, `consistent effect`가 자주 쓰입니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> Model B is more better than Model A.

정답:

> Model B is better than Model A.

설명: `better` 자체가 이미 비교급이라 `more`를 붙이지 않습니다.

- more better ❌
- better ✅

### 2

> But difference is not much clear.

더 자연스럽게:

> But the difference is not very clear.

또는 연구 영어로:

> But the difference is uncertain.

설명: `much clear`는 어색합니다.

- much clear ❌
- very clear ✅
- uncertain ✅

### 3

> The confidence interval is meaning uncertainty.

정답:

> The confidence interval represents uncertainty.

또는:

> The confidence interval reflects uncertainty in the estimate.

설명: 영어에서는 `is meaning`보다 `represents`, `indicates`, `reflects`가 자연스럽습니다.

### 4

> Their confidence interval are overlapped.

정답:

> Their confidence intervals overlap.

설명: `overlap`은 여기서 능동형으로 자연스럽게 사용합니다.

- are overlapped ❌
- overlap ✅

그리고 두 모델이므로 `intervals` 복수형이 필요합니다.

### 5

> We need more experiment for make conclusion.

정답:

> We need more experiments before drawing a conclusion.

또는:

> We need additional experiments to support a stronger conclusion.

설명:

- more experiment ❌
- more experiments ✅
- for make ❌
- to make / before drawing ✅

연구 영어에서는 `draw a conclusion`이 매우 자연스럽습니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> Model B has a higher point estimate, but …  
> The confidence intervals …  
> This means that …  
> Therefore, the observed difference may …  
> I would want to see …  
> If the same trend remains consistent across …  
> then I would be more confident that …

초급식 표현:

> Model B has higher accuracy, so it is better.

더 정확한 표현:

> Model B has higher average accuracy, but the uncertainty around the estimate is still substantial.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although Model B achieves a higher point estimate, the substantial overlap between the confidence intervals suggests that the observed difference may not be sufficiently stable to support a strong claim of superiority.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> Model B has a higher average accuracy, but I would not immediately conclude that it is clearly better. The point estimate favors Model B, yet the substantial overlap between the confidence intervals indicates considerable uncertainty around the performance difference. This means that the observed gap may partly reflect sampling variation or run-to-run variability. I would therefore want to see results from additional runs, larger evaluation sets, or repeated experiments before making a stronger claim. If Model B continues to outperform Model A consistently and the confidence interval around the difference becomes narrower, I would have greater confidence that the improvement is real. Based on the current evidence, I would say that Model B appears better on average, but the result should still be interpreted cautiously.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“The point estimate favors Model B, but uncertainty remains.” → “The confidence intervals overlap substantially.” → “We need more evidence before drawing a strong conclusion.”**
