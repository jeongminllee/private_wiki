# Day 29 — Explaining Limitations Without Undermining Your Own Work

오늘 목표는 **연구나 프로젝트의 한계를 인정하면서도, 결과의 가치를 스스로 약화시키지 않는 방식으로 설명하는 것**입니다. C1 수준에서는 limitation을 말할 때 **limitation → impact → boundary → value → next step** 구조로 정리하는 것이 중요합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> Your study shows promising results, but it was conducted on a relatively small dataset from a single domain. How would you explain this limitation without making the entire study sound unreliable?

답변에는 다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. 어떤 limitation이 있는지  
2. 그 limitation이 결과에 어떤 영향을 줄 수 있는지  
3. 그럼에도 현재 결과가 왜 의미 있는지  
4. 다음 연구에서 무엇을 확장해야 하는지  

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> One important limitation is that the study was conducted on a relatively small dataset from a single domain.

---

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: limitation 인정

> One limitation of the current study is **[limitation]**, which may affect **[aspect]**.

예:

> One limitation of the current study is the relatively small sample size, which may affect the stability of the estimated performance.

### Template 2: 영향 범위 제한

> This limitation does not invalidate **[finding]**, but it does restrict how broadly we can interpret **[result]**.

예:

> This limitation does not invalidate the observed trend, but it does restrict how broadly we can interpret the result.

### Template 3: 가치 + future work

> Despite this limitation, the results provide **[value]**, and future work should **[next step]**.

예:

> Despite this limitation, the results provide useful preliminary evidence, and future work should evaluate the method on larger and more diverse datasets.

---

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. limitation
**한계, 제약**

> One limitation of the study is the narrow range of datasets used.

논문에서 가장 기본적이지만 매우 중요한 표현입니다.

> The method has several practical limitations.

---

### 2. constrain
**범위를 제한하다**

> The small sample size constrains the generalizability of the findings.

`limit`보다 조금 더 학술적으로 들립니다.

> Computational cost may constrain the applicability of the method.

---

### 3. undermine
**신뢰도나 가치를 약화시키다**

> This limitation does not necessarily undermine the main conclusion.

일반 영어에서도 자주 사용합니다.

> Repeated delays can undermine confidence in the project.

주의할 점은 꽤 강한 표현이라는 것입니다.

---

### 4. preliminary evidence
**예비적 근거**

> The study provides preliminary evidence that the method may improve performance.

매우 유용한 표현입니다. 결과가 의미는 있지만 아직 확정적이지 않을 때 씁니다.

---

### 5. scope
**적용 범위, 연구 범위**

> The conclusion should be interpreted within the scope of the current experiment.

일상 업무에서도 가능합니다.

> That issue is outside the scope of this project.

논문에서는 자주 이렇게 씁니다.

> within the scope of this study

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> Our study has a limitation that the data is too small.

더 자연스럽게:

> One limitation of our study is the small dataset size.

또는:

> One limitation is the relatively small sample size.

설명: `has a limitation that...`도 의미는 전달되지만 어색합니다.

- One limitation is … ✅
- One limitation of the study is … ✅

---

### 2

> Because of this limitation, our result cannot trust.

정답:

> Because of this limitation, the result should be interpreted with caution.

설명: `result cannot trust`는 문법적으로 틀렸습니다.

- cannot be trusted ✅
- should be interpreted with caution ✅

하지만 후자가 연구 영어에서 훨씬 자연스럽고 덜 과격합니다.

---

### 3

> This limitation doesn't mean our study is meaningless.

더 자연스럽게:

> This limitation does not invalidate the main finding.

설명: `meaningless`는 너무 강하고 감정적인 표현입니다. 연구에서는 `invalidate`, `undermine`, `restrict`를 더 자주 씁니다.

---

### 4

> We need bigger and more various datasets.

정답:

> We need larger and more diverse datasets.

설명:

- bigger dataset △
- larger dataset ✅
- more various datasets ❌
- more diverse datasets ✅

논문 영어에서는 `larger`와 `diverse`를 선호합니다.

---

### 5

> Future study should test this method with another domains.

정답:

> Future studies should test this method in other domains.

또는 더 학술적으로:

> Future work should evaluate the method across additional domains.

설명:

- another + 단수명사
- other + 복수명사

그리고 논문에서는 `future work`가 매우 자주 쓰입니다.

---

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> One important limitation is …  
> This may affect …  
> However, it does not necessarily undermine …  
> The current conclusion should therefore be interpreted within …  
> Despite this limitation, the study provides …  
> Future work should …  
> If the same pattern is observed across …, we would have stronger evidence that …

오늘은 특히 **한계를 인정하면서도 결과를 과도하게 깎아내리지 않는 것**에 집중하세요.

초급식 표현:

> Our dataset is small, so the result may be bad.

더 정확한 표현:

> The small dataset limits how confidently we can generalize the result.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> The limited sample size constrains the generalizability of the findings, but it does not invalidate the consistent trend observed within the current experimental setting.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> One important limitation of the current study is that it was conducted on a relatively small dataset from a single domain. This may reduce the generalizability of the findings and increase uncertainty around the estimated performance. However, the limitation does not necessarily undermine the main result, especially if the observed trend is consistent across multiple runs. I would therefore interpret the conclusion within the scope of the current experimental setting rather than generalizing it too broadly. Despite this limitation, the study provides useful preliminary evidence that the method may be effective. Future work should evaluate the approach on larger, more diverse datasets and across additional domains to determine whether the same pattern holds under broader conditions.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“One limitation of the current study is …” → “This does not necessarily invalidate the main finding.” → “The conclusion should be interpreted within the scope of the current experiment.”**
