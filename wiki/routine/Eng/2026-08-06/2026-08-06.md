# Day 9 — Evaluating Whether a Benchmark Comparison Is Fair

오늘 목표는 **실험 조건을 비교하고, 결과 차이가 모델 자체 때문인지 외부 조건 때문인지 설명하는 것**입니다. 벤치마크 리뷰, 연구 미팅, 기술 보고에서 바로 활용할 수 있습니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> Two models were tested on the same benchmark, but they used different hardware, batch sizes, and precision settings. Can we fairly compare the results? What should be controlled?

답변에 다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. 현재 비교가 공정한지
2. 결과에 영향을 줄 수 있는 외부 조건
3. 반드시 동일하게 맞춰야 할 조건
4. 완전히 동일하게 만들 수 없을 때의 대응 방법

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 비교의 문제점 제시

> It is difficult to make a fair comparison because **[conditions differ]**.

예:

> It is difficult to make a fair comparison because the two models were evaluated under different hardware conditions.

### Template 2: 통제해야 할 변수 설명

> To isolate the effect of **[target variable]**, we need to control for **[other variables]**.

예:

> To isolate the effect of model architecture, we need to control for hardware, batch size, and numerical precision.

### Template 3: 제한이 있을 때 결론 조절

> If the conditions cannot be fully standardized, the results should be reported with **[qualification]**.

예:

> If the conditions cannot be fully standardized, the results should be reported with clear caveats.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. control for

**다른 변수의 영향을 통제하다**

> We controlled for dataset size and training duration.

논문 예문:

> The analysis controlled for age, income, and education level.

단순히 `control`이라고만 하지 않고, 변수를 말할 때는 보통 `control for`를 사용합니다.

---

### 2. confounding variable

**교란 변수**

> Hardware performance may act as a confounding variable in this comparison.

교란 변수는 실제로 알고 싶은 변수 외에 결과에 영향을 주는 요인입니다.

예를 들어 모델 A가 더 빨랐더라도 더 좋은 GPU를 사용했다면, 속도 차이가 모델 구조 때문인지 GPU 때문인지 알기 어렵습니다.

---

### 3. isolate the effect of

**~의 효과만 분리해서 확인하다**

> The experiment was designed to isolate the effect of quantization.

연구 영어에서 매우 유용한 표현입니다.

> We changed only one factor to isolate its effect on latency.

---

### 4. standardized conditions

**표준화된 조건, 동일하게 맞춘 조건**

> All models should be evaluated under standardized conditions.

논문식 표현:

> The experiments were conducted under standardized settings.

---

### 5. caveat

**주의사항, 결과 해석상의 제한**

> One important caveat is that the models were tested on different GPUs.

자주 쓰는 구조:

* with one important caveat
* a major caveat
* interpret the result with this caveat in mind

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> We should make all conditions same.

정답:

> We should keep all conditions the same.

또는:

> We should standardize the experimental conditions.

설명:

`make all conditions same`은 관사가 빠져 어색합니다.

* keep A the same
* standardize A

두 표현을 기억하세요.

---

### 2

> The hardware can affect to the result.

정답:

> The hardware can affect the result.

설명:

`affect`는 타동사이므로 뒤에 `to`를 쓰지 않습니다.

* affect the result ✅
* have an effect on the result ✅
* affect to the result ❌

---

### 3

> We need to control other variables for compare the models fairly.

정답:

> We need to control for other variables to compare the models fairly.

설명:

두 부분을 구분하세요.

* control for other variables
* to compare the models

목적을 나타낼 때는 `to + 동사원형`을 사용합니다.

---

### 4

> The result is not reliable because the environments are different each other.

정답:

> The result may not be reliable because the environments differ from each other.

더 간결하게:

> The result may not be reliable because the environments are different.

설명:

* are different each other ❌
* are different from each other ✅
* differ from each other ✅

또한 절대적으로 신뢰할 수 없다고 단정하기보다는 `may not be reliable`이 더 신중합니다.

---

### 5

> We only changed the batch size for knowing its effect.

정답:

> We changed only the batch size to determine its effect.

또는:

> We changed only the batch size to isolate its effect.

설명:

목적을 표현할 때 `for knowing`보다 `to determine`, `to isolate`가 자연스럽습니다.

또한 `only`의 위치도 중요합니다.

> We only changed the batch size.

문맥에 따라 “우리는 바꾸기만 했다”처럼 해석될 수 있습니다.

> We changed only the batch size.

“배치 크기만 바꿨다”는 의미가 더 명확합니다.

---

## 5. 60–90초 Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름을 따라 말해보세요.

> At first glance, the comparison appears to be …
> However, the two experiments differ in terms of …
> These differences may act as confounding variables.
> To isolate the effect of …, we should control for …
> Ideally, both models should be tested under standardized conditions.
> If that is not possible, the results should include …
> Therefore, I would interpret the conclusion as …

오늘은 결론을 이분법적으로 말하지 마세요.

기본 표현:

> The comparison is unfair.

더 정교한 표현:

> The comparison is not fully controlled, so the result should be interpreted with caution.

고급 표현:

> The result may still be informative, but it does not isolate the effect of model architecture.

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> I do not think the results can be compared directly because the two models were evaluated under different hardware, batch-size, and precision settings. These factors may act as confounding variables, especially when we compare latency or throughput. To isolate the effect of model architecture, we should control for the GPU type, input length, batch size, numerical precision, software versions, and measurement procedure. Ideally, both models should be tested under standardized conditions using the same dataset and evaluation script. If complete standardization is not possible, the differences should be reported clearly, and the conclusion should include appropriate caveats. The results may still be useful, but they should not be treated as definitive evidence that one model is inherently better.

## 오늘의 핵심 교정

> The hardware affects to the result. ❌
> The hardware affects the result. ✅

> We should make every condition same. ❌
> We should keep every condition the same. ✅

> We changed the batch size for knowing the effect. ❌
> We changed only the batch size to isolate its effect. ✅

> The result is unfair. △
> The comparison is not fully controlled, so the result should be interpreted with caution. ✅
