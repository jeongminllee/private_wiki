# Day 8 — Explaining Experimental Results Without Overclaiming

오늘 목표는 **실험 결과를 수치로 설명하고, 상관관계와 인과관계를 구분하며, 결과의 의미를 신중하게 해석하는 것**입니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 답해보세요.

> You tested a new training method, and the model’s accuracy increased from 82% to 85%. How would you explain the result, its significance, and its limitations?

답변에 다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. 무엇을 비교했는가
2. 결과가 얼마나 개선되었는가
3. 개선의 가능한 원인은 무엇인가
4. 아직 확신할 수 없는 부분은 무엇인가

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 말하기 템플릿 — 3분

### Template 1: 수치 변화 설명

> The model’s accuracy increased from **[A]** to **[B]**, representing an improvement of **[difference] percentage points**.

예:

> The model’s accuracy increased from 82% to 85%, representing an improvement of 3 percentage points.

중요한 차이:

* 82% → 85%: **3 percentage points**
* 상대적 증가율: 약 **3.7 percent**

기술 발표에서는 둘을 구분하는 것이 좋습니다.

---

### Template 2: 결과 해석

> This improvement may be attributable to **[possible cause]**, although further experiments are needed to confirm the explanation.

예:

> This improvement may be attributable to more stable optimization, although further experiments are needed to confirm the explanation.

---

### Template 3: 과도한 결론 피하기

> While the result is encouraging, it does not establish that **[strong conclusion]**.

예:

> While the result is encouraging, it does not establish that the new method will outperform the baseline in every setting.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. percentage point

**퍼센트포인트**

> Accuracy improved by 4 percentage points, from 76% to 80%.

`percent`와 혼동하지 마세요.

> Accuracy increased by 4%.
> 상대적인 증가율로 해석될 수 있습니다.

> Accuracy increased by 4 percentage points.
> 76%에서 80%처럼 절대 차이를 뜻합니다.

---

### 2. be attributable to

**~에 기인할 수 있다**

> The improvement may be attributable to better data preprocessing.

논문에서는 원인을 단정하지 않기 위해 `may be attributable to`를 자주 사용합니다.

---

### 3. correlate with

**~와 상관관계가 있다**

> Model size tends to correlate with training cost.

주의:

> correlate to ❌
> correlate with ✅

그리고 상관관계가 있다고 해서 반드시 원인이라는 뜻은 아닙니다.

---

### 4. establish causality

**인과관계를 입증하다**

> The experiment shows an association, but it does not establish causality.

논문에서 매우 중요한 구분입니다.

* correlation: 함께 변하는 관계
* causality: 하나가 다른 하나를 직접 변화시키는 관계

---

### 5. statistically significant

**통계적으로 유의한**

> The difference was statistically significant at the 5% level.

주의할 점:

통계적으로 유의하다고 해서 반드시 실제 효과가 크거나 실무적으로 중요하다는 뜻은 아닙니다.

> The result was statistically significant but practically small.

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The accuracy was increased from 82% to 85%.

정답:

> The accuracy increased from 82% to 85%.

설명:

`increase`는 자동사로 쓸 수 있으므로 수동태가 필요하지 않습니다.

* Accuracy increased. ✅
* We increased the accuracy. ✅
* Accuracy was increased by us. 문법적으로 가능하지만 보통 불필요함

---

### 2

> The accuracy improved 3%.

더 정확한 표현:

> The accuracy improved by 3 percentage points.

또는:

> The accuracy increased from 82% to 85%.

설명:

변화량 앞에는 `by`를 사용합니다.

* increased by 3 percentage points
* increased to 85%
* increased from 82% to 85%

---

### 3

> This result proves that the new method is better.

더 적절한 표현:

> This result suggests that the new method may be more effective under the tested conditions.

설명:

단일 실험 결과는 일반적으로 `prove`보다 `suggest`, `indicate`, `provide evidence that`으로 표현합니다.

---

### 4

> The model size has a correlation with the accuracy.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> Model size is correlated with accuracy.

또는:

> There is a correlation between model size and accuracy.

설명:

다음 두 구조를 기억하세요.

* A is correlated with B.
* There is a correlation between A and B.

---

### 5

> The result is meaningful statistically.

정답:

> The result is statistically significant.

설명:

한국어의 “통계적으로 의미 있다”를 직역하면 어색합니다. 학술 영어에서는 `statistically significant`가 표준 표현입니다.

## 5. 60–90초 Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 순서로 소리 내어 말하세요.

> We compared …
> The accuracy increased from … to …
> This represents an improvement of … percentage points.
> One possible explanation is that …
> However, the result does not establish that …
> We would need additional experiments to …
> Therefore, the result is promising, but …

오늘은 다음 표현을 의식적으로 사용하세요.

* under the tested conditions
* may be attributable to
* does not establish causality
* requires further validation
* practically meaningful

---

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> We compared a new training method with the original baseline under the same experimental conditions. The model’s accuracy increased from 82% to 85%, representing an improvement of 3 percentage points. This improvement may be attributable to more stable optimization or better use of the training data. However, the result does not establish that the new method is consistently superior. The experiment was conducted on only one dataset, and we have not yet measured the variance across multiple random seeds. We would need additional experiments to determine whether the improvement is statistically significant and whether it generalizes to other tasks. Overall, the result is encouraging, but it requires further validation before we draw a strong conclusion.

## 오늘의 핵심 교정

> Accuracy was increased. △
> Accuracy increased. ✅

> Accuracy improved 3%. ❌
> Accuracy improved by 3 percentage points. ✅

> This proves the method is better. ❌
> This suggests that the method may be more effective under the tested conditions. ✅

> A has correlation with B. △
> A is correlated with B. ✅
