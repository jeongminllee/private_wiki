# Day 20 — Explaining What an Ablation Study Actually Shows

오늘 목표는 **모델의 여러 구성요소 중 어떤 부분이 실제 성능 향상에 기여하는지 설명하는 것**입니다. 단순히 “이 모듈이 좋았다”라고 말하는 것이 아니라, **component → removal/change → observed effect → cautious interpretation** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 답해보세요.

> A model contains three new components: A, B, and C. When component B is removed, accuracy drops significantly, while removing A or C has little effect. What can we reasonably conclude?

답변에는 다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. 어떤 실험을 했는가
2. 어떤 구성요소가 가장 중요한 것으로 보이는가
3. 무엇을 아직 확신할 수 없는가
4. 어떤 추가 실험이 필요한가

시작은 이렇게 하면 좋습니다.

> The ablation results suggest that component B makes the largest contribution to performance.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **구성요소 제거와 결과를 연결**할 때 사용합니다.

> When **[component]** was removed, **[metric]** decreased by **[amount]**, suggesting that **[interpretation]**.

예:

> When component B was removed, accuracy decreased by four percentage points, suggesting that it plays an important role in the model’s performance.

두 번째는 **과도한 결론을 피할 때** 유용합니다.

> However, this does not necessarily mean that **[strong conclusion]**, because **[possible interaction/limitation]**.

예:

> However, this does not necessarily mean that component B works independently, because its effect may depend on the other components.

세 번째는 **추가 검증을 제안**할 때 씁니다.

> To better understand the contribution of **[component]**, I would test **[additional condition]**.

예:

> To better understand the contribution of component B, I would test it both alone and in combination with the other modules.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. contribution

**기여도, 기여**

> Component B appears to make the largest contribution to overall performance.

논문에서 매우 자주 씁니다.

> We measured the contribution of each feature through an ablation study.

`effect`보다 “전체 결과에 얼마나 기여했는가”에 초점이 있습니다.

### 2. ablate / ablation

**특정 구성요소를 제거해서 효과를 확인하다**

> We ablated the attention module to measure its contribution.

명사형:

> The ablation results indicate that the module is important.

일상 영어에서는 거의 쓰지 않지만, ML 논문에서는 매우 자주 등장합니다.

### 3. interaction effect

**상호작용 효과**

> There may be an interaction effect between components B and C.

즉, B 하나만의 효과가 아니라 **B와 C가 함께 있을 때 생기는 효과**일 수 있다는 뜻입니다.

> The benefit of component B may depend on whether component C is present.

### 4. isolate

**효과를 분리해서 확인하다**

> Removing one component at a time helps isolate its contribution.

이미 여러 번 등장한 표현이지만, ablation 문맥에서는 특히 중요합니다.

> The experiment was designed to isolate the effect of the new loss function.

### 5. indispensable

**없어서는 안 되는, 필수적인**

> The results suggest that component B may be indispensable to the current architecture.

주의할 점은 `important`보다 훨씬 강한 표현이라는 것입니다.

따라서 근거가 충분하지 않다면:

> Component B appears to be important.

가 더 안전합니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1

> We removed B for checking its effect.

정답:

> We removed B to check its effect.

더 자연스럽게:

> We removed B to isolate its contribution.

설명: 목적을 표현할 때는 `for + 동명사`보다 `to + 동사원형`이 자연스럽습니다.

* for checking ❌
* to check ✅
* to isolate ✅

---

### 2

> The accuracy was decreased when B was removed.

정답:

> The accuracy decreased when B was removed.

설명: `decrease`는 자동사로 사용할 수 있습니다.

* accuracy decreased ✅
* we decreased the accuracy ✅
* accuracy was decreased △

기술 결과를 설명할 때는 능동형이 더 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 3

> B has the most big effect.

정답:

> B has the largest effect.

또는:

> B makes the largest contribution.

설명:

* the most big ❌
* the biggest ✅
* the largest ✅

학술 영어에서는 `largest effect` 또는 `largest contribution`이 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 4

> This proves B is the most important component.

더 적절한 표현:

> This suggests that B is an important component.

또는:

> The results provide evidence that B makes a substantial contribution.

설명: ablation 하나만으로 `prove`라고 말하는 것은 너무 강합니다.

특히 여러 component 사이에 interaction이 있을 수 있기 때문입니다.

---

### 5

> Maybe B and C affect each other together.

더 자연스럽게:

> There may be an interaction between B and C.

또는:

> The effect of B may depend on the presence of C.

설명: `affect each other together`는 의미가 중복되고 어색합니다. 연구 영어에서는 `interaction`이 훨씬 정확합니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 말해보세요.

> We conducted an ablation study to …
> When component B was removed, …
> In contrast, removing A or C …
> This suggests that …
> However, I would be cautious about concluding that …
> There may be an interaction between …
> To isolate the contribution more clearly, I would …

오늘은 특히 **“중요해 보인다”와 “필수다”를 구분해서 말하는 것**을 연습하세요.

너무 강한 표현:

> Component B is necessary.

더 신중한 표현:

> Component B appears to make a substantial contribution.

한 단계 더:

> The ablation results suggest that component B contributes substantially to performance, although its effect may depend on interactions with the other components.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> We conducted an ablation study to understand the contribution of each component. When component B was removed, accuracy dropped substantially, while removing A or C had only a minor effect. This suggests that component B makes the largest contribution to the current model’s performance. However, I would be cautious about concluding that B is independently responsible for the improvement. Its effect may depend on interactions with the other components. To investigate this further, I would evaluate B both alone and in different combinations with A and C. I would also repeat the experiments across multiple random seeds and datasets. If the same pattern remains consistent, we would have stronger evidence that component B is a key part of the architecture.

오늘은 이 세 문장을 입에 붙이세요: **“The results suggest that B makes the largest contribution.” → “There may be an interaction effect.” → “We need additional experiments to isolate its contribution.”**
