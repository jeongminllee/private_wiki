# Day 37 — Explaining Reproducibility: Can Other Researchers Get the Same Result?

오늘 목표는 **한 번 좋은 결과가 나왔다는 것과, 다른 연구자가 같은 조건에서 비슷한 결과를 재현할 수 있다는 것을 구분해서 설명하는 것**입니다. 오늘은 **reported result → reproducibility → missing details → variance → stronger evidence** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A paper reports impressive model performance, but it does not provide the random seeds, exact training configuration, or evaluation code. How confident would you be in the result?

답변에는 **왜 reproducibility가 중요한지, 어떤 정보가 빠져 있는지, 결과 변동성이 왜 문제인지, 무엇이 추가되면 신뢰도가 높아지는지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The reported result is promising, but my confidence would be limited if the experimental setup could not be reproduced independently.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **재현 가능성의 중요성을 설명할 때** 사용합니다.

> A result is more convincing when **[independent researchers / repeated runs]** can reproduce **[the same trend / similar performance]**.

예:

> A result is more convincing when independent researchers can reproduce similar performance under the same conditions.

두 번째는 **필수 정보가 부족하다고 말할 때** 유용합니다.

> Without **[implementation detail]**, it is difficult to determine whether **[result]** is reproducible.

예:

> Without the exact training configuration, it is difficult to determine whether the reported improvement is reproducible.

세 번째는 **더 강한 검증을 제안할 때** 씁니다.

> I would have greater confidence if the authors reported **[details]** and repeated the experiment across **[conditions]**.

예:

> I would have greater confidence if the authors reported multiple random seeds and released the evaluation code.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**reproducible**은 다른 사람이 같은 절차를 따라 비슷한 결과를 얻을 수 있다는 뜻입니다.

> The results should be reproducible across independent implementations.

논문에서는 `reproducibility`라는 명사형도 매우 자주 사용합니다.

> Reproducibility is essential for reliable scientific evaluation.

**random seed**는 난수 생성 과정을 고정하기 위한 값입니다.

> The authors reported results across five random seeds.

딥러닝에서는 seed가 달라지면 초기화나 데이터 순서가 바뀌면서 결과도 달라질 수 있습니다.

**variance**는 반복 실험에서 결과가 얼마나 달라지는지를 의미합니다.

> The model shows high variance across training runs.

자주 쓰는 표현:

> run-to-run variance  
> performance variance  
> reduce variance

**implementation detail**은 실험을 실제로 재현하는 데 필요한 구체적인 구현 정보입니다.

> Important implementation details are missing from the paper.

예:

> Learning rate, batch size, optimizer settings, and preprocessing steps are important implementation details.

**replicate**는 실험을 다시 수행하여 비슷한 결과가 나오는지 확인한다는 뜻입니다.

> Another research group was able to replicate the main finding.

일상 영어에서 단순히 `repeat`를 쓸 수도 있지만, 연구 문맥에서는 `replicate`가 더 정확합니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> Other researchers should can reproduce the result.

정답:

> Other researchers should be able to reproduce the result.

설명: `should`와 `can`을 연속으로 사용할 수 없습니다.

- should can ❌
- should be able to ✅

### 2

> The paper didn't write the exact settings.

더 자연스럽게:

> The paper did not provide the exact experimental settings.

설명: 논문이 정보를 “쓴다”기보다 `provide`, `report`, `describe`를 사용하는 것이 자연스럽습니다.

- write the settings △
- provide the settings ✅
- report the settings ✅

### 3

> The result changes a lot depends on seed.

정답:

> The result varies substantially depending on the random seed.

설명:

- changes a lot depends on ❌
- varies depending on ✅

`vary depending on`은 연구 영어에서 매우 유용한 구조입니다.

### 4

> They should experiment several times.

더 자연스럽게:

> They should run the experiment multiple times.

설명: `experiment`는 보통 명사로 사용합니다.

- experiment several times △
- run the experiment multiple times ✅

### 5

> If only one experiment is good, we cannot believe it.

더 정확하게:

> A strong result from a single run may not be sufficient to establish reliability.

설명: `cannot believe it`은 너무 일상적이고 강합니다. 연구에서는:

> may not be sufficient  
> provides limited evidence  
> should be interpreted cautiously

같은 표현이 더 적절합니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 말해보세요.

> The paper reports strong performance, but …  
> One concern is that …  
> Without information about …  
> it is difficult to determine whether …  
> The result may also vary depending on …  
> I would therefore want to see …  
> If similar performance is observed across …  
> I would have greater confidence that …

초급식 표현:

> They got a good result, but maybe it was lucky.

더 정확한 표현:

> The strong result may partly reflect random variation across runs.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Without sufficient implementation details and repeated evaluations across random seeds, it is difficult to determine whether the reported improvement reflects a robust effect or run-to-run variance.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The reported performance is impressive, but I would be cautious if the paper does not provide enough information to reproduce the experiment. Missing details such as random seeds, training hyperparameters, preprocessing steps, and evaluation code make it difficult to determine whether the improvement is robust. This is especially important in machine learning because performance can vary across training runs. I would therefore prefer to see results averaged over multiple random seeds together with measures of variance. Releasing the training configuration and evaluation code would also allow independent researchers to replicate the findings. If similar results are consistently observed across repeated runs and independent implementations, I would have much greater confidence in the conclusion.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“The result should be reproducible across repeated runs.” → “Performance may vary depending on the random seed.” → “A single strong run may not be sufficient to establish reliability.”**
