# Day 10 — Synthesizing Conflicting Evidence

오늘 목표는 **서로 다른 실험이나 논문이 상반된 결과를 보여줄 때, 한쪽을 단정적으로 선택하지 않고 차이의 원인을 분석한 뒤 잠정적인 결론을 제시하는 것**입니다. 이제 단순한 “내 의견 말하기”에서 한 단계 올라가, **evidence → discrepancy → interpretation → next step** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 답해보세요.

> Two studies evaluate the same machine-learning method. One reports a clear improvement, while the other finds almost no benefit. How would you interpret these conflicting results?

답변에는 다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. 두 연구가 무엇에 동의하거나 충돌하는지
2. 결과 차이가 발생한 가능한 이유
3. 현재 어떤 결론까지 말할 수 있는지
4. 무엇을 추가로 검증해야 하는지

오늘은 바로 결론부터 내리지 않고 이렇게 시작해보세요.

> The evidence is mixed, so I would first look at how the two studies differ.

---

## 2. 재사용 가능한 Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: 상반된 결과 정리

> While **[Study A]** found that **[result A]**, **[Study B]** reported **[different result]**.

예:

> While the first study found that the method significantly improved accuracy, the second study reported only a marginal improvement.

`A says X, but B says Y`보다 훨씬 학술적이고 자연스럽습니다.

### Template 2: 차이의 원인 추론

> The discrepancy may stem from differences in **[dataset / methodology / sample size / evaluation setting]**.

예:

> The discrepancy may stem from differences in dataset size and evaluation methodology.

### Template 3: 잠정적 결론 제시

> Taken together, the evidence suggests that **[balanced conclusion]**, but further validation is needed before **[stronger claim]**.

예:

> Taken together, the evidence suggests that the method can be effective under certain conditions, but further validation is needed before claiming that it consistently outperforms the baseline.

---

## 3. 오늘의 핵심 표현 5개 — 4분

### 1. discrepancy

**불일치, 결과 차이**

> There is a noticeable discrepancy between the results of the two experiments.

논문에서 결과가 예상과 다르거나 연구 간 차이가 있을 때 자주 사용합니다.

> This discrepancy may be due to differences in data preprocessing.

---

### 2. stem from

**~에서 비롯되다**

> The performance gap may stem from differences in training data.

`come from`보다 학술적이고 분석적인 표현입니다.

비교:

> The problem comes from the dataset.
> The problem may stem from differences in the dataset.

두 번째가 원인을 더 신중하게 추론합니다.

---

### 3. replicate

**연구 결과를 재현하다**

> We were unable to replicate the reported improvement.

논문 문맥에서는 단순히 코드를 다시 실행한다는 의미보다 **동일하거나 유사한 조건에서 같은 연구 결과를 다시 얻는다**는 뜻입니다.

> The finding should be replicated across multiple datasets.

---

### 4. tentative

**잠정적인, 아직 확정적이지 않은**

> Any conclusion at this stage should remain tentative.

말하기에서도 매우 유용합니다.

> My tentative interpretation is that the method is sensitive to the dataset.

즉, “현재 근거로는 이렇게 보이지만 확정은 아니다”라는 뜻입니다.

---

### 5. implication

**함의, 결과가 시사하는 의미**

> One practical implication is that the method may require dataset-specific tuning.

논문에서 단순한 `result`와 구분하세요.

* result = 실제 관찰된 결과
* implication = 그 결과가 의미하는 바

예:

> The model was 20% faster.
> → result

> This suggests that the model may be suitable for latency-sensitive applications.
> → implication

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> Despite of the same method, the results were different.

정답:

> Despite using the same method, the results were different.

또는:

> Although the studies used the same method, the results were different.

설명:

`despite` 뒤에는 `of`를 붙이지 않습니다.

* despite of ❌
* despite + 명사 ✅
* despite + -ing ✅
* although + 문장 ✅

예:

> Despite the small sample size, the result was significant.

---

### 2

> There are not enough evidences to make a conclusion.

정답:

> There is not enough evidence to draw a conclusion.

설명:

`evidence`는 일반적으로 **불가산명사**입니다.

* evidences ❌
* evidence ✅

그리고 학술 영어에서는:

> make a conclusion △
> draw a conclusion ✅

를 더 자주 사용합니다.

---

### 3

> The second research could not reproduce the result.

더 자연스럽게:

> The second study could not replicate the result.

설명:

`research`는 보통 불가산명사이므로 개별 논문이나 실험을 가리킬 때는 `study`를 사용하는 것이 자연스럽습니다.

* a research ❌
* a study ✅
* research shows that … ✅

또한 연구 결과 재현에는 `replicate`가 더 정확합니다.

---

### 4

> The difference may be occurred because of the dataset.

정답:

> The difference may be due to the dataset.

또는:

> The difference may stem from differences in the dataset.

설명:

`occur`는 자동사이므로 `be occurred` 형태로 쓰지 않습니다.

* may be occurred ❌
* may occur ✅

하지만 원인을 설명할 때는 `be due to`나 `stem from`이 더 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 5

> We cannot conclude which method is better yet because two results are opposite.

더 자연스럽게:

> We cannot yet conclude which method is better because the findings are inconsistent.

또는 조금 더 고급스럽게:

> The evidence is currently too inconsistent to support a definitive conclusion.

설명:

연구 결과가 서로 다를 때 `opposite`보다 다음 표현들이 더 자연스럽습니다.

* conflicting
* inconsistent
* mixed

특히:

> mixed evidence
> conflicting findings

를 기억해두세요.

---

## 5. 60–90초 Speaking Practice — 2분

아래 흐름을 따라 실제 연구 미팅이라고 생각하고 말해보세요.

> The two studies report conflicting findings.
> While the first study found that …, the second study …
> One possible explanation for this discrepancy is …
> It may also stem from …
> At this point, I would be cautious about concluding that …
> Taken together, the evidence suggests that …
> To obtain a more definitive answer, I would …

오늘의 핵심은 **확신의 강도 조절**입니다.

너무 강한 표현:

> The method does not work.

조금 더 정확한 표현:

> The method does not appear to work consistently across different settings.

더 연구자다운 표현:

> The available evidence does not yet support the claim that the method consistently improves performance.

이렇게 말하면 단순히 영어가 어려워지는 것이 아니라 **내 주장과 증거의 강도를 정확하게 맞출 수 있게 됩니다.**

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The two studies report conflicting findings, so I would be cautious about concluding that the method is either clearly effective or ineffective. While the first study found a substantial improvement, the second study was unable to replicate that result. One possible explanation for this discrepancy is that the studies used different datasets, evaluation metrics, or training configurations. The effect may also depend on characteristics of the task itself. Taken together, the evidence suggests that the method may be beneficial under certain conditions, but the conclusion should remain tentative. I would want to replicate the experiments using standardized settings and multiple datasets before making a stronger claim. One practical implication is that the method may require more careful evaluation before being adopted in production.

### 오늘의 핵심 교정

> Despite of the difference … ❌
> Despite the difference … ✅

> We need more evidences. ❌
> We need more evidence. ✅

> The result could not be reproduced by another research. △
> Another study could not replicate the result. ✅

> The evidence is opposite. △
> The evidence is conflicting / mixed. ✅

오늘부터는 답변할 때 **“정답을 말한다”보다 “현재 근거로 어디까지 말할 수 있는가”**를 의식해보세요. 이것이 B2 수준의 설명에서 C1 수준의 분석적 말하기로 넘어갈 때 매우 중요한 차이입니다.
