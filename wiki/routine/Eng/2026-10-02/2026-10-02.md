# Day 50 — Explaining Distribution Shift and Why Deployment Data May Behave Differently

오늘 목표는 **학습·평가 데이터에서 잘 작동한 모델이 실제 배포 환경에서는 성능이 떨어질 수 있는 이유**를 설명하는 것입니다. 오늘은 **training distribution → deployment environment → distribution shift → performance degradation → monitoring and adaptation** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A malware detection model performs very well on its benchmark, but its accuracy drops after deployment because the real-world data looks different from the training data. Why might this happen, and what should the team do?

답변에는 **distribution shift가 무엇인지, 왜 성능 저하가 생기는지, 어떤 변화를 모니터링해야 하는지, 재학습이나 threshold 조정이 필요한지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The performance drop may be caused by distribution shift between the training data and the data encountered after deployment.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **학습 환경과 실제 환경의 차이를 설명할 때** 사용합니다.

> The model was trained under **[condition A]**, but the deployment environment contains **[condition B]**.

예:

> The model was trained on historical malware samples, but the deployment environment contains newer and more diverse threats.

두 번째는 **성능 저하 원인을 설명할 때** 유용합니다.

> If the input distribution changes, the model may no longer perform as reliably as it did during evaluation.

예:

> If the code patterns change over time, the model may no longer perform as reliably as it did on the benchmark.

세 번째는 **대응 전략을 제안할 때** 씁니다.

> I would monitor **[data/model metric]** and update the model if **[change]** becomes substantial.

예:

> I would monitor class frequencies, error rates, and confidence scores and update the model if the shift becomes substantial.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**distribution shift**는 학습 데이터와 실제 입력 데이터의 분포가 달라지는 현상입니다.

> The model failed because of a substantial distribution shift.

논문에서는 다음 표현도 자주 나옵니다.

> domain shift  
> dataset shift  
> covariate shift

**degrade**는 성능이 점차 나빠진다는 뜻입니다.

> Model performance may degrade over time.

일반 영어에서도 유용합니다.

> Battery performance degrades with age.

**drift**는 시간이 지나면서 데이터나 관계가 서서히 바뀌는 현상을 뜻합니다.

> We observed data drift after deployment.

자주 쓰는 표현:

> data drift  
> concept drift  
> model drift

**representative**는 어떤 데이터가 실제 모집단이나 환경을 잘 대표한다는 뜻입니다.

> The training set may not be representative of real-world traffic.

일반 영어에서도 쓸 수 있습니다.

> This sample is not representative of the entire population.

**adapt**는 변화한 환경에 맞게 모델이나 시스템을 조정한다는 뜻입니다.

> The system needs to adapt to changing attack patterns.

연구에서는 다음 표현으로 확장할 수 있습니다.

> domain adaptation  
> continual adaptation  
> adaptive thresholding

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The real data is different with training data.

정답:

> The real-world data is different from the training data.

설명:

- different with ❌
- different from ✅

연구에서는 `real data`보다 `real-world data`가 더 자연스럽습니다.

### 2

> Model performance became worse after deploy.

정답:

> Model performance became worse after deployment.

더 자연스럽게:

> Model performance degraded after deployment.

설명:

- after deploy ❌
- after deployment ✅

`degrade`는 성능 저하를 설명할 때 매우 유용한 동사입니다.

### 3

> The model cannot adapt new patterns.

정답:

> The model cannot adapt to new patterns.

설명:

`adapt`는 보통 `adapt to + 환경/조건` 구조를 씁니다.

- adapt new patterns ❌
- adapt to new patterns ✅

### 4

> We need monitor the data continuously.

정답:

> We need to monitor the data continuously.

설명:

- need monitor ❌
- need to monitor ✅

조동사처럼 보이지만 `need` 뒤에는 일반적으로 `to + 동사원형`이 옵니다.

### 5

> If data distribution changes, we should retrain again the model.

정답:

> If the data distribution changes, we may need to retrain the model.

설명:

`retrain` 자체에 “다시 학습하다”라는 뜻이 있으므로 `again`은 불필요합니다.

- retrain again ❌
- retrain ✅

또한 항상 재학습이 필요한 것은 아니므로 `should`보다 `may need to`가 더 신중한 표현입니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The model performed well during evaluation, but …  
> One possible reason is …  
> The deployment data may differ in terms of …  
> As a result, …  
> I would monitor …  
> If the shift becomes substantial, …  
> This would help the model …

초급식 표현:

> The model became bad because real data changed.

더 정확한 표현:

> The model’s performance degraded because the deployment data differed from the training distribution.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> The benchmark result may not fully transfer to deployment because the model is operating under a shifted data distribution that was not adequately represented during training.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The performance drop may be caused by distribution shift between the training environment and the real-world deployment environment. The model may have been trained on historical malware samples, while newly deployed systems encounter different file types, attack patterns, and class frequencies. If these inputs are not representative of the original training distribution, the model’s predictions can become less reliable. I would therefore monitor data drift, error rates, class balance, and model confidence after deployment. If the shift becomes substantial, I would consider updating the training data, recalibrating the decision threshold, or retraining the model. The key point is that strong benchmark performance does not guarantee stable performance under changing real-world conditions.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“The deployment data may differ from the training distribution.” → “Model performance can degrade under distribution shift.” → “Continuous monitoring is necessary to maintain reliable performance.”**
