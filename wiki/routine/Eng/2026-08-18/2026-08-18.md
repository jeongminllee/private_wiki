# Day 17 — Distinguishing Association from Causation

오늘 목표는 **두 변수가 함께 움직인다는 사실과, 하나가 다른 하나를 실제로 변화시켰다는 주장을 구분해서 설명하는 것**입니다. 논문을 읽거나 실험 결과를 발표할 때 매우 중요한 능력이고, C1 수준에서는 특히 **observation → alternative explanation → causal claim → required evidence** 구조로 말할 수 있어야 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A study finds that larger language models tend to achieve higher reasoning scores. Can we conclude that increasing model size causes better reasoning performance? Why or why not?

답변에는 다음 내용을 포함하세요.

1. 실제로 관찰된 관계
2. 왜 바로 인과관계라고 할 수 없는지
3. 다른 가능한 설명
4. 인과관계를 주장하려면 어떤 추가 근거가 필요한지

말문이 막히면 이렇게 시작하세요.

> The results show an association between model size and reasoning performance, but that does not necessarily establish causality.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **상관관계와 인과관계를 구분**할 때 쓰는 핵심 구조입니다.

> The fact that **[A]** is associated with **[B]** does not necessarily mean that **[A]** causes **[B]**.

예:

> The fact that larger models are associated with higher accuracy does not necessarily mean that model size alone causes the improvement.

두 번째는 **다른 원인을 제시**할 때 사용합니다.

> An alternative explanation is that **[third factor]** may influence both **[A]** and **[B]**.

예:

> An alternative explanation is that larger models may also be trained on more data, which could influence performance.

세 번째는 **필요한 검증을 제안**할 때 사용합니다.

> To make a stronger causal claim, we would need to **[experimental control]** while holding **[other factors]** constant.

예:

> To make a stronger causal claim, we would need to vary model size while holding the training data and optimization procedure constant.

---

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. association

**연관성, 함께 나타나는 관계**

> The study found a strong association between model scale and benchmark performance.

`association`은 두 현상이 관련 있다는 뜻이지, 원인을 뜻하지는 않습니다.

> An association does not by itself demonstrate causation.

---

### 2. causal

**인과적인**

> The observational result does not provide sufficient evidence for a causal relationship.

자주 쓰는 조합은 다음과 같습니다.

* causal relationship
* causal effect
* causal claim
* causal inference

---

### 3. confound

**인과관계 해석을 교란하다**

> Differences in training data could confound the relationship between model size and performance.

명사형은 앞에서 배운 `confounding variable`입니다.

> Training compute is a potential confounding variable.

---

### 4. hold constant

**다른 조건을 일정하게 유지하다**

> We should hold the dataset and training budget constant while changing model size.

실험 설계에서 매우 자주 사용하는 표현입니다.

> Holding all other factors constant allows us to isolate the effect of the variable of interest.

---

### 5. alternative explanation

**대안적 설명, 다른 가능한 원인**

> One alternative explanation is that the larger models received more training compute.

C1 수준에서는 자신의 설명 하나만 제시하기보다 **다른 가능한 설명도 고려하는 것**이 중요합니다.

> We should consider alternative explanations before drawing a causal conclusion.

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 후 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> Because model size and performance are related, model size makes the performance better.

더 적절한 표현:

> Because model size and performance are correlated, we cannot conclude that model size alone causes better performance.

설명: `A와 B가 관련 있다 → A가 B의 원인이다`는 논리적으로 바로 성립하지 않습니다. 연구 영어에서는 `correlated with`, `associated with`를 먼저 사용하세요.

---

### 2

> There may be another reasons affecting the result.

정답:

> There may be other reasons affecting the result.

또는 더 자연스럽게:

> There may be other factors influencing the result.

설명:

* another + 단수명사: `another reason`
* other + 복수명사: `other reasons`

따라서 `another reasons`는 틀립니다.

---

### 3

> We need to fix all other conditions same.

정답:

> We need to keep all other conditions the same.

더 학술적으로:

> We need to hold all other conditions constant.

설명: `fix`는 여기서 한국어 “고정하다”의 직역이라 어색합니다. 실험에서는 `keep ... the same` 또는 `hold ... constant`가 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 4

> The larger model may trained with more data.

정답:

> The larger model may have been trained on more data.

설명: 과거에 이루어진 학습을 추측하므로:

> may + have been + p.p.

구조를 사용합니다.

또한:

* train a model on data ✅
* be trained on data ✅

입니다.

---

### 5

> We cannot say the model size is the reason of better accuracy.

더 자연스럽게:

> We cannot say that model size is the cause of the higher accuracy.

더 고급스럽게:

> We cannot attribute the higher accuracy to model size alone.

설명: `reason of`보다는 다음 표현이 자연스럽습니다.

* the reason for
* the cause of
* attribute A to B

특히 논문에서는:

> attribute the improvement to X

를 매우 자주 사용합니다.

---

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The study shows that …
> However, an association does not necessarily imply …
> One alternative explanation is that …
> Another potential confounding factor is …
> Therefore, I would avoid attributing the improvement solely to …
> To make a stronger causal claim, we would need to …
> Ideally, we would hold … constant while varying …

오늘은 특히 **확신 수준을 조절하는 표현**에 집중하세요.

너무 강한 표현:

> Larger models reason better because they have more parameters.

더 적절한 표현:

> Larger models tend to achieve higher reasoning scores, but the relationship may be influenced by other factors.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> Although model scale is positively associated with reasoning performance, the available evidence is insufficient to isolate model size as the causal factor.

여기서 핵심은 어려운 단어가 아니라 **관찰한 것과 해석한 것을 분리하는 능력**입니다.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The study shows a positive association between model size and reasoning performance, but that does not necessarily establish a causal relationship. Larger models may also differ in other important ways, such as training data, total compute, optimization strategies, or training duration. These factors could confound the relationship between model size and performance. Therefore, I would avoid attributing the improvement solely to the number of parameters. To make a stronger causal claim, we would need a controlled experiment in which model size is varied while other factors, such as the dataset, training budget, and evaluation procedure, are held constant. If performance consistently improves under those controlled conditions, we would have stronger evidence that model size itself contributes to the observed gain.

오늘은 이 세 문장을 확실히 입에 붙이세요: **“Association does not necessarily imply causation.” → “An alternative explanation is that …” → “We need to hold other factors constant.”**

특히 세 번째 문장은 연구 발표에서 *“그 차이가 정말 이 변수 때문인가요?”*라는 질문을 받았을 때 바로 사용할 수 있습니다.
