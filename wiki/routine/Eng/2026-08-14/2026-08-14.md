# Day 15 — Identifying a Research Gap and Proposing the Next Experiment

오늘 목표는 **현재 연구가 무엇을 보여줬는지, 아직 무엇을 모르는지, 그리고 그 빈틈을 메우기 위해 어떤 실험을 해야 하는지** 논리적으로 설명하는 것입니다. C1 수준에서는 단순히 “more research is needed”라고 끝내기보다, **known → unknown → why it matters → next step** 구조로 말하는 능력이 중요합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A new LLM training method performs well on English benchmarks, but it has not been tested on Korean or other languages. What can we conclude now, and what should be investigated next?

답변에는 다음 내용을 넣으세요.

1. 현재 결과가 보여주는 것
2. 아직 알 수 없는 것
3. 왜 그 부분이 중요한지
4. 다음 실험에서 무엇을 검증할지

말문이 막히면 이렇게 시작하세요.

> The current results are promising, but there is still an important gap in the evidence.

---

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: 현재 근거의 범위 제한

> The current evidence supports **[claim]**, but only within **[scope]**.

예:

> The current evidence supports the effectiveness of the method, but only within English-language benchmarks.

이 구조는 발표에서 과장된 일반화를 피할 때 매우 유용합니다.

### Template 2: 아직 모르는 부분 제시

> What remains unclear is whether **[unanswered question]**.

예:

> What remains unclear is whether the same improvement would hold across different languages.

`We don't know if ...`보다 훨씬 전문적으로 들립니다.

### Template 3: 다음 실험 제안

> A logical next step would be to **[experiment]** in order to determine whether **[question]**.

예:

> A logical next step would be to evaluate the method on multilingual datasets in order to determine whether the gains generalize beyond English.

---

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. research gap

**연구 공백, 아직 충분히 검증되지 않은 부분**

> There is a clear research gap regarding multilingual performance.

논문 리뷰에서 자주 볼 수 있습니다.

> This study attempts to address a gap in the existing literature.

`gap`은 꼭 논문에만 쓰이지 않습니다.

> There is a gap between research performance and real-world performance.

---

### 2. remain unclear

**여전히 불분명하다**

> It remains unclear whether the method is robust to domain shifts.

자연스러운 패턴:

> It remains unclear why ...
> It remains unclear whether ...
> It remains unclear how ...

`still don't know`보다 객관적이고 학술적입니다.

---

### 3. generalize beyond

**~을 넘어 일반화되다**

> We need to determine whether the findings generalize beyond English benchmarks.

또 다른 예:

> It is unclear whether this result generalizes beyond laboratory settings.

`generalize to`도 사용 가능합니다.

> generalize to Korean datasets

반면 `generalize beyond English`는 **현재 범위를 넘어서는가**를 강조합니다.

---

### 4. representative

**대표성이 있는**

> The benchmark may not be representative of real-world user queries.

논문 예문:

> The sample is too small to be representative of the broader population.

일상에서도 쓸 수 있습니다.

> This example is not really representative of my usual work.

---

### 5. shed light on

**~을 더 잘 이해하게 해주다, 실마리를 제공하다**

> Additional experiments could shed light on why the method performs differently across languages.

매우 자연스러운 C1 표현입니다.

다만 `prove`만큼 강하지 않습니다.

> The analysis sheds light on the relationship between data quality and model performance.

---

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> This result can be applied for all languages.

더 적절한 표현:

> We cannot yet assume that this result applies to all languages.

또는:

> It is unclear whether the result generalizes to other languages.

설명: `apply`는 이 문맥에서 보통 `apply to`를 사용합니다.

* apply for a job ✅
* apply to a situation ✅

하지만 연구 결과라면 `generalize to`가 더 정확합니다.

---

### 2

> We need to test with more various languages.

정답:

> We need to test the method on a wider variety of languages.

또는:

> We need to evaluate the method across a more diverse set of languages.

설명: `more various`는 한국인 학습자가 자주 만드는 어색한 표현입니다.

* more various ❌
* a wider variety of ✅
* more diverse ✅

---

### 3

> There are still many things we don't know about.

정답:

> There are still many things we don't know.

더 학술적으로:

> Several important questions remain unanswered.

설명: `know about`은 목적어가 필요합니다.

> We don't know much about multilingual transfer. ✅

하지만:

> many things we don't know about ❌

처럼 `about` 뒤에 아무것도 없으면 어색합니다.

---

### 4

> The English benchmark cannot represent the real users.

더 자연스럽게:

> The English benchmark may not be representative of real-world users.

또는:

> The benchmark may not reflect real-world usage.

설명: 데이터나 벤치마크의 **대표성**을 말할 때 `be representative of`가 매우 유용합니다.

---

### 5

> We should experiment Korean dataset to check it.

정답:

> We should evaluate the method on a Korean dataset.

더 고급스럽게:

> We should evaluate the method on Korean datasets to determine whether the improvement generalizes across languages.

설명: `experiment`는 보통 이렇게 씁니다.

> conduct an experiment
> experiment with a method

평가 대상에는:

> test the model on a dataset
> evaluate the method on a dataset

이 자연스럽습니다.

---

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

아래 흐름을 그대로 소리 내어 말해보세요.

> The current evidence shows that …
> However, this conclusion is limited to …
> What remains unclear is whether …
> This matters because …
> The current benchmark may not be representative of …
> A logical next step would be to …
> This experiment could shed light on …
> Until then, I would avoid claiming that …

오늘은 특히 **“모른다”를 전문적으로 말하는 법**을 연습하세요.

초급식:

> We don't know.

더 좋은 표현:

> It is still unclear whether the effect generalizes.

한 단계 더:

> The available evidence is insufficient to determine whether the effect generalizes beyond the current benchmark.

이 차이가 C1식 말하기에서 상당히 중요합니다.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The current evidence suggests that the training method is effective on English-language benchmarks, but the conclusion is limited to that setting. What remains unclear is whether the same improvement generalizes to Korean or other languages. This is important because English benchmarks may not be representative of multilingual usage, and differences in tokenization, syntax, or training data could affect performance. A logical next step would be to evaluate the method across a diverse set of languages using comparable datasets and evaluation procedures. I would also examine whether the magnitude of the improvement varies by language. These experiments could shed light on whether the method provides a general benefit or mainly works well in English. Until then, I would avoid claiming that the method is universally effective.

오늘은 이 세 문장을 확실히 입에 붙이세요: **“What remains unclear is whether …” → “A logical next step would be to …” → “Until then, I would avoid claiming that …”**
