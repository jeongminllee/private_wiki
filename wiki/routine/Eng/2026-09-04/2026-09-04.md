# Day 30 — Explaining Data Leakage and Benchmark Contamination

오늘 목표는 **모델의 높은 성능이 실제 일반화 능력 때문인지, 아니면 평가 데이터에 대한 간접적인 노출 때문인지 구분해서 설명하는 것**입니다. 오늘은 **unexpectedly high performance → possible leakage → evidence → validation → cautious conclusion** 구조로 말하는 연습을 합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

다음 질문에 60–90초 동안 영어로 답해보세요.

> A language model achieves unusually high scores on a benchmark that may have appeared somewhere in its training data. How would you evaluate whether the result reflects genuine capability?

답변에는 **왜 높은 점수만으로 충분하지 않은지, 어떤 종류의 contamination을 의심할 수 있는지, 무엇을 추가로 확인해야 하는지, 현재 어디까지 결론 내릴 수 있는지**를 포함하세요.

시작은 이렇게 해보세요.

> The strong benchmark result is encouraging, but I would first check whether the evaluation data may have been exposed during training.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

첫 번째는 **성능과 오염 가능성을 분리해서 말할 때** 씁니다.

> The high score may reflect **[genuine capability]**, but it could also be influenced by **[possible leakage]**.

예:

> The high score may reflect genuine reasoning ability, but it could also be influenced by benchmark contamination.

두 번째는 **무엇을 검증해야 하는지 설명**할 때 좋습니다.

> To distinguish between **[explanation A]** and **[explanation B]**, I would **[validation method]**.

예:

> To distinguish between generalization and memorization, I would evaluate the model on newly constructed test sets.

세 번째는 **주장 강도를 낮출 때** 사용합니다.

> Until we can rule out **[problem]**, I would avoid claiming that **[strong conclusion]**.

예:

> Until we can rule out data leakage, I would avoid claiming that the benchmark score represents genuine out-of-sample performance.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

**data leakage**는 학습 단계에서 사용해서는 안 되는 정보가 모델에 들어간 경우를 뜻합니다.

> Data leakage can lead to artificially high evaluation scores.

**benchmark contamination**은 평가용 benchmark의 문항이나 유사한 내용이 모델의 학습 데이터에 포함된 상황입니다.

> Benchmark contamination is a serious concern when evaluating large language models.

**memorization**은 모델이 일반적인 패턴을 학습했다기보다 특정 예시를 기억한 경우입니다.

> The result may reflect memorization rather than generalization.

**rule out**은 어떤 가능성을 충분히 검토해서 배제한다는 뜻입니다.

> We need additional tests to rule out contamination.

일상 영어에서도 사용할 수 있습니다.

> The doctor wanted to rule out other possible causes.

**out-of-sample performance**는 학습 과정에서 보지 않은 새로운 데이터에서의 성능입니다.

> The model should be evaluated on fresh data to estimate its out-of-sample performance.

논문이나 연구 미팅에서 매우 자주 쓰이는 표현입니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐본 뒤 정답을 확인하세요.

### 1

> The model maybe saw the benchmark during training.

정답:

> The model may have seen the benchmark during training.

설명: 과거에 일어난 가능성을 말하므로 `may have + p.p.` 구조를 사용합니다.

- may saw ❌
- may have seen ✅

---

### 2

> The high score can be because of data leakage.

더 자연스럽게:

> The high score may be due to data leakage.

또는:

> Data leakage may account for the unusually high score.

설명: 불확실한 원인을 말할 때 `may be due to`가 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 3

> We need new data which model never saw before.

정답:

> We need new data that the model has never seen before.

더 학술적으로:

> We need a fresh evaluation set that was not exposed during training.

설명: 특정 모델을 가리키므로 `the model`이 필요하고, 지금까지의 경험을 말하므로 `has never seen`이 자연스럽습니다.

---

### 4

> The model may just remember the answers, not understand.

정답:

> The model may simply be memorizing the answers rather than understanding the task.

설명: `not A, but B`도 가능하지만 비교 구조에서는 `rather than`이 자연스럽습니다.

또한:

- remember the answers △
- memorize the answers ✅

가 연구 문맥에 더 정확합니다.

---

### 5

> We cannot trust the benchmark until checking contamination.

더 자연스럽게:

> We should interpret the benchmark result cautiously until we rule out contamination.

설명: `cannot trust`는 너무 강한 표현입니다. 연구 영어에서는 보통 결과를 버리는 대신 **interpret cautiously**라고 말합니다.

그리고:

- until checking △
- until we rule out ✅

가 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

다음 흐름으로 한 번에 말해보세요.

> The model achieved unusually high performance on …  
> However, the score alone does not establish …  
> One concern is that …  
> If the benchmark appeared in the training data, …  
> The result could therefore reflect … rather than …  
> To distinguish between these explanations, I would …  
> Until we can rule out …, I would …

오늘은 특히 **높은 benchmark 점수를 바로 capability로 해석하지 않는 것**에 집중하세요.

초급식 표현:

> The model got a very high score, so it understands the task very well.

더 정확한 표현:

> The model achieved a high score, but we need to determine whether the result reflects generalization or memorization.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> The unusually strong benchmark performance is difficult to interpret without first excluding the possibility that benchmark contamination inflated the reported score.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The model’s unusually high benchmark score is encouraging, but I would not immediately interpret it as evidence of genuine reasoning ability. One important concern is benchmark contamination. If the benchmark questions or closely related examples appeared in the training data, the model may have partially memorized the answers rather than generalized to unseen problems. To evaluate this possibility, I would test the model on newly constructed or time-separated datasets that were unlikely to have been included in training. I would also compare performance on paraphrased and structurally similar but unseen questions. If the model maintains strong performance under those conditions, I would have greater confidence that the result reflects genuine out-of-sample capability. Until contamination can be reasonably ruled out, I would interpret the benchmark score cautiously.

오늘 입에 붙일 세 문장은 이것입니다: **“The result may reflect memorization rather than generalization.” → “We need to rule out data leakage.” → “I would evaluate the model on fresh, out-of-sample data.”**
