# Day 19 — Giving a Clear 90-Second Paper Summary

오늘 목표는 **논문을 읽고 핵심만 짧게 요약해서 말하는 능력**입니다. C1에 가까워질수록 문장을 복잡하게 만드는 것보다, **problem → method → finding → limitation → implication**을 빠르게 정리하는 능력이 중요합니다.

## 1. Speaking Prompt — 2분

아래 상황을 가정하고 60–90초 동안 영어로 말해보세요.

> You have just read a paper proposing a new method that reduces LLM inference cost by 30% while maintaining roughly the same accuracy. Explain the paper to a colleague who has not read it.

다섯 가지만 포함하면 됩니다.

1. 논문이 해결하려는 문제
2. 제안한 방법
3. 핵심 결과
4. 중요한 한계
5. 실무적으로 왜 의미가 있는지

시작이 어렵다면 이렇게 말하세요.

> The paper addresses the problem of reducing inference cost without significantly sacrificing model performance.

## 2. Reusable Speaking Templates — 3분

### Template 1: 문제와 방법 요약

> The paper addresses **[problem]** by proposing **[method]**.

예:

> The paper addresses high inference costs by proposing a more efficient decoding strategy.

`This paper is about...`보다 훨씬 구체적입니다.

### Template 2: 핵심 결과 전달

> The main finding is that **[result]**, while **[important condition]**.

예:

> The main finding is that inference cost was reduced by approximately 30%, while accuracy remained nearly unchanged.

### Template 3: 한계와 의미 연결

> Although **[limitation]**, the results suggest that **[implication]**.

예:

> Although the method was evaluated on only a limited number of models, the results suggest that it could be useful for cost-sensitive deployment.

이 구조 하나로 **장점만 과장하지 않는 요약**을 만들 수 있습니다.

## 3. Useful Words & Expressions — 4분

### 1. address

**문제·과제를 다루다, 해결하려고 하다**

> The study addresses a major limitation of current language models.

`solve`보다 범위가 넓습니다. 논문이 반드시 문제를 완전히 해결한 것은 아니므로 `address`가 더 안전합니다.

일반 영어에서도 가능합니다.

> We need to address this issue before the next meeting.

### 2. retain

**유지하다**

> The compressed model retained most of the original model’s accuracy.

단순한 `keep`보다 논문 문맥에서 자연스럽습니다.

> The method reduces memory usage while retaining performance.

### 3. negligible

**무시할 수 있을 정도로 작은**

> The accuracy difference was negligible.

예:

> The latency increased slightly, but the difference was negligible in practice.

`small`보다 “실질적으로 중요하지 않을 정도로 작다”는 의미가 강합니다.

### 4. implication

**결과가 시사하는 바, 함의**

> One practical implication is that smaller models may be sufficient for many applications.

논문을 요약할 때 결과와 implication을 구분하세요.

> The model was 30% faster.
> → result

> This may reduce serving costs in production.
> → implication

### 5. preliminary

**예비적인, 아직 확정적이지 않은**

> The results are promising but still preliminary.

연구자가 자주 쓰는 표현입니다.

> These preliminary findings should be validated on larger datasets.

강한 결론을 피하면서도 결과의 가치를 인정할 수 있습니다.

## 4. Correction Exercise — 3분

먼저 직접 고쳐보세요.

### 1

> This paper suggests a new method for reduce inference cost.

정답:

> This paper proposes a new method for reducing inference cost.

설명: `for`는 전치사이므로 뒤에 동명사가 옵니다.

* for reduce ❌
* for reducing ✅

그리고 논문이 방법 자체를 제안한다면 `suggest`보다 `propose`가 정확합니다.

### 2

> The accuracy was almost same.

정답:

> The accuracy was almost the same.

또는 기술적으로 더 자연스럽게:

> The accuracy remained nearly unchanged.

설명: `same` 앞에는 일반적으로 `the`가 필요합니다.

* almost same ❌
* almost the same ✅

### 3

> They experimented the method on three models.

정답:

> They tested the method on three models.

또는:

> They evaluated the method on three models.

설명: `experiment`는 보통 다음처럼 사용합니다.

> experiment with a method
> conduct an experiment

방법의 성능을 평가했다면 `test`나 `evaluate`가 자연스럽습니다.

### 4

> The result is very impressive, but we need more researches.

정답:

> The result is promising, but more research is needed.

설명: `research`는 일반적으로 불가산명사입니다.

* researches ❌
* research ✅

그리고 학술적인 결과 설명에서는 `impressive`보다 `promising`이 덜 주관적입니다.

### 5

> This method can be useful to companies which have not many GPUs.

더 자연스럽게:

> This method could be useful for companies with limited GPU resources.

설명: 한국어식으로 관계절을 길게 만드는 대신 **with + 명사구**로 압축할 수 있습니다.

또한 아직 실무 적용이 확정된 것이 아니므로 `can`보다 `could`가 더 신중합니다.

## 5. 60–90 Second Speaking Practice — 2분

아래 순서로 끊지 않고 말해보세요.

> The paper addresses …
> The authors propose …
> The main finding is that …
> Interestingly, …
> However, one limitation is that …
> Therefore, the results should still be considered preliminary.
> One practical implication is that …

오늘 특히 연습할 것은 **정보 압축**입니다.

초급식 표현:

> The paper talks about inference. They made a new method. It is faster. The accuracy is similar.

더 자연스러운 표현:

> The paper proposes a method that reduces inference cost while maintaining comparable accuracy.

C1에 가까운 표현:

> The paper presents an efficiency-oriented approach that substantially reduces inference cost while retaining nearly all of the baseline performance.

핵심은 단어를 어렵게 만드는 것이 아니라 **여러 정보를 하나의 논리적인 문장으로 묶는 것**입니다.

## Compact Model Answer — 1분

> The paper addresses the high computational cost of LLM inference by proposing a more efficient inference method. The main finding is that the approach reduces inference cost by roughly 30% while retaining nearly the same accuracy as the baseline. This is particularly interesting because efficiency improvements often come at the expense of model performance. However, the evaluation was conducted on only a limited set of models and benchmarks, so the findings should still be considered preliminary. Further experiments would be needed to determine whether the method generalizes to different model sizes and workloads. One practical implication is that the approach could reduce serving costs for organizations with limited GPU resources, especially if the performance difference remains negligible in real-world applications.

오늘은 이 세 문장을 확실히 익히세요: **“The paper addresses … by proposing …” → “The main finding is that …” → “One practical implication is that …”**

이 세 문장만 자연스럽게 연결해도, 논문을 읽은 뒤 **1분 안에 핵심을 영어로 설명하는 기본 골격**이 만들어집니다.
