### 💡 답변 (Assistant)
Anthropic's research article "Mapping the Mind of a Large Language Model" details their breakthrough in understanding AI models' inner workings. They applied dictionary learning to the Claude 3.0 Sonnet model, identifying millions of features that correspond to human concepts, ranging from simple entities to abstract ideas. This research allows manipulation of these features to alter model behavior, demonstrating the potential for improving AI safety and reliability. The findings mark a significant step towards making AI models more interpretable and safer.

For more details, read the full article [here](https://www.anthropic.com/research/mapping-mind-language-model).
